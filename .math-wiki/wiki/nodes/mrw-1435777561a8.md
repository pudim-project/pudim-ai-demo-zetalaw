# Definition: Zeta free energy

## Statement

The zeta free energy is
\[
A(\beta)=\log\zeta(\beta),\qquad \beta>1.
\]
Under the zeta law, the formal thermodynamic identities are
\[
A'(\beta)=-\mathbb E_\beta[\log N],
\qquad
A''(\beta)=\operatorname{Var}_\beta(\log N).
\]

## Proof

Differentiating \(A(\beta)=\log\sum_{n\ge 1}n^{-\beta}\) under the absolutely convergent series for \(\beta>1\) gives
\[
A'(\beta)=\frac{\zeta'(\beta)}{\zeta(\beta)}
=-\sum_{n\ge1}\rho_\beta(n)\log n.
\]
A second differentiation gives the variance identity.
