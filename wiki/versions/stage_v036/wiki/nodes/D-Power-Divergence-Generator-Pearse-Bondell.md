# Definition: Pearse-Bondell power-divergence generator

## Statement

For the Pearse--Bondell power-divergence family and \(\lambda=-\gamma\le -1\), the source generator satisfies \(\phi_{-\gamma}(x)=(x^{1-\gamma}+ (\gamma-1)x-\gamma)/(\gamma(\gamma-1))\) for \(\gamma>1\), while \(\phi_{-1}(x)=x-1-\log x\). In both cases \(\phi_{-\gamma}'(x)=(1-x^{-\gamma})/\gamma\), \(\phi_{-\gamma}(1)=0\), and \(\phi_{-\gamma}(0+)=+\infty\).
