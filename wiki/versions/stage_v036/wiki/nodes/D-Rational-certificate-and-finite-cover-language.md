# Definition: Rational certificates and finite covers

## Statement

A rational certificate is a sign or enclosure proof reduced to explicit rational inequalities. A finite cover certificate partitions a compact interval into finitely many rational subintervals and proves the required inequality on each piece, often using an analytic-denominator reduction, a Taylor model, or derivative bounds.
