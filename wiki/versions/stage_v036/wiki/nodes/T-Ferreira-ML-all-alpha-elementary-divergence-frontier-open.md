# Theorem: Ferreira Mittag Leffler all alpha elementary divergence frontier

## Statement

Give an elementary proof, without complex analytic methods and without using the known integral representation, that \(E_\alpha(t^\alpha)>e^t\) for every \(0<\alpha<1\) and \(t>0\), and hence the corresponding Laplace integral diverges for \(\lambda\le -1\).
