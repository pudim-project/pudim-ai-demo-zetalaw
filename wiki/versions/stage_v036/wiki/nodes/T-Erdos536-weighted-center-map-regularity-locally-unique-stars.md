# Theorem: Erdos 536 weighted center map regularity for locally unique moving star centers

## Statement

Weighted center-map regularity theorem: every positive-mass fork-free threshold-sharp moving-star system with locally unique full star centers admits, after discarding \(o(1)\) \(\nu_k\)-mass, a weighted regular decomposition of the center map \((C,r)\mapsto q(C,r)\). Either the regular cells are contained in \(o(\sqrt{S_k})\) rank/center layers and hence have vanishing mass, or two regular cells satisfy the hypotheses of `T-Erdos536-locally-unique-star-union-pushforward-hitting`. Therefore the occupied-top density conclusion of `T-Erdos536-star-fiber-union-occupied-top-density` follows.
