# Theorem: Erdos 536 sparse predecessor layer alternative collapses to zero prime biased mass

## Statement

Sparse predecessor-layer mass-collapse theorem: every positive-mass fork-free coherent moving-star system in the predecessor regime that avoids the partial predecessor-window fork hypothesis is captured, after discarding \(o(1)\) \(\nu_k\)-mass, by \(o(\sqrt{S_k})\) predecessor rank/center layers or by central \(2\)-lacunary predecessor windows. By rank-block anti-concentration and the central-lacunary rank-window theorem, this sparse alternative has vanishing \(\nu_k\)-mass. Consequently `T-Erdos536-fork-free-coherence-links-top-window-to-lower-star-ranks` holds.
