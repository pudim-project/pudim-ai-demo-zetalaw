# Theorem: Erdos536 diagnostic admissible matching defect construction

## Statement

Diagnostic construction problem: construct, or rule out within natural templates, an admissible sequence of occupied-top lower-trace systems whose conditional defect laws have positive mass, separated defect ranks, no moving common core, no endpoint-tower recursion, and disjointness graph asymptotic to a sparse matching so that \(\lambda_C^{\otimes2}\{D\cap E=\varnothing\}\to0\). The construction must be realized by actual lower traces of a candidate \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\), not merely by an abstract set representation.
