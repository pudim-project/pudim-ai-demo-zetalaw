# Negative_Result: T-not-Tao-Sendov-fixed-circle-uniform-product-root-Rouche

## Statement

For every fixed R in (sqrt(2/3),1), the exact tangent-cluster logarithmic derivative F_0 has no uniform positive lower bound on |y|=R across all r,m,N; the second free root can approach that circle.

## Scope

- Obstruction to one fixed-circle Rouche proof, not an obstruction to product-root stability.
