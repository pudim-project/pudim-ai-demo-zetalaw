# Open_Problem: GRWS Sector II Bernstein interpolation source gate

## Statement

Verify the exact source status and later-literature novelty for the Benhida--Curto--Exner Conjecture 1.3(1) assertion that, in Sector II except the boundary shared with Sector I, the geometrically regular weighted-shift weight-squared sequence \(a_n=(p^n+N)/(p^n+D)\), \(p>1\), is not interpolated by a Bernstein function. Determine whether the interior condition \(-1<N<0<D<1\) is source-matching and whether a signed Hausdorff-atom obstruction for the first differences would solve the exact source target.
