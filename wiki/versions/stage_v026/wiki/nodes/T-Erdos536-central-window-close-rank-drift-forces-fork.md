# Theorem: Erdos 536 central window close rank moving center drift forces cross top union fork

## Statement

For \(P_k=\{p_1,\ldots,p_k\}\), \(S_k=\sum_{i\le k}1/p_i\), \(\nu_k(p_i\in S)=1/p_i\), and \(H_{k,\theta}=\{S\subseteq P_k: |S|>\theta S_k\}\), every positive-mass fork-free residual moving-center obstruction in \(H_{k,\theta}\) has no nonsparse close-rank central window. More explicitly, if the occupied-top defect shadows are coherent and intersecting, are not captured by \(o(\sqrt{S_k})\) center-stable blocks, and carry nonnegligible lower-trace mass on central ranks \(r<s\le2r\) with asymptotically incompatible centers, then there exist distinct \(A,B,C\in\mathcal F_k\) with \(A\cup B=C\). Consequently the nonsparse close-rank moving-center branch cannot be a positive-mass union-free counterexample to the Erdos 536 weighted frontier.
