# Theorem: Du Wang h31 endpoint infinity sign profile

## Statement

For Du-Wang's driver \(h_{31}(t)=-(t-a)^3\psi''(t)+(t-a)^2\psi'(t)-2(t-a)\psi(t)+2\log\Gamma(t)\), one has \(h_{31}(a+)=2\log\Gamma(a)\) and \(h_{31}(t)=(2a-1)\log t+O(1)\) as \(t\to\infty\).
