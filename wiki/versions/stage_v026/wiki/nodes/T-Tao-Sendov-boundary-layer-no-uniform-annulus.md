# Theorem: Tao-Sendov boundary layer no uniform annulus

## Statement

No q-uniform positive lower bound for the tangent-cluster root-gap defect can hold, because Delta(r,q) tends to zero as q approaches r or 1 and the limiting large root approaches the unit circle.
\[
q\downarrow r\text{ or }q\uparrow1\Rightarrow \Delta(r,q)\to0\Rightarrow 1-s\to0\text{ along large }d.
\]

## Do not claim

- This does not refute fixed-parameter tangent-cluster stability.
- This only rules out a uniform root-gap annulus for this model.
