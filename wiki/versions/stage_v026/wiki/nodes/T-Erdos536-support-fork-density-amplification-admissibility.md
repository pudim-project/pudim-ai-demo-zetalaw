# Theorem: Erdos536 support fork density amplification admissibility

## Statement

Density-amplification admissibility theorem: for every fixed \(0<\theta<1\), \(\eta>0\), and every admissible non-rank-thin positive-mass family \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,	heta}\), support-level disjoint defect edges below occupied tops cannot occur only in vanishing conditional mass unless the family is rank-thin or already has a support-level fork obstruction sufficient for the union-free frontier. Equivalently, any remaining density route converts admissible support-level matching edges into \(\mathbf E[\Omega_k(C)\mid C\in\mathcal F_k]>0\).
