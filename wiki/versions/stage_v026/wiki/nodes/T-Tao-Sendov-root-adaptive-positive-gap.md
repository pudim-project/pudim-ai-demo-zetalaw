# Corollary: Tao-Sendov root-adaptive positive gap

## Statement

In the interior regime, if Delta(r,q) is at least 40/(dc0), then the root-adaptive defect has the positive lower bound 1-s >= Delta(r,q)/4.
\[
\Delta(r,q)\ge40/(dc_0)\Rightarrow 1-s\ge\Delta(r,q)/4.
\]
