# Theorem: Laplace transform of completely monotone density is Stieltjes

## Statement

If \(\phi\) is completely monotone on \((0,\infty)\) and \(F(x)=\int_0^\infty e^{-xt}\phi(t)\,dt\) is finite for \(x>0\), then \(F\) is a Stieltjes function.
