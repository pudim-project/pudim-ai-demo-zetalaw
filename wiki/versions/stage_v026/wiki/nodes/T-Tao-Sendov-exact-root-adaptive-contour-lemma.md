# Theorem: Tao-Sendov exact root-adaptive contour lemma

## Statement

For the tangent-cluster quadratic, if s is the largest free-critical root modulus below one, then rho=(1+s)/2 avoids both root radii, encloses exactly the roots below one, and has product gap at least ((1-s)/2)^2.
\[
\rho_*=(1+s)/2,\quad \prod_j|\rho_*-|y_j||\ge((1-s)/2)^2.
\]
