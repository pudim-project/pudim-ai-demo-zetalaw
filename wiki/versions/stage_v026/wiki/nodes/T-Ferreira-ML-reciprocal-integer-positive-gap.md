# Theorem: Ferreira Mittag Leffler reciprocal integer positive exponential gap

## Statement

For every integer \(m\ge2\) and every \(t>0\), \(E_{1/m}(t^{1/m})>e^t\), where \(E_\alpha(z)=\sum_{k\ge0}z^k/\Gamma(\alpha k+1)\).
