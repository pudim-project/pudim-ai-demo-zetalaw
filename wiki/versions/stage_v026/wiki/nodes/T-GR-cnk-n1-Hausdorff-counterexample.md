# Theorem: Girjoaba Rasa c(n,k) n=1 Hausdorff moment counterexample

## Statement

The \(n=1\) Girjoaba--Rasa sequence \(c(1,0)=1,c(1,1)=1/2,c(1,2)=1\) violates the nonincreasing condition for Hausdorff moment sequences.
