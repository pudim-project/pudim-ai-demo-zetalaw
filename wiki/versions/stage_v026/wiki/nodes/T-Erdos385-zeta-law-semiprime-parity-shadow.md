# Theorem: Erdos 385 zeta-law semiprime parity shadow

## Statement

In the finite zeta-law model over a finite prime set P, the mass of Omega=2 and the Liouville parity expectation have exact Euler-product formulas, and local parity correlations decay across an omitted prime tail.
\[
\mu_P(\Omega=2)=\prod_{p\in P}(1-1/p)(A_P^2+B_P)/2,\quad \mathbb E(-1)^\Omega=\prod_{p\in P}(p-1)/(p+1).
\]
