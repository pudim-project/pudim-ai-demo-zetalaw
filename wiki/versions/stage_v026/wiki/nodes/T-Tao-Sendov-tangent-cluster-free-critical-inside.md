# Theorem: Tao-Sendov tangent-cluster free critical point inside

## Statement

For the tangent-cluster normal form P_{m,N,r}(z)=(z-r)(z+1)^m(z-tau)^N, tau=r/2+i sqrt(1-r^2/4), with 0<r<=1 and m,N>=1, there is a free critical point inside D(r,1).
\[
\exists\zeta\text{ free}: P'_{m,N,r}(\zeta)=0,\quad |\zeta-r|<1.
\]
