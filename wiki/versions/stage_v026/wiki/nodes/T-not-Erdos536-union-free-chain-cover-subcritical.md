# Theorem: Erdos 536 exact rank layers refute universal union free chain cover subcriticality

## Statement

not(For every fixed \(0<\theta<1\), every high-support union-free family \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) has subcritical weighted maximum-fiber chain-cover number \(C_k(\theta)=o(\sqrt{S_k})\).) Exact rank layers \(\mathcal R_k=\{S\subseteq P_k:|S|=\lfloor\theta S_k\rfloor+1\}\) are union-free and high-support, but their maximum fiber at \(m=k\) has chain-cover number \(\binom{k-1}{r_k-1}\), giving \(C_k(\theta)\ge p_k^{-1}\binom{k-1}{r_k-1}\not=o(\sqrt{S_k})\).
