# Theorem: determine sharp alpha beta exponents for Delta=(psi')^2+psi'' double inequality

## Statement

For \(\Delta(x)=(\psi'(x))^2+\psi''(x)\) and \(A(x)=(x^2+4x+12)/(12(x+1)^2)\), determine the sharp exponent window in the Qi--Agarwal double inequality for \(\Delta(x)\); equivalently, prove the source claim that the inequality holds on \((0,\infty)\) exactly for \(\alpha\ge6/5\) and \(\beta\le1\).
