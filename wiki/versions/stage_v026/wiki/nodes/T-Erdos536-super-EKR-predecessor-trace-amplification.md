# Theorem: Erdos 536 non sparse predecessor regime amplifies above local EKR threshold

## Statement

Super-EKR predecessor-trace amplification theorem: in a positive-mass fork-free coherent predecessor-star system with occupied tops in a central linear window, suppose the predecessor regime is not captured, up to \(o(1)\) \(\nu_k\)-mass, by \(o(\sqrt{S_k})\) rank/center layers and is not central-lacunary. Then some central close-rank occupied top \(C\), with \(|C|=s\), has a predecessor lower trace \(\mathcal A_r(C)=\{A\in\mathcal F_k:A\subsetneq C, |A|=r\}\), \(r<s\le2r\), satisfying \(|\mathcal A_r(C)|>\binom{s-1}{r}\). Hence `T-Erdos536-top-local-EKR-trace-threshold-forces-fork` gives a fork, and the fork-free alternative in `T-Erdos536-sparse-predecessor-layer-mass-collapse` follows.
