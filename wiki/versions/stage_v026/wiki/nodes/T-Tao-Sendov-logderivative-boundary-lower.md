# Theorem: Tao-Sendov logarithmic-derivative boundary lower bound

## Statement

For the exact tangent-cluster normalized logarithmic derivative, the boundary minimum on |y|=rho has a lower bound depending only on the free-critical root moduli and denominator upper bounds.
\[
\mu_\rho\ge d\prod_{j=1}^2|\rho-|y_j||/((1+r+\rho)(1+\rho)).
\]
