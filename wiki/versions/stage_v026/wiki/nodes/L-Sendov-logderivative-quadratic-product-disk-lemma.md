# Lemma: Sendov logarithmic-derivative quadratic product disk lemma

## Statement

If a quadratic Ay^2+By+C has roots y1,y2 and |C/A|<1, then at least one root has modulus less than one; quantitatively min(|y1|,|y2|)<=sqrt(|C/A|).
\[
y_1y_2=C/A\Rightarrow \min(|y_1|,|y_2|)\le\sqrt{|C/A|}.
\]
