# Theorem: Yang Detemple R normalized n0 forced constants a0 21 over 5 b0 1

## Statement

In the normalized \(n=0\) Yang--Detemple problem for \(R(x)=\psi(x+1/2)-\log x\), setting \(a_1=24\) forces \(b_0=1\) and \(a_0=21/5\) if the first nonzero tail order is to be \(x^{-4}\).
