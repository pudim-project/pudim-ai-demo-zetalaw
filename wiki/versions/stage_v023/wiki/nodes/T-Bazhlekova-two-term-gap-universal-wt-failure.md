# Theorem: Bazhlekova two term gap universal wt positivity failure

## Statement

For every two-term symbol \(g(s)=c s^a+d s^b\), with \(c,d>0\), \(1<a\le2\), \(0<b<a\), and \(a-b>1\), Bazhlekova's propagation property \(w_t\ge0\) fails for some \(x,t>0\). Equivalently, for some \(x>0\), the Laplace transform \(e^{-x\sqrt{g(s)}}\) is not completely monotone on \((0,\infty)\).
