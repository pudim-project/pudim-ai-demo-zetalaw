# Theorem: Tao-Sendov logarithmic-derivative Rouche stability

## Statement

If the normalized logarithmic-derivative perturbation bound is smaller than the exact model boundary minimum on |y|=rho, then the perturbed and exact models have the same free critical zero count inside |y|<rho.
\[
E_{\rho,e}<\mu_\rho\Rightarrow N(F,|y|<\rho)=N(F_0,|y|<\rho).
\]
