# Theorem: Bazhlekova Wright two seed compact zero twenty positive

## Statement

For the two no-cover seed Wright functions \(\mathcal W_{3/4,11/10}\) and \(\mathcal W_{11/20,21/20}\), one has \(\mathcal W_{\alpha,p}(x)>0\) on \(0\le x\le20\).
