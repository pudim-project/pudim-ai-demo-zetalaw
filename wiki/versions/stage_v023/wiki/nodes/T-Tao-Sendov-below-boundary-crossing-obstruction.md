# Theorem: T-Tao-Sendov-below-boundary-crossing-obstruction

## Statement

In the below-boundary chart q=r-mu/d for the tangent-cluster quadratic, the large-root unit-circle gap has leading term kappa_r(r^2(3-r^2)-mu)/d + O(d^-2). Thus the leading crossing occurs at mu=r^2(3-r^2).

## Scope

- Asymptotic obstruction in the q<r tangent-cluster layer.
