# Theorem: Gomilko Tomilov arbitrary nonspecial alpha greater one half residual open

## Statement

After the source partial cases and the finite-factor denominator subclass, the arbitrary non-special Gomilko--Tomilov fractional-power closure problem remains open locally, especially for \(1/2<\alpha<1\) when no finite Bernstein-factor representation of \(x/\psi(x)\) is available.
