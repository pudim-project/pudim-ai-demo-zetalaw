# Theorem: near zero scaled Q2 cover below qJ plus compact and remaining covers proves terminal endpoint

## Statement

There is a near-zero asymptotic or scaled rational enclosure for \(Q_2(x)\) on \((0,\epsilon]\), for an explicit rational \(\epsilon<1409/5000\), proving \(Q_2(x)<q_J\), where \(q_J\) is the certified inner witness from \(J\). Combined with the true endpoint-sign certificate on \(J\), a compact one-crossing certificate for \(G\), and finite-middle/near-one upper bounds below \(q_J\), this determines \(L_2=Q_2(\xi)\) and \(\mathcal I_2=(Q_2(\xi),3]\).
