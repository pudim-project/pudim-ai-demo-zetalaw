# Theorem: x times Karp Sitnik sigma one delta one shifted ratio is complete Bernstein up to positive scalar

## Statement

Under the hypotheses \(q\ge1\) and \(b_i>a_i>0\), \(x\) times the Karp--Sitnik \(\sigma=1,\delta=1\) shifted ratio is a complete Bernstein function up to the positive scalar \(\prod_i b_i/a_i\).
