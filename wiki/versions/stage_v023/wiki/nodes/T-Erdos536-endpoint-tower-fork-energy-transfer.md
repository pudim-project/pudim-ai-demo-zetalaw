# Theorem: Erdos 536 endpoint tower residual transfers terminal mass into fork energy or is rank thin

## Statement

Every positive-mass residual endpoint-pair or endpoint-tower branch for Erdos 536 transfers positive terminal mass into the fork energy \(\Phi_k\), unless its terminal fibers are rank-thin and hence have vanishing prime-biased mass. Equivalently, coherent triangle-free endpoint shields cannot carry positive high-support mass while keeping \(\Phi_k=0\).
