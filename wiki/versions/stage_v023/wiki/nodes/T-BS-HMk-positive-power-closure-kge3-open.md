# Theorem: Bondesson Simon HMk positive power closure remains open for k at least 3

## Statement

Bondesson--Simon Remark 4(a)'s positive-power closure question remains open for \(HM_k\) in the higher finite orders \(k\ge3\): whether \(f\in HM_k\) implies \(f^p\in HM_k\) for every \(p\ge1\).
