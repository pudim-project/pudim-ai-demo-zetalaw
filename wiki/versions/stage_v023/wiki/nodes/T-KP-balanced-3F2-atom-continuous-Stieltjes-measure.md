# Theorem: balanced 3F2 hypergeometric Stieltjes measure has atom at one plus continuous hypergeometric density

## Statement

In the source-admissible balanced \(q=2\) case \(b_1+b_2=a_1+a_2\), Karp--Prilepkina's limiting formula represents \({}_3F_2(\sigma,a_1,a_2;b_1,b_2;-z)\) by a generalized Stieltjes measure with an atom at \(t=1\) and a continuous part proportional to \(t^{a_2-1}{}_2F_1(b_1-a_1+1,b_2-a_1+1;2;1-t)\,dt\).
