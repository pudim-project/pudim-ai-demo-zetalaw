# Definition: Bernstein polynomial basis

## Statement

For \(0\le k\le n\), \(p_{n,k}(x)=\binom nkx^k(1-x)^{n-k}\), and \((B_nf)(x)=\sum_{k=0}^n p_{n,k}(x)f(k/n)\).
