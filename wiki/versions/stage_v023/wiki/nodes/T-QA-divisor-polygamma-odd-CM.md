# Theorem: Qi-Agarwal divisor-polygamma sums are completely monotone for odd n

## Statement

For every odd positive integer \(n\), the divisor-polygamma sum \(f_n(x)=\sum_{km=n}[\psi^{(k)}(x)]^m\) is completely monotone on \((0,\infty)\).
