# Theorem: T-Tao-Sendov-product-root-uniform-perturbative-stability

## Statement

There exists an absolute epsilon_0>0 such that for every 0<r<=1 and all m,N>=1, any perturbation of the tangent-cluster roots with |alpha_i+1|<=epsilon_0 and |beta_j-tau|<=epsilon_0 has a critical point in D(r,1).

## Scope

- Local perturbative theorem for the tangent-cluster model; not a global Sendov theorem.
