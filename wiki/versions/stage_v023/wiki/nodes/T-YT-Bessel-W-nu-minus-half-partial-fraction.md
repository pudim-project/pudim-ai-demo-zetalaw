# Theorem: partial fraction expansion proves sqrt(s)coth(sqrt(s)) is Bernstein

## Statement

For \(g(s)=\sqrt{s}\coth\sqrt{s}\), one has \(g(s)=1+2s\sum_{n=1}^\infty(s+\pi^2n^2)^{-1}\) and \(g'(s)=2\sum_{n=1}^\infty \pi^2n^2(s+\pi^2n^2)^{-2}\); hence \(g'\) is completely monotone and \(g\) is a Bernstein function.
