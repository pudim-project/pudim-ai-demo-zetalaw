# Theorem: three regime outside Q2 cover below J witness plus compact one zero proves terminal endpoint

## Statement

There is a finite outside-cover certificate for \(Q_2\), split into a near-zero regime, a finite-middle regime, and a near-one regime, such that the atanh logarithm enclosure and Hurwitz-zeta integral-tail enclosure give exact upper bounds \(Q_2(x)<q_J\) on every outside interval, where \(q_J\) is a certified lower witness from \(J=[287345/1000000,287346/1000000]\). Combined with the compact one-zero certificate for \(G\), this determines \(L_2=Q_2(\xi)\) and \(\mathcal I_2=(Q_2(\xi),3]\).
