# Theorem: Erdos 536 linear central window predecessor star code avoids locally unique union pushforward

## Statement

Fix \(0\le\theta<1\). Choose constants \(\theta<a<1<b<2a\), and let \(\mathcal T_k=\{C\subseteq P_k: aS_k\le |C|\le bS_k\}\), where \(S_k=\sum_{i\le k}1/p_i\) and \(\nu_k(p_i\in S)=1/p_i\). Then \(\nu_k(\mathcal T_k)\to1\). If each \(C\in\mathcal T_k\) is assigned one center \(q(C)\in C\) and the singleton threshold-sharp predecessor star \(\{C\setminus\{q(C)\}\}\), then the union push-forward of two independent predecessor traces has \(\nu_k\)-conditional probability tending to zero of landing back in \(\mathcal T_k\). Thus positive top-code mass plus locally unique threshold-sharp centers alone does not force occupied-top hits of the lower-star union push-forward.
