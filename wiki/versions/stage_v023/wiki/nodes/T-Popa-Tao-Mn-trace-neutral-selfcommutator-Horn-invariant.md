# Theorem: T-Popa-Tao-Mn-trace-neutral-selfcommutator-Horn-invariant

## Statement

For trace-zero Hermitian H in M_n(C), inf{||V||^2 : H=VV^*-V^*V} equals the minimum beta_1 over beta_1>=...>=beta_n>=0 such that lambda(H) belongs to the Horn polytope Horn(beta,(-beta_n,...,-beta_1)). Equivalently it is the least t such that H=B-UBU^* with 0<=B<=tI.

## Scope

- Exact finite-dimensional Horn invariant for trace-neutral self-commutator norm.
