# Theorem: Tao-Sendov logarithmic derivative removes exponential cluster loss

## Statement

In tangent-cluster stability, switching from direct P'-Rouché to normalized logarithmic-derivative Rouché replaces multiplicative cluster-product perturbation by additive reciprocal perturbation, removing the exponential cluster-factor loss.
\[
F-F_0=y\sum(1/(a+y-\eta)-1/(a+y))+y\sum(1/(b+y-\theta)-1/(b+y)).
\]
