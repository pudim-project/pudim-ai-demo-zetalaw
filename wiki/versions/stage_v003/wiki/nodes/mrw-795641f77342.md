# Proposition: Euler-score decomposition

## Statement

For every \(\beta>1\),
\[
-\bigl(\log\zeta\bigr)'(\beta)
=\mathbb E_\beta[\log N]
=\sum_{d=1}^{\infty}\Lambda(d)\mu_{d,\beta}(0)
=\sum_{d=1}^{\infty}\frac{\Lambda(d)}{d^\beta}.
\]
Equivalently,
\[
-\frac{\zeta'(\beta)}{\zeta(\beta)}
=\sum_{d=1}^{\infty}\frac{\Lambda(d)}{d^\beta}.
\]

## Proof

Use the von Mangoldt identity
\[
\log n=\sum_{d\mid n}\Lambda(d).
\]
Then
\[
\mathbb E_\beta[\log N]
=\sum_{n\ge1}\rho_\beta(n)\sum_{d\mid n}\Lambda(d)
=\sum_{d\ge1}\Lambda(d)\mathbb P_\beta(d\mid N).
\]
For the zeta law,
\[
\mathbb P_\beta(d\mid N)
=\frac{1}{\zeta(\beta)}\sum_{m\ge1}(dm)^{-\beta}
=d^{-\beta}.
\]
The identity with \(-(\log\zeta)'(\beta)\) follows from the zeta free-energy derivative.
