# Theorem: not Erdos 536 cover ready biased lower trace mass theorem on rank diffuse tops

## Statement

not(For every fixed \(0<\theta<1\) and \(\eta>0\), every positive-mass non-rank-thin \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) has cover-ready biased lower-trace mass below a positive-mass set of occupied tops forcing \(\mathbf E[\psi_k(C)\mid C\in\mathcal F_k]>0\).) The counterexample to the lower-trace-mass part is \(\mathcal F_k=H_{k,\theta}\), whose biased lower-trace mass below typical tops tends to zero while its \(\nu_k\)-mass tends to one and it is not rank-thin.
