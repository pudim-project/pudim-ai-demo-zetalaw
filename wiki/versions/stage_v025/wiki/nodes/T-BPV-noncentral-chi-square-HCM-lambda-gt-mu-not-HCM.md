# Theorem: BPV noncentral chi-square lambda greater than mu not HCM

## Statement

For \(\mu>0\) and \(\lambda>\mu\), the noncentral chi-square density \(\chi_{\mu,\lambda}\) is not hyperbolically completely monotone.
