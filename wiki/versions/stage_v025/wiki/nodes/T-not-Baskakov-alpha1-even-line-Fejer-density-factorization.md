# Theorem: not Baskakov alpha one even line universal Fejer density factorization

## Statement

There is no universal nonnegative Fejer-type or sine-square factorization of the actual inverse-Laplace density of \(f^{[2m]}_1\) for all integers \(m\ge2\).
