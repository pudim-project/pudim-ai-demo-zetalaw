# Theorem: not Baskakov alpha one even line positive inverse Laplace density for all m

## Statement

Not every \(f^{[2m]}_1(x)=1/((1+x)^{2m}-x^{2m})\) admits a nonnegative inverse-Laplace density on \([0,\infty)\).
