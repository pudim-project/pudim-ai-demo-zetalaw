# Theorem: one over x times one plus x squared has nonnegative Laplace kernel one minus cos t

## Statement

For \(x>0\), \(1/(x(1+x^2))=\int_0^\infty e^{-xt}(1-\cos t)\,dt\), and therefore \(x\mapsto 1/(x(1+x^2))\) is completely monotone.
