# Theorem: Bazhlekova no cover seeds all order coefficient discriminant Bernstein sign pattern

## Statement

At the no-cover seeds \((a,b)=(3/2,2/5)\) and \((a,b)=(11/10,1/20)\), the signed derivative polynomials \(R_n(y)=(-1)^{n-1}Q_n(y)\) satisfy the all-order coefficient/discriminant pattern observed through order \(201\): for every \(n\ge1\), either all coefficients of \(R_n\) are positive, or the only negative coefficient is the coefficient of \(y^{n-1}\) and the top quadratic has negative discriminant. Consequently \(R_n(y)>0\) for all \(y>0\) and all \(n\ge1\) at both seeds.
