# Theorem: unique G zero in B and global Q2 maximum at xi

## Statement

Let \(B=[1409/5000,293/1000]\) and \(G(x)=x\log x\,\Lambda(x)-\log R(x)\). The function \(G\) has a unique zero \(\xi\in B\), \(Q_2\) attains its global supremum on \((0,1)\) at \(\xi\), and no \(x\notin B\) has \(Q_2(x)\ge Q_2(\xi)\).
