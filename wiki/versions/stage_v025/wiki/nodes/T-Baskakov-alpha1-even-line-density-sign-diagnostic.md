# Theorem: Baskakov alpha one even line finite trigonometric density sign diagnostic

## Statement

A diagnostic audit of the finite trigonometric inverse-Laplace density for \(f^{[2m]}_1\) either certifies nonnegativity for the first unresolved values \(m\ge3\), or isolates a rigorous negative-density witness and the smallest affected \(m\).
