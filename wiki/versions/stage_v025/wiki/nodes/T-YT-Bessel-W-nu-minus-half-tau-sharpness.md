# Theorem: Yang Tian Bessel W boundary slice tau exponent sharpness tau > 1/2 not Bernstein

## Statement

For \(\tau>1/2\), the function \(x\mapsto x^\tau\coth(x^\tau)=W_{-1/2}(x^\tau)\) is not a Bernstein function on \((0,\infty)\).
