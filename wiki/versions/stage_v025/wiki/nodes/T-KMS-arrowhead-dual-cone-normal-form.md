# Theorem: KMS arrowhead cone dual normal form under zw pairing

## Statement

For \(C=\{(x,y,z):x>0,xy>z^2\}\) and pairing \(xu+yv+zw\), the dual cone is \(C^*=\{(u,v,w):u\ge0,v\ge0,4uv\ge w^2\}\).
