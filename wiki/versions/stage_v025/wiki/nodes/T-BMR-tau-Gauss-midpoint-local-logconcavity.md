# Theorem: BMR tau Gauss hypergeometric midpoint local log concavity

## Statement

For \(c>0\), \(\tau>0\), and \(0<z<1\), the function \(a\mapsto {}_2\phi^\tau_1(a,c-a;c;z)\) is strictly locally log-concave at \(a=c/2\).
