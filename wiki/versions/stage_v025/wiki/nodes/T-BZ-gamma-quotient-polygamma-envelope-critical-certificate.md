# Theorem: Bulboaca Zayed gamma quotient finite polygamma envelope critical certificate

## Statement

There is a finite rational cover of \([1,8]\), using explicit logarithm, \(\log\Gamma\), \(\psi\), and \(\psi'\) enclosures, that proves the Bulboaca--Zayed numerator \(N\) is negative up to one rational microinterval around \(1.126207061\ldots\), positive after that microinterval, and has exactly one zero inside it.
