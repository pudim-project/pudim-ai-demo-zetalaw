# Theorem: delta_a=a-x log(1+a/x) strictly completely monotonic for a>0

## Statement

For every \(a>0\), the logarithmic defect \(\delta_a(x)=a-x\log(1+a/x)\) is strictly completely monotonic on \((0,\infty)\).
