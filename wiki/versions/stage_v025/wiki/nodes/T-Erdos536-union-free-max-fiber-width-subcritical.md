# Theorem: Erdos 536 union free high support families have subcritical max fiber antichain width

## Statement

For every fixed \(0<\theta<1\), every high-support union-free family \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) has subcritical weighted maximum-fiber antichain-cover width \(B_k(\theta)=o(\sqrt{S_k})\).
