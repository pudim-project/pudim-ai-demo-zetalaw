# Theorem: Baskakov alpha one even line positive inverse Laplace density implies complete monotonicity

## Statement

For every integer \(m\ge2\), the rational function \(f^{[2m]}_1(x)=1/((1+x)^{2m}-x^{2m})\) admits a nonnegative inverse-Laplace density on \([0,\infty)\). Hence \(f^{[2m]}_1\) is completely monotone on \((0,\infty)\).
