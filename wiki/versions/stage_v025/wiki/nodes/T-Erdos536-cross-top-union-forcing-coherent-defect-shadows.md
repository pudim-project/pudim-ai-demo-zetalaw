# Theorem: Erdos 536 coherent intersecting occupied top defect shadows force cross top union fork

## Statement

For every fixed \(0<\theta<1\) and \(\eta>0\), let \(S_k=\sum_{i\le k}1/p_i\). If \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) has \(\nu_k(\mathcal F_k)\ge\eta\), is not rank-thin, and every occupied-top defect shadow \(\mathsf D_{\mathcal F_k}(C)=\{C\setminus A:A\in\mathcal F_k,\ A\subsetneq C\}\) is intersecting, then cross-top coherence forces a union fork: there exist distinct \(A,B,C\in\mathcal F_k\) with \(A\cup B=C\), where the witnesses \(A\) and \(B\) arise as lower traces under occupied tops in the same global system.
