# Theorem: Bazhlekova finite high order split map near a=3/2 and a=11/10 residual line slices

## Statement

Construct a certified finite high-order split map near the residual line slices \((a,b)=(3/2,b)\) and \((a,b)=(11/10,b)\): rational \(b\)-intervals are classified either by explicit odd-polynomial witnesses \(Q_{2q+1}(y)<0\) up to a stated order cutoff, or by finite signed-polynomial positivity certificates through a stated order cutoff.
