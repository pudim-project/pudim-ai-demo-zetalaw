# Theorem: GGPS h small-x expansion and local convexity

## Statement

The GGPS boundary function has the small-\(x\) expansion \(h(x)=(\sqrt2-1)x+(\sqrt2\pi^2/12)x^3+O(x^4)\); consequently \(h''(x)>0\) for all sufficiently small positive \(x\).
