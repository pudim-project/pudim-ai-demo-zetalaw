# Open_Problem: Ressel five-scalar angular inequality

## Statement

For U,M,H>=0 and alpha,beta in [0,pi/2], prove or refute L_{U,H}(min(pi/2,alpha+beta))+L_{M,M}(0)-L_{U,M}(alpha)-L_{M,H}(beta)>=0, where L_{r,s}(theta)=exp(-sqrt(r^2+s^2-2rs cos theta))+exp(-sqrt(r^2+s^2+2rs cos theta)).
