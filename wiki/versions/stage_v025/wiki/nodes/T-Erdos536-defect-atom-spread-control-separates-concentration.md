# Theorem: Erdos536 defect atom spread control separates concentration

## Statement

Defect atom/spread control theorem: in a lower-trace-rich admissible local model, separate large atom mass \(\sum_D\lambda_C(D)^2\) from honest coordinate concentration \(\sum_i\lambda_C(i\in D)^2\). Either atom mass is small enough that the top-averaged \(L^2\)-small coverage theorem applies after distinct-pair conditioning, or a large atom produces an explicit repeated lower-trace/chain obstruction that must be classified independently of coordinate concentration.
