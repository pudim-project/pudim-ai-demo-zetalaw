# Theorem: Yin p,k-digamma complete monotonicity sharp alpha necessity alpha <= 1

## Statement

For \(p\in\mathbb N\), \(k>0\), and \(\delta_{p,k,\alpha}(x)=x^\alpha\left[\frac1k\log\frac{pkx}{x+k(p+1)}-\psi_{p,k}(x)\right]\), prove that complete monotonicity of \(\delta_{p,k,\alpha}\) on \((0,\infty)\) implies \(\alpha\le1\).
