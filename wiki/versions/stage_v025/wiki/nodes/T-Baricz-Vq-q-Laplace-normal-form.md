# Theorem: Baricz V_q positive Laplace representation in q plus one

## Statement

For every \(x>0\) and \(q>-1\), Baricz's function satisfies \(V_q(x)=\pi^{-1/2}\int_0^\infty s^{-1/2}e^{-x^2s}(1+s)^{-(q+1)}\,ds\).
