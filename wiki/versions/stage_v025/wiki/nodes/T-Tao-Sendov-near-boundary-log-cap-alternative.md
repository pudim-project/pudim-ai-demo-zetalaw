# Theorem: Tao-Sendov near-boundary logarithmic cap alternative

## Statement

If Sendov fails at a simple zero a=re^{i theta}, then at least floor(log n/log(1+r))+1 other zeros lie in the opposite cap Re(e^{-i theta}z)<=r/2; in particular at least floor(log_2 n)+1 lie in Re(e^{-i theta}z)<=1/2.
\[
\#\{i\ge2:\Re(e^{-i\theta}z_i)\le r/2\}\ge\lfloor\log n/\log(1+r)\rfloor+1.
\]
