# Theorem: Erdos 536 full high support family is non rank thin but has vanishing biased lower trace mass under typical tops

## Statement

For every fixed \(0<\theta<1\), the full high-support family \(H_{k,\theta}\) satisfies \(\nu_k(H_{k,\theta})\to1\) and is not rank-thin, but for \(C\sim\nu_k\) conditioned on \(C\in H_{k,\theta}\), \(\mathbf E[\mu_C(\mathcal L_{H_{k,\theta}}(C))\mid C\in H_{k,\theta}]\to0\).
