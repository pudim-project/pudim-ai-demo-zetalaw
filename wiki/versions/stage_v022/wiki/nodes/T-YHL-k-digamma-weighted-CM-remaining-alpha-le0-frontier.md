# Theorem: YHL weighted k-digamma CM remaining alpha <= 0 frontier

## Statement

After the \(\alpha=0\) base classification and the positive-\(\alpha\) endpoint obstruction, the remaining nontrivial Yin--Huang--Lin weighted complete-monotonicity frontier is the classification for \(\alpha\le0\), together with possible zero-bracket degeneracies.
