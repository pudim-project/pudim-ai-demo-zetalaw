# Theorem: T Erdos536 residual moving base family construction or exit

## Statement

Residual moving-base family construction or forced exit: attempt to construct a positive-mass high-support non-rank-thin lower-trace-poor admissible family satisfying every negated exit in the residual-frontier normal form. If construction fails, prove that one named exit must occur: \(o(\sqrt{V_k})\)-antichain cover, subcritical deletion multiplicity, finite core, endpoint/core shield, rank-thinness, source-forbidden union/comparable configuration, or \(\nu_k\)-vanishing.
