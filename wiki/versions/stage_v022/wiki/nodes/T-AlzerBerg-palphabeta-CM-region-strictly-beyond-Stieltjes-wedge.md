# Theorem: Alzer-Berg p alpha beta CM region strictly exceeds the Stieltjes wedge

## Statement

The complete-monotonicity region for \(p_{\alpha,\beta}(x)=e^\alpha-(1+\alpha/x)^{x+\beta}\) strictly contains the Stieltjes wedge \(-1<\beta\le0\), \(0<\alpha\le\beta+1\): specifically, \(p_{2,0}\) is completely monotone although \((2,0)\) is outside the wedge.
