# Note: Four-layer zeta-law framework

## Statement

The PDF summary organizes the theory into four layers:

- Moment layer:
  \[
  \frac{\zeta(s+r)}{\zeta(s)}=\mathbb E_s[N^{-r}].
  \]
- Free-energy curvature layer:
  \[
  (\log\zeta)''(s)=\operatorname{Var}_s(\log N).
  \]
- Mellin-Planck free-energy layer:
  \[
  M(s)=\Gamma(s)\zeta(s),\qquad
  (\log M)''(s)=\operatorname{Var}_{\nu_s}(\log t).
  \]
- Modular entropy layer:
  \[
  \Sigma(\beta)=\sup_{q\ge2}B_q(\beta).
  \]
