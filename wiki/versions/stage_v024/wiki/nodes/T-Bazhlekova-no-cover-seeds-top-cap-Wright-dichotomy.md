# Theorem: Bazhlekova no cover seeds top cap Wright positivity negativity dichotomy

## Statement

At the no-cover seeds \((a,b)=(3/2,2/5)\) and \((11/10,1/20)\), the high-degree top-cap scaling of \(R_n(y)=(-1)^{n-1}Q_n(y)\), with \(y\sim n^{a-b}\lambda\), has a rigorous Wright-type limit that either stays positive for every \(\lambda>0\) or gives a certified negative limit value yielding high-order odd-derivative obstructions.
