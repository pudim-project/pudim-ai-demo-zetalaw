# Theorem: T-Popa-Tao-M4-selfcommutator-spectral-radius-formula

## Statement

For every trace-zero Hermitian H in M4(C), inf{||V||^2 : H=VV^*-V^*V}=max(lambda_max(H),-lambda_min(H)).

## Scope

- Finite-dimensional trace-neutral Hermitian self-commutator classification in M4; not a solution of Tao's infinite B(H) model.
