# Negative_Result: T-not-Tao-Sendov-q-ge-r-enforced-in-tangent-reduction

## Statement

The tangent-cluster reduction does not enforce q=m/(m+N)>=r. For every fixed 0<r<1 there are admissible m,N>=1 with q<r, and the cap-obstruction family has q<r for all sufficiently large n. At r=1 every admissible choice has q<r.

## Scope

- Local tangent-cluster parameter analysis; not a global Sendov theorem.
