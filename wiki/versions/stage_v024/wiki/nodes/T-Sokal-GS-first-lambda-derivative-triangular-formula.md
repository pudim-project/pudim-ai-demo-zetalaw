# Theorem: first lambda derivative of Sokal generalized Stieltjes tests is positive triangular combination

## Statement

For Sokal's generalized-Stieltjes tests, \(\partial_\lambda F^{[\lambda]}_{n,k}(x)=\sum_{r=0}^{k-1} k!F^{[\lambda]}_{n,r}(x)/(r!(k-r))\) for \(k\ge1\), with the \(k=0\) derivative equal to \(0\).
