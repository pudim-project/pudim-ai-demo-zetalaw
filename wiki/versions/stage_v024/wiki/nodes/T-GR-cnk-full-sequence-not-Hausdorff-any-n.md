# Theorem: Girjoaba Rasa c(n,k) full sequence not Hausdorff for any n>=1

## Statement

For every \(n\ge1\), the full Girjoaba--Rasa sequence \(k\mapsto c(n,k)\), \(0\le k\le2n\), is not a Hausdorff moment sequence in the natural order.
