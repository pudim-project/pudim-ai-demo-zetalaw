# Theorem: Alzer Berg reciprocal Gini Gamma quotient logarithm diagonal cubic expansion

## Statement

For \(Q_{a,b}=1/P_{a,b}\), \(r=a+b\), and \(y=x+u\), one has \(\log Q_{a,b}(u,u+h;x)=h^3(\psi''(y)/24-(r-1)\psi'(y)/(8u))+O(h^4)\) as \(h\downarrow0\).
