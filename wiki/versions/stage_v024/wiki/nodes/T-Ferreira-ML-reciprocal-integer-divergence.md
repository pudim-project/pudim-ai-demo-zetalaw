# Theorem: Ferreira Mittag Leffler reciprocal integer lambda less equal minus one divergence

## Statement

For every integer \(m\ge2\) and every \(\lambda\le -1\), \(\int_0^\infty e^{-t}E_{1/m}(-\lambda t^{1/m})\,dt=+\infty\).
