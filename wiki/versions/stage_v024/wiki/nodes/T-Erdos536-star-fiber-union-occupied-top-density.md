# Theorem: Erdos 536 occupied top density for unions of threshold sharp star fibers

## Statement

Occupied-top density theorem for unions of star fibers: in a positive-mass threshold-sharp moving-star system \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) with nonsparse close central ranks, let \(A\) and \(B\) be lower traces sampled from two nonnegligible threshold-star fibers \(A\subseteq C_1\setminus\{q(C_1)\}\), \(B\subseteq C_2\setminus\{q(C_2)\}\) with incompatible centers. Then the union distribution assigns nonnegligible mass to occupied tops \(C\in\mathcal F_k\), unless the occupied top code has vanishing \(\nu_k\)-mass. Consequently, in the positive-mass case, there exist distinct \(A,B,C\in\mathcal F_k\) with \(A\cup B=C\).
