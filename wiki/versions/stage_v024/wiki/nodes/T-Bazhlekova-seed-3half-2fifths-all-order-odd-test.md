# Theorem: Bazhlekova no cover seed three halves two fifths all order odd derivative exact test

## Statement

For the no-cover seed \((a,b)=(3/2,2/5)\), an exact all-order audit of the recurrence polynomials \(Q_{2q+1}^{3/2,2/5}\) decides whether there exists an odd order \(2q+1\ge11\) and \(y_0>0\) with \(Q_{2q+1}^{3/2,2/5}(y_0)<0\), or whether all odd derivative polynomials stay nonnegative on \((0,\infty)\).
