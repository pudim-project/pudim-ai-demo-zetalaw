# Theorem: all lambda derivatives of Sokal generalized Stieltjes tests are nonnegative lower k combinations

## Statement

For every \(\ell\ge1\), Sokal's generalized-Stieltjes tests satisfy \(\partial_\lambda^\ell F^{[\lambda]}_{n,k}(x)=\sum_{r=0}^{k-\ell}(k!/r!)[z^{k-r}](-\log(1-z))^\ell F^{[\lambda]}_{n,r}(x)\), with nonnegative coefficients and empty sum \(0\).
