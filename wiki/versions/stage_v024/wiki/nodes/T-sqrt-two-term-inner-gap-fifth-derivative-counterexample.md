# Theorem: Bazhlekova two-term inner gap exact fifth derivative counterexample to Bernstein square root

## Statement

For \(h(s)=\sqrt{s^{28/25}+s^{1/50}}\), the exponents lie in the Bazhlekova two-term inner gap \(1<a<2\), \(0<b<a-1\), \(a-b>1\), \((a-1)^2+(b-1)^2<1\), but \(h^{(5)}(1)=-5570045943\sqrt2/320000000000<0\). Hence \(h\) is not a Bernstein function.
