# Theorem: Yin Zhang Open Problem 5.1 for Nielsen k-beta derivative ratio resolved by parity monotonicity

## Statement

Yin--Zhang Open Problem 5.1 on the monotonicity of \((x\beta_k(x))^{(n+1)}/((x\beta_k(x))^{(n)}(x\beta_k(x))^{(n+2)})\) is resolved: the ratio is strictly increasing for odd \(n\) and strictly decreasing for even \(n\).
