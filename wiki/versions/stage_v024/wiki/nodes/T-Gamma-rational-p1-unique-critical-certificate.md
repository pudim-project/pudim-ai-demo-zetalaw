# Theorem: H has unique global maximizer xi and p1 star equals H(xi)

## Statement

The function \(H(x)=x(\Gamma(1+x)-x)/(1-\Gamma(1+x))\) has a unique global maximizer \(\xi\in(0,1)\), the endpoint limits are strictly below \(H(\xi)\), and therefore \(p_1^*=H(\xi)\) for the Yang--Qian--Chu--Zhang upper rational Gamma bound.
