# Theorem: Szabo psi difference exact alpha0 equals one

## Statement

For \(0<d<1\), the sharp cutoff for complete monotonicity of \(y^lpha[\psi(y+d)-\psi(y)-d/y]\) is \(lpha_0=1\).
