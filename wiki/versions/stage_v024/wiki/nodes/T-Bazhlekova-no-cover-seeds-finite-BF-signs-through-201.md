# Theorem: Bazhlekova no cover seeds finite Bernstein derivative signs through order 201

## Statement

At the residual no-cover seeds \((a,b)=(3/2,2/5)\) and \((a,b)=(11/10,1/20)\), the derivative-polynomial normal form satisfies \((-1)^{n-1}Q_n(y)>0\) for every \(y>0\) and every \(1\le n\le201\), by an exact rational coefficient/discriminant certificate.
