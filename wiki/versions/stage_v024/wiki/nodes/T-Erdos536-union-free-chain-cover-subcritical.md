# Theorem: Erdos 536 union free high support families have subcritical weighted maximum fiber chain cover number

## Statement

For every fixed \(0<\theta<1\), every high-support union-free family \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) has subcritical weighted maximum-fiber chain-cover number \(C_k(\theta)=o(\sqrt{S_k})\), where \(c_{m,k}\) is the least number of chains covering the maximum fiber \(\mathcal F_{k,m}\) and \(C_k(\theta)=\sum_{T_m>\theta S_k/2}w_{m,k}c_{m,k}\).
