# Theorem: Mills ratio all-L fourth-order determinant inequality

## Statement

For every integer \(L\ge0\) and every \(t>0\), \(r^{(L+4)}(t)r^{(L)}(t)-4r^{(L+3)}(t)r^{(L+1)}(t)+3(r^{(L+2)}(t))^2\ge0\), where \(r\) is the standard normal Mills ratio.
