# Theorem: Erdos 536 fork free high support families are rank thin and hence vanish

## Statement

For every fixed \(0<\theta<1\), every high-support family \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) either contains a lower-shadow fork, or is rank-thin in the prime-biased measure sense: for every \(\varepsilon>0\), all but \(o(1)\) of \(\nu_k(\mathcal F_k)\) lies in at most \(\varepsilon\sqrt{S_k}\) exact cardinality layers. Since any \(o(\sqrt{S_k})\) exact-rank block has \(\nu_k\)-mass \(o(1)\), fork-free high-support families have \(\nu_k(\mathcal F_k)\to0\).
