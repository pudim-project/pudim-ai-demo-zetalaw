# Theorem: Karp Sitnik Gauss beta sigma one delta one shifted ratio is Stieltjes and completely monotone

## Statement

For \(b>a>0\), the Gauss ratio \({}_2F_1(1,a+1;b+1;-x)/{}_2F_1(1,a;b;-x)\) is a Stieltjes function, hence completely monotone, on \((0,\infty)\).
