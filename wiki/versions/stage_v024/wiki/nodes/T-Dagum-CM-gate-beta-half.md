# Theorem: Dagum auxiliary function is completely monotone for one less than beta at most two and alpha at least beta over two

## Statement

For \(f_{\alpha,\beta}(x)=x^{-\alpha}(1+x^\beta)^{-1}\), if \(1<\beta\le2\) and \(\alpha\ge\beta/2\), then \(f_{\alpha,\beta}\) is completely monotone on \((0,\infty)\).
