# Theorem: Bazhlekova Wright two seed compact zero ten positive

## Statement

For the two no-cover seed Wright functions \(\mathcal W_{3/4,11/10}\) and \(\mathcal W_{11/20,21/20}\), one has \(\mathcal W_{\alpha,p}(x)>0\) on \(0\le x\le10\). The proof combines the small-endpoint alternating domination on \([0,1]\) with an adaptive sign-separated interval certificate on \([1,10]\).
