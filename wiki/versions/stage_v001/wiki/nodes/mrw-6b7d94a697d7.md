# Theorem: Alzer-Kwong convexity and concavity pattern for reciprocal zeta

## Statement

Let \(F(x)=1/\zeta(x)\). The imported PDF claims that for every integer \(n\ge1\),
\[
F''(x)>0\quad\text{on }(-4n,-4n+2),
\]
and
\[
F''(x)<0\quad\text{on }(-4n-2,-4n).
\]

## Evidence

The PDF writes \(x=-u\), uses the functional equation to express \(1/\zeta(-u)\) as \(\pm G(u)\), and proves \(G''(u)>0\) from log-convexity estimates:
\[
\ell''(u)=-\psi'(u+1)-(\log\zeta)''(u+1)+\frac{\pi^2}{4}\csc^2\frac{\pi u}{2}.
\]
The curvature lemma supplies \((\log\zeta)''(u+1)<1/3\), while \(\psi'(u+1)<1/2\) and \(\csc^2(\pi u/2)\ge1\).
