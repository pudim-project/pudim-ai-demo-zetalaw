# Problem: Sroysang generalized Holder problem

## Statement

Sroysang asked how a Holder-type zeta inequality changes when the usual condition
\[
\sum_{i=1}^m\frac1{p_i}=1
\]
is replaced by
\[
\sum_{i=1}^m\frac1{p_i}=\frac1r,\qquad r\ge1.
\]

## Evidence

The PDF states that Sroysang's \(\xi(s)\) equals \(\zeta(s)\) for \(s>1\), not Riemann's completed xi-function, and imports a sharp extension.
