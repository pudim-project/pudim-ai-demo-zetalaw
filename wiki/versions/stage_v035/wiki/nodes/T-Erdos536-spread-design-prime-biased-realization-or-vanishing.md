# Theorem: T Erdos536 spread design prime biased realization or vanishing

## Statement

Spread-design prime-biased realization or vanishing theorem: for projective-plane-like pairwise-intersecting defect systems whose complement traces avoid the basic comparable/union obstruction, either realize them as actual lower traces below a positive-mass high-support non-rank-thin admissible family, or prove that any such realization is rank-thin, endpoint-shielded, violates actual lower-trace membership, or has vanishing \(\nu_k\)-mass. The proof must go beyond the already-refuted basic comparable/union exclusion.
