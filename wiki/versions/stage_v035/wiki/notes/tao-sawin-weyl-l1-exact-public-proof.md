# Tao--Sawin Weyl \(\ell^1\) exact minimum: public proof specialization

## Proof

Let \(A_1\) be the real Weyl algebra generated by \(X,D\) with \(DX-XD=1\), and let \(W_n\) be the finite set of all words with exactly \(n\) copies of \(D\) and \(n\) copies of \(X\). Apply Proposition~\ref{res:dual-certificate-extremal-l1-norm-principle} with \(V=A_1\), with coordinate atoms \(\mathcal A=W_n\), with target \(v=1\), and with
\[
M=\frac{2^n}{n!}.
\]

First we exhibit the primal representation of norm \(M\). Put
\[
P_n=\frac1{n!}\operatorname{ad}_D^n(X^n).
\]
Since \(\operatorname{ad}_D(X)=1\), repeated derivation gives \(\operatorname{ad}_D^n(X^n)=n!\), so \(P_n=1\) in \(A_1\). Expanding the iterated commutator gives
\[
P_n=
\frac1{n!}\sum_{a=0}^n(-1)^a\binom na D^{\,n-a}X^nD^a .
\]
Each displayed monomial is a word in \(W_n\), and therefore this is a primal representation of \(1\) by coordinate atoms. Its \(\ell^1\)-norm is
\[
\frac1{n!}\sum_{a=0}^n\binom na=\frac{2^n}{n!}=M.
\]

It remains to give the dual certificate. For \(q\in\mathbb C\), define the normal-order functional by
\[
\Phi_q(X^rD^s)=
\begin{cases}
q^r r!,& r=s,\\
0,& r\ne s.
\end{cases}
\]
Set
\[
\Lambda=\frac{2^n}{n!}\Phi_{-1/2}.
\]
Then \(\Lambda(1)=2^n/n!=M\). The Wick pairing formula for \(\Phi_q\) gives, for every balanced word \(w\in W_n\),
\[
|\Phi_{-1/2}(w)|\le n!\left(\frac12\right)^n=\frac{n!}{2^n},
\]
because every nonzero contraction has absolute value \(1/2\) and there are at most \(n!\) pairings. Hence \(|\Lambda(w)|\le1\) for every coordinate atom \(w\in W_n\).

Thus the primal representation and the dual functional satisfy the hypotheses of Proposition~\ref{res:dual-certificate-extremal-l1-norm-principle}. That proposition gives \(L_n\ge M\), while the displayed primal representation gives \(L_n\le M\). Consequently
\[
L_n=M=\frac{2^n}{n!},
\]
as claimed.
