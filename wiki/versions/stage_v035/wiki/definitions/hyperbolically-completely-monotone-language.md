## Definition

A positive density \(f\) on \((0,\infty)\) is hyperbolically completely monotone if, for each fixed \(u>0\), the function \(v\mapsto f(uv)f(u/v)\) is completely monotone as a function of \(v+v^{-1}\), in the usual hyperbolic variable.
