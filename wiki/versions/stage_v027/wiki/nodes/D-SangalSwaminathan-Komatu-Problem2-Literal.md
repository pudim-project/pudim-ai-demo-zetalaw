# Definition: Sangal-Swaminathan printed Komatu Problem 2 vocabulary

## Statement

Sangal--Swaminathan Section 4.3 prints Problem 2 asking for conditions on \(p,q,a,b\) such that, for \(f\in\mathcal A\), the generalized Komatu operator \(L[\eta]f(z)\) itself belongs to the Caratheodory class. The source displays the literal operator as \(L[\eta]f(z)=\phi_{p,q}(a,b;z)*f(z)\), a Hadamard product.
