# Theorem: Erdos 536 diagnostic lacunary moving center coherent shadow construction or block

## Statement

Diagnostic construction problem: attempt to construct a positive-mass, non-rank-thin, fork-free sequence \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) whose occupied-top defect shadows are coherent and intersecting but whose centers drift across a lacunary or moving-center rank profile. The construction should avoid the fixed-coordinate two-layer toggle collapse, avoid full-downset internal forks, and prevent cross-top unions by spacing ranks, centers, or top distributions. If no construction works, record which spacing or drift mechanism forces rank-thinness or a cross-top fork.
