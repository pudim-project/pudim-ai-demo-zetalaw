# Theorem: Baricz hypergeometric bivariate mean inequality classification problem for F_a(r)

## Statement

For bivariate means \(m_1\) and \(m_2\), determine conditions on \(a_1,a_2>0\) and \(c>0\) under which \(m_1(F_{a_1}(r),F_{a_2}(r))\le(\ge)F_{m_2(a_1,a_2)}(r)\) holds for all \(r\in(0,1)\), where \(F_a(r)={}_2F_1(a,c-a;c;r)\).
