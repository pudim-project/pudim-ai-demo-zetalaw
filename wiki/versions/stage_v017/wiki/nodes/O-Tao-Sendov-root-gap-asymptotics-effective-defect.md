# Open_Problem: Tao-Sendov root-gap asymptotics and effective defect frontier

## Statement

Estimate the root-gap defect 1-s in terms of r, q=m/(m+N), and d, especially through Delta(r,q)=(2+r)(q-r)(1-q), with boundary regimes handled separately.
\[
1-s\asymp \Delta(r,q)+O(d^{-1}),\quad \Delta(r,q)=(2+r)(q-r)(1-q).
\]
