# Theorem: Erdos 536 diagnostic coherent positive mass intersecting defect shadow system construction or block

## Statement

Diagnostic construction problem: construct, or rule out within natural templates, a sequence \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) with \(\liminf_k\nu_k(\mathcal F_k)>0\), not rank-thin, and every occupied-top defect shadow \(\mathsf D_{\mathcal F_k}(C)\) intersecting, while avoiding every union fork \(A\cup B=C\) among distinct members of \(\mathcal F_k\). Natural templates to test include moving-centered stars, layered intersecting defect families, random thinning of local shields, and top-distribution couplings designed to prevent cross-top unions.
