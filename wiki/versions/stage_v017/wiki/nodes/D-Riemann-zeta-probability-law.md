# Definition: Riemann zeta probability law

## Statement

For \(\beta>1\), the Riemann zeta probability law is the probability measure \(\rho_\beta\) on \(\mathbb N\) defined by \(\rho_\beta(n)=n^{-\beta}/\zeta(\beta)\). Equivalently, with energy \(E(n)=\log n\) and partition function \(Z(\beta)=\zeta(\beta)\), one has \(\rho_\beta(n)=e^{-\beta E(n)}/Z(\beta)\).
