# Open_Problem: Tao-Sendov boundary-layer large-root radial expansion

## Statement

Derive a uniform first-order formula for 1-|y_L| in the boundary layer Delta(r,q)=O(1/d), especially q-r=O(1/d) and 1-q=O(1/d), with explicit sign control.
\[
\Delta(r,q)\asymp d^{-1}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 1-|y_L|\text{ explicit to first order}.
\]
