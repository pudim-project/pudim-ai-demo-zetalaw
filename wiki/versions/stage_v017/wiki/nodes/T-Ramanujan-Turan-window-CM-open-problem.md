# Theorem: Ramanujan integral Turan window complete monotonicity open problem

## Statement

For \(n\ge2\), prove complete monotonicity of \(H_n(x;\alpha)=(I_R^{(n)}(x))^2-\alpha I_R^{(n-1)}(x)I_R^{(n+1)}(x)\) on \((0,\infty)\) for \((n-2)/(n-1)<\alpha<(n-1)/n\).
