# Theorem: Tao-Sendov radial logarithmic-derivative cap certificate

## Statement

Under conjugation symmetry after rotating a=r, if the real reciprocal cap contribution at some x0<r exceeds 1/(r-x0), then P' has a zero in D(a,1).
\[
\sum_{i=2}^n (x_0-\Re z_i)/|x_0-z_i|^2>1/(r-x_0)\Rightarrow \exists\xi\in(x_0,r):P'(\xi)=0.
\]
