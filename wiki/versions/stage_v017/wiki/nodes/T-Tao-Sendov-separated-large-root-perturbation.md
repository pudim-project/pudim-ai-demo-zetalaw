# Theorem: Tao-Sendov separated large-root perturbation

## Statement

If |C|>=c0 and d>=64/c0^2, the two roots of the tangent-cluster quadratic split into a small root of size at most 8/(dc0) and a large root within 10/(dc0) of -C.
\[
|y_S|\le8/(dc_0),\quad |y_L+C|\le10/(dc_0).
\]
