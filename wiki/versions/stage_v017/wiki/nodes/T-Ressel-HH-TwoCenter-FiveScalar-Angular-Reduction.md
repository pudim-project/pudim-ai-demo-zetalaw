# Theorem: Ressel two-center lemma reduces to a five-scalar angular inequality

## Statement

The Hilbert two-center lemma is implied by, and any failure is detected by, the five-scalar inequality L_{U,H}(min(pi/2,alpha+beta))+L_{M,M}(0)-L_{U,M}(alpha)-L_{M,H}(beta)>=0 for U,M,H>=0 and alpha,beta in [0,pi/2], where L_{r,s}(theta)=exp(-sqrt(r^2+s^2-2rs cos theta))+exp(-sqrt(r^2+s^2+2rs cos theta)).
