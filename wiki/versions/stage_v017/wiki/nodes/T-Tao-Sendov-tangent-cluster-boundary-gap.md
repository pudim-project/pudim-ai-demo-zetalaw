# Theorem: Tao-Sendov tangent-cluster boundary gap

## Statement

For the tangent-cluster model, the boundary minimum of P0' on |z-r|=rho has an explicit lower bound in terms of the cluster factors and the moduli of the two free-critical roots.
\[
\delta_\rho\ge(1+r-\rho)^{m-1}(1-\rho)^{N-1}d\prod_{j=1}^2|\rho-|y_j||.
\]
