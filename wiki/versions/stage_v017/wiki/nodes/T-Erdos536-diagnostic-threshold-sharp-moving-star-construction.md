# Theorem: Erdos 536 diagnostic threshold sharp moving star positive mass construction

## Statement

Threshold-sharp moving-star construction route: attempt to construct a positive-mass fork-free sequence \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) with nonsparse close central ranks such that each occupied top \(C\) carries only threshold-sharp star fibers \(\mathcal A_{r,q(C)}(C)\), the centers \(q(C)\) move so that no \(o(\sqrt{S_k})\) synchronized center/rank decomposition captures positive mass, and cross-top unions of lower star traces avoid occupied tops. If every natural template collapses, record whether the collapse comes from star-fiber mass concentration, cross-top center collisions, rank-thin lacunarity, or sparse-code vanishing.
