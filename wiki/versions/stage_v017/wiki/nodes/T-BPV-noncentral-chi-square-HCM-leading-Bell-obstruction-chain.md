# Theorem: noncentral chi square HCM leading Bell obstruction chain

## Statement

For the noncentral chi-square HCM problem, the leading small-\(u\) HCM sign constraints are generated by the Bell recurrence \[ B_0=1,\qquad B_{n+1}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}B_{n-k}c_{k+1}, \] with \[ c_1=\frac{\lambda/\mu-1}{2},\qquad c_j=j!\,b_j(\mu)\lambda^j\quad(j\ge2), \] where \[ \log\sum_{j\ge0}\frac{y^j}{4^j j!(\mu/2)_j}=\sum_{j\ge1}b_j(\mu)y^j. \] HCM requires \((-1)^nB_n\ge0\) for every \(n\ge1\).

## Proof

\emph{Setup.}
For
\[
\chi_{\mu,\lambda}(x)
=\frac12e^{-(x+\lambda)/2}
\left(\frac{x}{\lambda}\right)^{\mu/4-1/2}
I_{\mu/2-1}(\sqrt{\lambda x}),
\]
put
\[
H_u(w)=\chi_{\mu,\lambda}(uv)\chi_{\mu,\lambda}(u/v),
\qquad w=v+v^{-1}.
\]
The existing admitted expansion is
\[
\log H_u(w)
=C_u+\frac u2\left(\frac{\lambda}{\mu}-1\right)w
-\frac{\lambda^2u^2}{4\mu^2(\mu+2)}(w^2-2)
+O(u^3).
\]

The first two HCM necessary signs give the already admitted exclusions:
\[
\lambda>\mu
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\chi_{\mu,\lambda}\notin HCM,
\]
and
\[
(\lambda-\mu)^2<\frac{2\lambda^2}{\mu+2}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\chi_{\mu,\lambda}\notin HCM.
\]

Let
\[
\nu=\frac{\mu}{2}-1,
\qquad
S_\mu(y)=\sum_{j\ge0}
\frac{y^j}{4^j j!(\mu/2)_j},
\]
so that the non-power part of \(I_\nu(z)\) is \(S_\mu(z^2)\). Write
\[
\log S_\mu(y)=\sum_{j\ge1}b_j(\mu)y^j.
\]
The coefficients are generated by
\[
s_0=1,\qquad
s_j=\frac{1}{4^j j!(\mu/2)_j},
\]
and
\[
b_n=s_n-\frac1n\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}k\,b_k\,s_{n-k}.
\]

Set \(\rho=\lambda/\mu\). For the leading \(u^n\) coefficient of \(H_u^{(n)}(w)/H_u(w)\), define
\[
c_1=\frac{\rho-1}{2},
\qquad
c_j=j!\,b_j(\mu)(\mu\rho)^j
\quad(j\ge2).
\]
Let \(B_0=1\) and
\[
B_{n+1}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}B_{n-k}c_{k+1}.
\]
Then
\[
\frac{1}{H_u(w)}\frac{d^n}{dw^n}H_u(w)
=B_n(\mu,\rho)u^n+O(u^{n+1})
\]
uniformly on compact \(w\)-windows. Hence HCM requires
\[
(-1)^nB_n(\mu,\rho)\ge0
\qquad(n\ge1).
\]

This recovers the first- and second-derivative obstructions for \(n=1,2\).

A numerical scan of these leading sign constraints through \(n=20\) excludes additional portions of the previously surviving necessary range
\[
0<\lambda\le\mu,
\qquad
(\lambda-\mu)^2\ge\frac{2\lambda^2}{\mu+2}.
\]
Examples:

\[
\mu=2,\ \rho=0.2:
\quad
(-1)^7B_7<0,
\]
and
\[
\mu=10,\ \rho=0.5:
\quad
(-1)^4B_4<0.
\]

However, finite checks through \(n=20\) do not eliminate the whole surviving range. No full HCM range or full non-HCM theorem is proved.
