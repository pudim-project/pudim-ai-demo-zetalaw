# Theorem: boundary Kilbas Saigo conjecture as Laplace order between Gamma comparison and KS variable

## Statement

Let \(X_{\alpha,m}\) be a nonnegative random variable with Laplace transform \(\mathbb E e^{-xX_{\alpha,m}}=E_{\alpha,m,m-1/\alpha}(-x)\), and let \(Z_{\alpha,m}\) be Gamma with shape \(1+1/m\) and scale \(C_{\alpha,m}\). Then \(Z_{\alpha,m}\le_{Lt}X_{\alpha,m}\), meaning \(\mathbb E e^{-xZ_{\alpha,m}}\le \mathbb E e^{-xX_{\alpha,m}}\) for all \(x\ge0\).
