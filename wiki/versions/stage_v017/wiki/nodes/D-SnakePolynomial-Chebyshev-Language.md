# Definition: Snake polynomials and Chebyshev-expansion language

## Statement

A nonnegative continuous majorant \(\mu\) on \([-1,1]\) defines a degree-\(n\) snake-polynomial problem by the corridor condition \(|p(x)|\le \mu(x)\). A degree-\(n\) snake polynomial is the extremal polynomial, unique up to orientation in the source convention, with \(n+1\) alternating contact points. Chebyshev expansion means expansion in the first-kind Chebyshev basis \(T_0,T_1,T_2,\ldots\).
