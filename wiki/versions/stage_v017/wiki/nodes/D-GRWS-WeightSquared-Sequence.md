# Definition: GRWS weight-squared sequence

## Statement

For a geometrically regular weighted shift with parameters \(p>1\), \(-1<N,D<1\), the weights are \(\alpha_n=\sqrt{(p^n+N)/(p^n+D)}\), and the weights-squared interpolation sequence is \(a_n=\alpha_n^2=(p^n+N)/(p^n+D)\).
