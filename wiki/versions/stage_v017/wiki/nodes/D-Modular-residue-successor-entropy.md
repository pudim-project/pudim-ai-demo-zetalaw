# Definition: Modular residue distribution and successor entropy

## Statement

For a modulus \(q\), the modular residue distribution induced by \(\rho_\beta\) is \(\rho_{\beta,q}(a)=\sum_{n\equiv a\pmod q}\rho_\beta(n)\). The successor entropy is the relative-entropy expression comparing consecutive zeta-law weights, namely \(\sum_n\rho_\beta(n)\log(\rho_\beta(n)/\rho_\beta(n+1))\) whenever the displayed series is finite.
