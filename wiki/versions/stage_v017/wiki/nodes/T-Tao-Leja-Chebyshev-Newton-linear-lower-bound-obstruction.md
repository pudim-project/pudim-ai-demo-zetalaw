# Obstruction_Theorem: Ordering-independent linear lower bound for the Chebyshev-root Newton condition number

## Statement

Every ordering of the first-kind Chebyshev roots has Newton condition number at least csc(pi/(2n)), hence at least 2n/pi.
\[
Lambda_N(x) >= csc(pi/(2n)) >= 2n/pi for every ordering of X_n.
\]
