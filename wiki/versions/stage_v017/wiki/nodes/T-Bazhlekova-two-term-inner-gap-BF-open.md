# Theorem: Bazhlekova two-term inner gap Bernstein function or positivity failure remains open beyond concavity

## Statement

For \(g(s)=c s^a+d s^b\), \(c,d>0\), \(1<a<2\), \(0<b<a-1\), and \((a-1)^2+(b-1)^2\le1\), decide whether \(\sqrt g\) is a Bernstein function or whether Bazhlekova propagation positivity fails by a mechanism beyond concavity.
