# Theorem: Erdos 536 center profile regularization reduces close rank moving center density to coverage or rank thin sparse code alternatives

## Statement

Every positive-mass coherent intersecting-shadow moving-center system with nonsparse close-rank lower-trace density admits a center-profile regularization after discarding \(o(1)\) mass: either it is captured by \(o(\sqrt{S_k})\) center-stable rank/profile blocks, or it contains two regular center profiles satisfying the density and incompatibility hypotheses of `T-Erdos536-occupied-union-coverage-from-close-rank-density`. Consequently a fork-free residual close-rank moving-center obstruction must be rank-thin or sparse-code-like.
