# Theorem: Bazhlekova inner gap finite odd derivative resultant diagnostic map

## Statement

A finite resultant audit of the fifth, seventh, and ninth derivatives of \(h(s)=\sqrt{c s^a+d s^b}\), after the scaling \(y=(c/d)s^{a-b}\), either covers the residual inner gap by certified odd-derivative sign obstructions or returns explicit semialgebraic cells where those three derivative orders do not decide the problem.
