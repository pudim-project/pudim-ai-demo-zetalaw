# Open_Problem: Alzer-Berg p alpha beta residual CM region beyond Stieltjes wedge

## Statement

Determine whether the complete-monotonicity region for \(p_{\alpha,\beta}(x)=e^\alpha-(1+\alpha/x)^{x+\beta}\), inside the necessary strip \(-1<\beta\le0\), is exactly the Stieltjes wedge \(0<\alpha\le\beta+1\), or whether additional completely monotone but non-Stieltjes parameters occur.
