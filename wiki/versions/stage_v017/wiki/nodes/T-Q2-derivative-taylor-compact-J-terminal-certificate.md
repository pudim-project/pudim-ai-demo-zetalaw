# Theorem: derivative or Taylor compact certificate outside J plus covers proves terminal endpoint

## Statement

Using the true endpoint-sign certificate on \(J=[287345/1000000,287346/1000000]\), there is a derivative-bounded or Taylor-model rational certificate on \([1409/5000,293/1000]\setminus J\) proving \(G>0\) to the left of \(J\), \(G<0\) to the right of \(J\), and compact-bracket dominance below the certified witness \(q_J\). Combined with the true near-zero and near-one covers and a finite-middle cover below \(q_J\), this determines \(L_2=Q_2(\xi)\) and \(\mathcal I_2=(Q_2(\xi),3]\).
