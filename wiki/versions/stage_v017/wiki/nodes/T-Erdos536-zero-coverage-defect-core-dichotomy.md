# Theorem: Erdos536 zero coverage defect core dichotomy

## Statement

Assume an admissible sequence \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) has \(\nu_k(\mathcal F_k)\ge\eta\), is non-rank-thin, and has zero expected coordinate coverage below occupied tops. Then, after passing to positive-mass occupied tops, the conditional defect systems \(\mathcal D_C\) are asymptotically pairwise-intersecting and satisfy a dichotomy: either they are supported on \(o(\sqrt{S_k})\) defect ranks, or they have a moving common core/endpoint shield whose induced lower-trace family is coordinate-deficient. In both cases the original family is either rank-thin or fails the lower-trace spread needed by the selected node.
