# Theorem: Erdos 536 finite-prime valuation entropy extremal constants have infimum zero

## Statement

For every \(\varepsilon>0\) there is a finite prime set \(P\) such that the finite-prime weighted grid extremal constant \(M_P=\delta_P\int_1^\infty g_P(t)t^{-2}\,dt\) is at most \(\varepsilon\), where \(g_P\) is the largest grid-bad-free subset of \(\Gamma_P(t)\).
