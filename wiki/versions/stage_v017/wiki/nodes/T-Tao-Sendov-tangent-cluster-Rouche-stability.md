# Theorem: Tao-Sendov tangent-cluster Rouche stability

## Statement

For each fixed 0<r<=1 and m,N>=1, sufficiently small perturbations of the clusters at -1 and tau in the tangent-cluster normal form preserve at least one critical point in D(r,1).
\[
|u_\alpha+1|,|v_\beta-\tau|<\varepsilon(r,m,N,\rho)\Rightarrow \exists\zeta:P'(\zeta)=0,\ |\zeta-r|<1.
\]
