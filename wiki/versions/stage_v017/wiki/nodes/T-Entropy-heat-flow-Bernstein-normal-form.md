# Theorem: heat-flow entropy complete monotonicity has Bernstein Widder positive Laplace normal form

## Statement

For heat-flow entropy complete-monotonicity problems, the sign pattern for all time derivatives of the first entropy derivative is equivalent, when the relevant function is locally integrable and nonnegative, to a Bernstein--Widder positive Laplace representation in heat time.
