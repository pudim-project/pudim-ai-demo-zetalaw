# Definition: finite dimensional l1 primal representation coordinate atom dual linear functional certificate language

## Statement

Let \(V\) be a real vector space and let \(\mathcal A\subset V\) be a finite family. The elements of \(\mathcal A\) are the coordinate atoms. A primal representation of \(v\in V\) is a finite expansion \(v=\sum_{a\in\mathcal A} c_a a\), and its \(\ell^1\)-norm is \(\sum_{a\in\mathcal A}|c_a|\). A dual certificate for the lower bound \(M\) is a linear functional \(\Lambda:V\to\mathbb R\) such that \(\Lambda(v)=M\) and \(|\Lambda(a)|\le1\) for every \(a\in\mathcal A\).
