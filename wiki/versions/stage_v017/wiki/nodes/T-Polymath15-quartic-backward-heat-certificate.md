# Theorem: Polymath15 quartic backward-heat certificate

## Statement

For F0(x)=(x^2-1)(x^2-4), the deformation Ft=exp(-t d^2/dx^2)F0 has only real zeros for every t>=0.
\[
F_t(x)=x^4-(5+12t)x^2+(4+10t+12t^2),\quad \Delta(t)=96t^2+80t+9>0.
\]
