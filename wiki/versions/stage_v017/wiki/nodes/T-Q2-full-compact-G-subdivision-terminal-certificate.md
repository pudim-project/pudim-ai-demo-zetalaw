# Theorem: full compact G subdivision around J proves unique maximum and terminal endpoint

## Statement

There is a finite rational subdivision of \([1409/5000,293/1000]\), refined around \(J=[287345/1000000,287346/1000000]\), on which exact Hurwitz-zeta and atanh-logarithm enclosures prove \(G>0\) to the left of \(J\), \(G<0\) to the right of \(J\), a single zero \(\xi\in J\), and \(Q_2(x)<Q_2(\xi)\) on the rest of the compact bracket. Combined with certified endpoint-regime bounds, this determines \(L_2=Q_2(\xi)\) and \(\mathcal I_2=(Q_2(\xi),3]\).
