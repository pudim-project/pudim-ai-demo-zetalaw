# Theorem: Erdos 536 moving center drift in coherent intersecting shadows forces cross top fork

## Statement

For every fixed \(0<\theta<1\) and \(\eta>0\), let \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) have \(\nu_k(\mathcal F_k)\ge\eta\), not be rank-thin, and have coherent intersecting occupied-top defect shadows. If the shadow centers drift, in the sense that no \(o(\sqrt{S_k})\)-block center-stable decomposition captures all but \(o(1)\) mass, then there exist distinct \(A,B,C\in\mathcal F_k\) with \(A\cup B=C\). Hence every positive-mass fork-free coherent system is center-stable and collapses by the fixed-center theorem.
