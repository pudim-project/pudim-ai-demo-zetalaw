# Theorem: unique global critical zero xi gives L2=Q2(xi) and I2=(Q2(xi),3]

## Statement

There is a unique \(\xi\in(1409/5000,293/1000)\) with \(G(\xi)=0\), \(Q_2\) is strictly increasing before \(\xi\) and strictly decreasing after \(\xi\) on \((0,1)\), and therefore \(L_2=Q_2(\xi)\) and \(\mathcal I_2=(Q_2(\xi),3]\).
