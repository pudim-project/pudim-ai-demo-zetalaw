# Theorem: Erdos 536 sparse close rank moving center central window systems have vanishing biased mass

## Statement

For \((P_k,\nu_k,S_k,H_{k,\theta})\) as in the Erdos 536 prime-biased frontier, suppose \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) is fork-free, has coherent intersecting occupied-top defect shadows, and lies in the sparse-close-rank moving-center alternative: for every fixed \(M>1\), every dyadic rank interval \([r,2r]\subseteq[\theta S_k,M S_k]\) carries only \(o(1)\) \(\nu_k\)-mass from occupied tops whose lower traces have a nonnegligible common center profile. Then \(\nu_k(\mathcal F_k)\to0\). Equivalently, a positive-mass moving-center counterexample must have a nonsparse close-rank central window.
