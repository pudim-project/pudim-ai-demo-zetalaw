# Theorem: Erdos 536 center entropy compression or high entropy star fiber fork

## Statement

Center-entropy compression theorem: every positive-mass fork-free threshold-sharp moving-star system with nonsparse close central ranks admits a weighted center-profile decomposition in which either the star-center entropy compresses into \(o(\sqrt{S_k})\) rank/center layers of vanishing \(\nu_k\)-mass, or the remaining high-entropy profiles force an occupied-union fork \(A\cup B=C\) through incompatible threshold-star fibers. Hence no positive-mass nonsparse threshold-sharp moving-star obstruction remains.
