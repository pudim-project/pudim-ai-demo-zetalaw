# Theorem: Erdos 536 below EKR predecessor star centers synchronize or force occupied union fork

## Statement

Predecessor EKR-star center synchronization theorem: in a positive-mass fork-free coherent predecessor-star system whose central close-rank traces stay at or below the EKR threshold \(\binom{s-1}{r}\), the near-threshold traces are, after discarding \(o(1)\) \(\nu_k\)-mass, organized by EKR-star centers. These centers either synchronize into \(o(\sqrt{S_k})\) rank/center layers, giving the mass-collapse alternative of `T-Erdos536-sparse-predecessor-layer-mass-collapse`, or two unsynchronized center profiles force an occupied-union fork through `T-Erdos536-occupied-union-coverage-from-close-rank-density`.
