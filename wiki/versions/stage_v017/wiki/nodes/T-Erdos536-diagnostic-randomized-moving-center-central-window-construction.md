# Theorem: Erdos 536 diagnostic randomized moving center central window construction

## Statement

Diagnostic construction problem: construct, or rule out within natural randomized templates, a positive-mass fork-free sequence \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) whose occupied-top defect shadows are coherent and intersecting, whose centers move across central ranks, and whose rank support is not sparse-close-rank in the sense of `T-Erdos536-sparse-close-rank-moving-center-mass-collapse`. Natural templates include randomized center assignments \(q(C)\), random sparse star traces below occupied tops, and dyadic mixtures designed to avoid both fixed-center close-rank forks and central-window mass collapse.
