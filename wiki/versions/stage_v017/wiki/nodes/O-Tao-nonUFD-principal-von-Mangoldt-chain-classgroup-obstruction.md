# Open_Problem: Class-group obstruction for principal von Mangoldt chains in non-UFDs

## Statement

For a number field with nontrivial class group, construct or rule out a von-Mangoldt-type Markov chain on elements or principal ideals alone that stays in that state space, is stochastic, admits an explicit invariant or hitting weight, and yields an Erdos-type primitive-element tail bound.
\[
Principal-only removal of prime-ideal factors generally exits the principal class or loses sum Lambda_K(q)=log N(a).
\]
