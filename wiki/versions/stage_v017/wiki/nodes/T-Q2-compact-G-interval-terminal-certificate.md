# Theorem: finite rational compact bracket G certificate proves unique xi, global L2=Q2(xi), I2=(Q2(xi),3]

## Statement

There is a finite rational subdivision of \([1409/5000,293/1000]\), with exact Hurwitz-zeta and logarithm enclosures, proving that \(G\) has exactly one zero \(\xi\) in the bracket and that the certified local maximum value \(Q_2(\xi)\) is the global value \(L_2\). Consequently \(\mathcal I_2=(Q_2(\xi),3]\).
