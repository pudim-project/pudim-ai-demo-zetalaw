# Proposition: Free-energy derivatives

## Statement

For every \(\beta>1\), the zeta free energy satisfies \(A\'(\beta)=-\mathbb E_\beta[\log N]\) and \(A\'\'(\beta)=\operatorname{Var}_\beta(\log N)\).
