# Theorem: Tao-Sendov centroid equality rigidity

## Statement

In the equality case of the centroid half-plane certificate, if there is no critical point in the open disk D(a,1), then every critical point lies on the intersection of the unit circle and the boundary of D(a,1).
\[
S_\theta=(n/2-1)r\text{ and no }w\in D(a,1)\Rightarrow w_j\in\partial\mathbb D\cap\partial D(a,1)\ \forall j.
\]
