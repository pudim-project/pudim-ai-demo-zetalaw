# Obstruction: Finite first-kind Chebyshev-Leja prefixes are not exact dyadic prefixes

## Statement

For n=8, the canonical finite first-kind Chebyshev-Leja order begins 1,8,4,6, not the expected dyadic/bit-reversal prefix 1,8,4,5.
\[
After nodes 1,8,4, candidate 6 has Leja product 0.4903926... while candidate 5 has 0.3604799...
\]
