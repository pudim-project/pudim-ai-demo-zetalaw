# Theorem: Tao-Sendov Pprime Rouche uniform stability degenerates

## Statement

The direct P'-Rouché quantitative tangent-cluster stability bound degenerates through boundary grazing, cluster-factor decay, and degree-growth in the telescoping perturbation estimate; it does not provide parameter-uniform stability.
\[
\varepsilon_\rho\lesssim (1-\rho)^{N-1}(1+r-\rho)^{m-1}/(2+r+\rho)^{d-2}.
\]
