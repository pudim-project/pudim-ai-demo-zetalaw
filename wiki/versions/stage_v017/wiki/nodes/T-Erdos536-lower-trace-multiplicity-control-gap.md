# Theorem: Erdos536 lower trace multiplicity control gap

## Statement

Lower-trace multiplicity control gap: the product deletion mass-transport lemma becomes a vanishing theorem only after controlling the multiplicity with which an actual lower trace \(A\) can arise as \(A=C\setminus D\) from active tops. Bounded or sufficiently slow multiplicity yields mass decay for large deletion weights, while uncontrolled multiplicity is the main escape hatch for lower-trace-poor and spread-design branches.
