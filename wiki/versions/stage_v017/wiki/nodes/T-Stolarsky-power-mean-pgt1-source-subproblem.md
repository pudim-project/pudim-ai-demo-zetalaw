# Theorem: Stolarsky shifted power mean p greater than one source backed Bernstein subproblem

## Statement

In the Chen--Qi/Bessenyei shifted Stolarsky complete-monotonicity problem, decide the negative power-mean subfamily beyond the positive source range: for \(p>1\) and unequal positive shifts \(a\ne b\), whether \(x\mapsto H_p(x+a,x+b)\), \(H_p(u,v)=((u^p+v^p)/2)^{1/p}\), can be a Bernstein function on its natural half-line.
