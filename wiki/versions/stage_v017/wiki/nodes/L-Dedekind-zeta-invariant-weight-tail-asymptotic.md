# Lemma: Dedekind zeta invariant-weight tail asymptotic

## Statement

The Dedekind ideal invariant weight is asymptotic to 1/(kappa_K N a log N a), uniformly on norm tails.
\[
nu_K(a)~1/(kappa_K Na log Na), and inf_{Na>=X} kappa_K Na log Na nu_K(a)->1.
\]
