# Theorem: Erdos536 L2 large coordinate concentration classification

## Statement

L2-large coordinate-concentration classification: in a lower-trace-rich admissible model where \(\mathbf E_C\sum_{i\in C}\lambda_C(i\in D)^2\) is close to \(1\), classify the obstruction as common-core concentration, endpoint-shield concentration, or a genuine sparse-matching lower-trace obstruction using the well-founded core/shield complexity. The classification must produce explicit coordinates/blocks or an actual family-member sparse-matching construction; it must not invoke approximate deletion to intersecting support.
