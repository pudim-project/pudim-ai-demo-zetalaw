# Theorem: Erdos536 matching defect admissibility exclusion

## Statement

For every fixed \(0<\theta<1\) and \(\eta>0\), conditional defect laws \(\lambda_C\) arising from positive-mass lower traces below occupied tops in an admissible non-rank-thin family \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) cannot contain a positive-mass sparse matching-like disjointness component. Quantitatively, if the disjointness graph on defects has a matching component carrying conditional mass bounded below while its disjoint edge density is \(o(1)\), then either the ambient family is rank-thin, or the matched opposite endpoints lift to a positive fork elsewhere.
