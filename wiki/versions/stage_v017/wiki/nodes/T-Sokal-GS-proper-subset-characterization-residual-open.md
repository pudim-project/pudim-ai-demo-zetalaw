# Theorem: Sokal generalized Stieltjes proper subset characterization remains open

## Statement

Find proper subsets of Sokal's generalized-Stieltjes inequalities \(F^{[\lambda]}_{n,k}\ge0\) whose nonnegativity is equivalent to nonnegativity of the whole two-parameter family, beyond the known \(\lambda=1\) subset and possible integer-order variants.
