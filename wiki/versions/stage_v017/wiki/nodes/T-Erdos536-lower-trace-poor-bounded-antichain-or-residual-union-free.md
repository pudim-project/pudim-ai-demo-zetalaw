# Theorem: T Erdos536 lower trace poor bounded antichain or residual union free

## Statement

Lower-trace-poor bounded-antichain or residual-union-free reduction: for high-support non-rank-thin lower-trace-poor admissible families of positive \(\nu_k\)-mass, either prove that the family has a decomposition into \(M_k=o(\sqrt{V_k})\) antichains after discarding rank-thin/top-thin mass, or identify the exact residual union-free/lower-trace condition not captured by antichain or comparable-pair exclusion.
