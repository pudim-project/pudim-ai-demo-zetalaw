# Theorem: Tao-Sendov order-statistic distance certificate

## Statement

Under Sendov failure at a simple zero a, the ordered distances from a to the other zeros obey explicit lower bounds.
\[
d_{(k+1)}>(n/(1+|a|)^k)^{1/(n-1-k)},\quad 0\le k\le n-2.
\]
