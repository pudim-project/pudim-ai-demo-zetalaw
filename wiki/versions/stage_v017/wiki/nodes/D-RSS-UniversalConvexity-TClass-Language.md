# Definition: RSS universal convexity and T-class language

## Statement

In the Ruscheweyh-Salinas-Sugawa framework, \(\mathcal T\) is the class of generating functions of normalized completely monotone sequences, equivalently Hausdorff moment transforms on \([0,1]\). For the source-normalized Ramanujan \(\sigma\)-function, universal convexity is decided by \(D^3\sigma/D^2\sigma\in\mathcal T\), equivalently \(1+z\sigma''(z)/(2\sigma'(z))\in\mathcal T\).
