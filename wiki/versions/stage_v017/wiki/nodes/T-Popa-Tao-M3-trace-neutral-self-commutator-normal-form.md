# Theorem: Popa-Tao M3 trace-neutral self-commutator normal form

## Statement

Every trace-zero Hermitian 3x3 matrix H is a self-commutator H=VV*-V*V, and the optimal squared norm is max(lambda_max(H), -lambda_min(H)).
\[
\inf\{\|V\|^2:H=VV^*-V^*V\}=\max(\lambda_1,-\lambda_3).
\]
