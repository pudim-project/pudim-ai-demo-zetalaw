# Open_Problem: Tao-Sendov tangent-cluster normal-form critical point problem

## Statement

For the tangent-cluster normal form P_{m,N,r}(z)=(z-r)(z+1)^m(z-tau)^N, tau=r/2+i sqrt(1-r^2/4), decide whether at least one free critical point lies in D(r,1), or determine the exact parameter obstruction.
\[
1/(z-r)+m/(z+1)+N/(z-\tau)=0\quad\text{vs.}\quad |z-r|<1.
\]
