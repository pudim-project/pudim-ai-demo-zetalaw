# Theorem: Uniform logarithmic tail of Tao's adjoint upward von Mangoldt chain

## Statement

For Tao's adjoint upward von Mangoldt jump law K_n(q), the tail T_n(Q)=sum_{q>=Q}K_n(q) is comparable to log(n)/log(nQ), uniformly for n>1 and Q>=2.
\[
sum_{q>=Q}K_n(q) asymp log(n)/log(nQ), uniformly for n>1 and Q>=2.
\]
