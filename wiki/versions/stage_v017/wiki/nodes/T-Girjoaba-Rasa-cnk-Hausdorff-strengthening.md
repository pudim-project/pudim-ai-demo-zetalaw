# Theorem: Girjoaba Rasa c(n,k) Hausdorff moment strengthening

## Statement

For every \(n\), the full Girjoaba--Rasa sequence \(k\mapsto c(n,k)\), \(0\le k\le2n\), is a Hausdorff moment sequence, equivalently a completely monotone sequence.
