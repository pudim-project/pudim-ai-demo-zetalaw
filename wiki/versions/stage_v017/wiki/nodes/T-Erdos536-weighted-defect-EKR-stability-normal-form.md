# Theorem: Erdos536 weighted defect EKR stability normal form

## Statement

For a positive-mass set of occupied tops \(C\), zero or asymptotically zero disjoint-defect probability for the conditional defect law \(\lambda_C\) is equivalent, up to \(o(1)\) loss, to \(\lambda_C\)-mass being carried by a weighted intersecting subsystem. More precisely, if \(\lambda_C^{\otimes2}\{D\cap E=\varnothing\}=o(1)\), then after discarding \(o(1)\) conditional mass, the remaining defect family is pairwise intersecting in the prime-biased defect measure on \(C\).
