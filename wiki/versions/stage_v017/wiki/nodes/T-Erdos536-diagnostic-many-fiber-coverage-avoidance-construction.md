# Theorem: Erdos 536 diagnostic local many fiber trace model with positive trace mass and zero pair coverage

## Statement

Diagnostic model-construction problem: build a sequence of local many-fiber trace systems \((C,\mathcal T_C)\) with positive lower-trace mass in separated defect windows and rank-diffuse ambient profiles, but with zero pair-coverage statistic \(\mu_C^{\otimes2}\{(A,B)\in\mathcal T_C^2:A\ne B,\ A\cup B=C\}=0\) for typical \(C\). The construction must not be an exact-rank layer, an \(o(\sqrt{S_k})\) rank block, a single-chain template, or a naive endpoint-pair shield.
