# Theorem: Yang Detemple R best constants complete monotonicity all n open problem

## Statement

For \(R(x)=\psi(x+1/2)-\log x\), determine best constants \(a_k,b_k\) such that \(\left(\sum_{k=0}^{n+1}a_kx^{2k}\right)R(x)-\sum_{k=0}^{n}b_kx^{2k}\) is completely monotone on \((0,\infty)\) and has first nonzero tail order \(x^{-2n-4}\).
