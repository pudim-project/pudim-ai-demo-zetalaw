# Theorem: Bazhlekova residual inner gap fifth seventh ninth derivative diagnostic no cover seed

## Statement

At the residual inner-gap exponent pairs \((a,b)=(3/2,2/5)\) and \((a,b)=(11/10,1/20)\), the derivative polynomials \(Q_5,Q_7,Q_9\) have no positive roots and are positive on \((0,\infty)\); hence the fifth/seventh/ninth odd-derivative diagnostic does not cover the residual Bazhlekova inner gap.
