# Theorem: Erdos 536 biased squarefree pair link supersaturation lifts to high support residual decay

## Statement

For the first \(k\) primes, every fixed-positive-mass high-support family under the biased squarefree law \(\nu_{P_k}(p\in S)=1/p\) contains a squarefree cosunflower pair-link, and this pair-link supersaturation lifts to the exponent-grid high-support residual needed for \(R_{P_k}(\theta)\to0\).
