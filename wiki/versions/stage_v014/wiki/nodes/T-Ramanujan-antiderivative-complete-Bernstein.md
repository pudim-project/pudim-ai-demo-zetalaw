# Theorem: Ramanujan integral antiderivative is complete Bernstein

## Statement

The antiderivative \(\widetilde I_R(x)=a+\int_0^\infty(1-e^{-xt})\,dt/[t(\pi^2+\log^2 t)]\) of the Ramanujan integral is a complete Bernstein function.
