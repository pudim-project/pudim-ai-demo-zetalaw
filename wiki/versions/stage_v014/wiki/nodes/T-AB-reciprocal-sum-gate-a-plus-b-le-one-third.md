# Theorem: Alzer Berg reciprocal Gini Gamma quotient complete monotonicity forces a plus b at most one third

## Statement

If \(x\mapsto1/P_{a,b}(u,v;x)\) is completely monotone on \((0,\infty)\) for every \(v>u>0\), then \(a+b\le1/3\).
