# Theorem: Erdos536 lower trace abundance or few comparables countertemplate

## Statement

Lower-trace abundance/countertemplate dichotomy: for fixed \(0<\theta<1\) and \(\eta>0\), every positive-mass non-rank-thin sequence \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) either has a positive-mass law of occupied tops \(\tau_k\) for which the proper lower-trace systems \(\mathcal L_{\mathcal F_k}(C)\) are nonempty and carry non-negligible spread defect laws \(\lambda_C\), or else admits an explicit positive-mass countertemplate with few comparable pairs. The countertemplate must preserve high support and non-rank-thinness and explain why the lower-trace route cannot start.
