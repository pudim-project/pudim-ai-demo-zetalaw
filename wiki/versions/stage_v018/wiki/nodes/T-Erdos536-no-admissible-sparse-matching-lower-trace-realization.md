# Theorem: Erdos536 no admissible sparse matching lower trace realization

## Statement

Sparse matching realizability theorem/diagnostic: a matching-like disjoint-defect graph with vanishing two-sample disjointness probability cannot be realized by actual lower traces \(A=C\setminus D\in\mathcal F_k\) below positive-mass occupied tops in a non-rank-thin high-support family, except through rank-thinness or one of the core/shield descent branches. Equivalently, abstract matching counterexamples to weighted EKR stability are not admissible Erdos536 lower-trace geometries unless they collapse to a named obstruction already handled elsewhere.
