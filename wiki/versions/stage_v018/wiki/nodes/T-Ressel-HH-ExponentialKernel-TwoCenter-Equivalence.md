# Theorem: Ressel exponential Hornich-Hlawka kernel has a two-center form

## Statement

For Hilbert vectors x,y,z, the exponential-kernel Hornich-Hlawka defect is nonnegative if and only if, after w=x+y+z, m=(x+y)/2, h=(x-y)/2, and u=w-m, one has H_h(u)+H_m(m)>=H_m(u)+H_h(m), where H_a(v)=exp(-|v-a|)+exp(-|v+a|).
