# Theorem: T Erdos536 conditional lower trace poor vanishing combined sinks

## Statement

Conditional lower-trace-poor vanishing by combined sinks: prove that any lower-trace-poor branch satisfying both \(M_k=o(\sqrt{V_k})\) antichain-decomposition control and \(M_k/L_k\to0\) multiplicity-controlled deletion decay has \(\nu_k\)-mass tending to zero, with explicit hypotheses separating antichain-cover mass decay from deletion-weight/multiplicity decay. Record the theorem as conditional unless the source-reduction and multiplicity-exclusion candidates supply the hypotheses.
