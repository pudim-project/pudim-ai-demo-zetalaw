# Theorem: Erdos 536 weighted star fiber mass either synchronizes into rank thin blocks or forces occupied coverage

## Statement

Weighted star-fiber mass theorem: let \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) be a positive-mass coherent moving-center system with nonsparse close central ranks \(r<s\le2r\). Suppose the relevant lower \(r\)-traces below occupied tops are threshold-sharp EKR star fibers \(\mathcal A_{r,q(C)}(C)=\{A\subseteq C\setminus\{q(C)\}: |A|=r\}\), up to negligible error. Then the total \(\nu_k\)-mass carried by such moving star fibers either concentrates into \(o(\sqrt{S_k})\) synchronized center/rank blocks, hence vanishes by rank-block anti-concentration, or supplies two nonnegligible incompatible center profiles satisfying `T-Erdos536-occupied-union-coverage-from-close-rank-density`.
