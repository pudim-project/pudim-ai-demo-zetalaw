# Lemma: Endpoint side is invariant under open-range polynomial shifts

## Statement

Within the open range where Berg--Szwarc Proposition 1.5 applies, multiplying a special \(N\)-extremal measure by \((1+x^2)^\eta\) shifts \(a(\mu)\) by \(\eta\) but leaves the endpoint measure \((1+x^2)^{-a(\mu)}d\mu\) unchanged. Therefore open-range shifts preserve whether the endpoint side is \(A\) or \(B\).
