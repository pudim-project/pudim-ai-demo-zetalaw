# Theorem: Erdos 536 diagnostic search for positive mass rank diffuse zero fork family

## Statement

Diagnostic construction problem: attempt to construct a sequence \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) with \(\liminf_k\nu_k(\mathcal F_k)>0\), not rank-thin under `T-Erdos536-rank-block-anti-concentration`, and zero fork energy \(\Phi_k(\mathcal F_k)=0\). The construction should be rank-diffuse, should avoid all lower-shadow forks, and should not be a negligible exact-rank-layer example.
