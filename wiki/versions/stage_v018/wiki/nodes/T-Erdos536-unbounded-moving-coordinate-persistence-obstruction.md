# Theorem: Erdos536 unbounded moving coordinate persistence obstruction

## Statement

Unbounded moving-coordinate persistence obstruction: local coordinate concentration may move through an unbounded coordinate window so that no fixed coordinate persists on positive top mass. Core/shield descent remains open unless this moving-coordinate escape is excluded, shown rank-thin or vanishing, or realized as an explicit obstruction construction.
