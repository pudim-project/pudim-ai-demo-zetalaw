# Theorem: Tao-Sendov fixed-parameter root-adaptive logderivative stability

## Statement

At the root-adaptive contour, an explicit inequality involving the perturbation size e, gamma=(1-s)/2, and m,N preserves the free critical zero count and yields a critical point in D(r,1).
\[
E_{\rho_*,e}<d\gamma^2/((1+r+\rho_*)(1+\rho_*))\Rightarrow \exists\zeta:P'(\zeta)=0,\ |\zeta-r|<\rho_*<1.
\]
