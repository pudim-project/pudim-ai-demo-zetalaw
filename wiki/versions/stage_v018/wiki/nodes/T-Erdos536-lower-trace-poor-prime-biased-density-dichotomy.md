# Theorem: T Erdos536 lower trace poor prime biased density dichotomy

## Statement

Lower-trace-poor prime-biased density dichotomy: under the product law \(\nu_k(p_i\in S)=1/p_i\), every admissible high-support non-rank-thin lower-trace-poor family either has \(\nu_k\)-mass tending to zero by a weighted comparable-pair, shadow, chain, entropy, or LYM estimate, or admits an explicit positive-mass non-rank-thin lower-trace-poor construction. This branch forbids defect laws, square sums, and lower-trace-rich assumptions.
