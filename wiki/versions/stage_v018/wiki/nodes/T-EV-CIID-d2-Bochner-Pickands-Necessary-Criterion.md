# Theorem: Bochner necessary criterion for bivariate conditionally iid Pickands functions

## Statement

Let \(A\) be a symmetric bivariate Pickands function. Put \(B(t)=1-A(t)\), \(t=e^v/(1+e^v)\), and \(R_A(v)=2\cosh(v/2)B(e^v/(1+e^v))\). If \(A\in\mathcal A^*\), then \(R_A\) is positive definite on \(\mathbb R\). Equivalently, for the associated symmetric simplex-coordinate measure \(\nu_A\) pushed to \(a=\log((1-q)/q)\), the transform \(\int_{[0,\infty)}\cos(\xi a)\operatorname{sech}(a/2)\,\nu_A(da)\) is nonnegative for every real \(\xi\).
