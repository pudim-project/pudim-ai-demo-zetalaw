# Definition: Generalized Gaussian source normalization

## Statement

For the Dytso--Bustin--Poor--Shamai source, \(X_p\sim N_p(0,1)\) denotes the centered unit-scale generalized Gaussian random variable with density proportional to \(\exp(-|x|^p/2)\). Its absolute Mellin transform is \(\mathbb E|X_p|^s=2^{s/p}\Gamma((s+1)/p)/\Gamma(1/p)\) for \(\Re s>-1\).
