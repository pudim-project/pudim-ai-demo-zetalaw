# Theorem_Import: Poly-double-gamma derivative Laplace representation

## Statement

For \(y>0\) and \(r\ge2\), Mishra--Swaminathan's source representation gives \(\psi_2^{(r)}(y)=(-1)^{r+1}\int_0^\infty e^{-yt}t^r/(1-e^{-t})^2\,dt\).
