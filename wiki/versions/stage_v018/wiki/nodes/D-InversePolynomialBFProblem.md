# Definition: Inverse-polynomial Bernstein coefficient problem

## Statement

For \(n\ge2\) and coefficients \(a_j\ge0\), the inverse-polynomial Bernstein coefficient problem asks for conditions under which the positive inverse \(\varphi(\lambda)\) of \(\lambda=\varphi^n+a_1\varphi^{n-1}+\cdots+a_{n-1}\varphi\) is a Bernstein function.
