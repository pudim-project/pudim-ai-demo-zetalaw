# Theorem: T Erdos536 weighted LYM normalized matching resolution

## Statement

Weighted LYM/normalized-matching resolution for the prime-biased product law: prove or refute that for \(\nu_k=\bigotimes_{i\le k}\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/p_i)\), every antichain \(\mathcal A_k\subseteq2^{[k]}\) satisfies \(\nu_k(\mathcal A_k)\le C\sup_m\nu_k(|S|=m)\), hence \(\nu_k(\mathcal A_k)=o(1)\) by rank-level anti-concentration; or construct an explicit antichain of positive \(\nu_k\)-mass showing weighted LYM fails in this inhomogeneous setting.
