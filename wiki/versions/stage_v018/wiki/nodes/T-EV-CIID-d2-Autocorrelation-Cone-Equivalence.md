# Theorem: Autocorrelation cone reformulation of the bivariate conditionally iid class

## Statement

With \(R_A(v)=2\cosh(v/2)(1-A(e^v/(1+e^v)))\), a symmetric bivariate Pickands function \(A\) belongs to \(\mathcal A^*\) if and only if \(R_A\) lies in the closed convex hull of autocorrelations \(v\mapsto\int_{\mathbb R}k_F(u)k_F(u-v)\,du\), where \(F\) ranges over probability laws on \([0,\infty)\) with mean \(1\) and \(k_F(u)=e^{u/2}\overline F(e^u)\).
