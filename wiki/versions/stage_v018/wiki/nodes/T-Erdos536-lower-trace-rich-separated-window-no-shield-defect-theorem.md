# Theorem: Erdos536 lower trace rich separated window no shield defect theorem

## Statement

Lower-trace-rich conditional defect theorem: on a lower-trace-rich admissible sublaw, assume two separated defect size/weight windows and explicit no-core/no-shield hypotheses expressed by the well-founded core/shield complexity. Then either \(\mathbf E_{C}\Omega_k(C)>0\), giving a support-level fork by the true conditional coverage reduction, or there is an actual sparse-matching lower-trace construction satisfying all admissibility hypotheses. Sparse-matching exclusion may be attempted only after the separated-window and no-core/no-shield hypotheses are fixed.
