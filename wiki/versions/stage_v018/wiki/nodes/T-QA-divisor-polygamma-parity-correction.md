# Theorem: T-QA-divisor-polygamma-parity-correction

## Statement

The Qi--Agarwal/Yin divisor-polygamma parity problem is resolved as follows: every odd divisor-polygamma sum is completely monotone, while the claimed universal even non-complete-monotonicity is false because \(f_2\) is completely monotone.

## Proof

The node the QA divisor polygamma odd CM proves that every odd divisor-polygamma sum \(f_n\) is completely monotone. The node the QA divisor polygamma even claim refuted, itself following from the QA divisor polygamma f2 CM, proves that the source's universal even non-complete-monotonicity clause is false. These two admitted statements exactly resolve the parity claim: the odd assertion holds, while the even negation asserted in the source does not hold in full generality.
