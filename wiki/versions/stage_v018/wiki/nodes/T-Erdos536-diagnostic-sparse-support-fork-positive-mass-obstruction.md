# Theorem: Erdos536 diagnostic sparse support fork positive mass obstruction

## Statement

Diagnostic sparse-fork obstruction problem: construct, or rule out within natural templates, a positive-mass non-rank-thin sequence \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,	heta}\) in which support-level lower-trace forks exist only on a vanishing conditional-mass set of pairs below occupied tops, so \(\mathbf E\Omega_k(C)\to0\), while no support-level theorem immediately forces the weighted union-free frontier. The construction must respect actual family membership of lower traces, not merely abstract defect atoms.
