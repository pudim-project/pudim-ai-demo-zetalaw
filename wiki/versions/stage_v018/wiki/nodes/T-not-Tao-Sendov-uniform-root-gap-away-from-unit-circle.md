# Theorem: No uniform Tao-Sendov tangent-cluster root gap away from the unit circle

## Statement

There is no universal positive delta such that the largest free-critical root modulus below one is at most 1-delta for all tangent-cluster parameters.
\[
\not\exists\delta>0\ \forall r,m,N:\ s\le1-\delta.
\]
