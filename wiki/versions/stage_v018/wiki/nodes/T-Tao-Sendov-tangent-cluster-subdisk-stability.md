# Theorem: Tao-Sendov tangent-cluster subdisk stability

## Statement

For fixed parameters, if rho<1 encloses at least one exact-model critical point and no critical point lies on |z-r|=rho, then sufficiently small cluster perturbations preserve the critical-point count in D(r,rho).
\[
P^{0\prime}\neq0\text{ on }|z-r|=\rho\Rightarrow \#Z(P',D(r,\rho))=\#Z(P^{0\prime},D(r,\rho)).
\]
