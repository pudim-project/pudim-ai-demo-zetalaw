# Theorem: Townes finite-interval BLT root-preservation restatement

## Statement

Let \(X\) be a real-valued infinitely divisible probability law with bilateral Laplace transform \(L(s)=\mathbb E e^{-sX}\) finite near \([0,1]\). Suppose \(L\) is completely monotone on \([0,1]\), so \(G(z)=L(1-z)\) is a probability generating function. Is \(L^{1/n}\) completely monotone on \([0,1]\) for every integer \(n\ge2\)?
