# Theorem: Erdos536 bounded mean persistent coordinate descent

## Statement

Bounded-mean persistent-coordinate descent theorem: in a lower-trace-rich branch where expected defect size is bounded or a small defect occurs on a positive-mass top set, use the true coordinate-marginal lemmas to extract coordinates with marginals bounded below, then prove a persistence theorem turning them into a fixed core, moving core, or endpoint shield with strict lexicographic decrease of \(\mathcal C=(\ell_{\mathrm{end}},w_{\mathrm{core}},m_{\mathrm{avail}},r_{\mathrm{width}})\), or isolate a non-persistent coordinate obstruction.
