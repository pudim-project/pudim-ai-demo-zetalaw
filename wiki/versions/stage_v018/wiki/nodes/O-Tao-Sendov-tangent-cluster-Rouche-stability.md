# Open_Problem: Tao-Sendov tangent-cluster Rouche stability frontier

## Statement

Prove that the tangent-cluster free critical point inside D(r,1) persists when the clusters at -1 and tau are perturbed by a sufficiently small amount, first for fixed parameters and then uniformly if possible.
\[
P_{m,N,r}\text{ cluster perturbation}\Rightarrow\exists\zeta:P'(\zeta)=0,\ |\zeta-r|<1.
\]
