# Definition: Kummer M local series

## Statement

For \(c\notin\{0,-1,-2,\ldots\}\), the Kummer confluent hypergeometric function has the local expansion \({\rm M}(a,c,z)={}_1F_1(a;c;z)=1+(a/c)z+a(a+1)z^2/[2c(c+1)]+O(z^3)\).
