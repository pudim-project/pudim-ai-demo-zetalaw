# Theorem: Tao-Sendov centroid half-plane certificate

## Statement

Let a=re^{i theta} be a simple zero of a degree n polynomial with all zeros in the closed unit disk. If the directional sum of the other zeros is at least (n/2-1)r, then Sendov's conclusion holds at a; strict inequality gives an interior critical point.
\[
\Re(e^{-i\theta}\sum_{i=2}^n z_i)\ge (n/2-1)r\Rightarrow \exists\zeta:P'(\zeta)=0,\ |\zeta-a|\le1.
\]
