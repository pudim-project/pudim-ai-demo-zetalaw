# Theorem: Erdos536 lower trace poor comparable pair sink or construction

## Statement

Lower-trace-poor comparable-pair sink or construction theorem: for the lower-trace-poor branch of the two-gate schema, prove a weighted comparable-pair, chain, entropy, or LYM sink showing admissible high-support non-rank-thin families have \(\nu_k\)-mass tending to zero; otherwise construct an explicit positive-mass non-rank-thin lower-trace-poor family under the prime-biased product law. This branch remains independent of defect laws and square-sum quantities.
