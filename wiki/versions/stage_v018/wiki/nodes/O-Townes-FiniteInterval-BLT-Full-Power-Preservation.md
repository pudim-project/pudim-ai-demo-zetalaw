# Theorem: Townes finite-interval BLT full-power preservation

## Statement

Under the Townes hypotheses, if \(L\) is the bilateral Laplace transform of a real-valued infinitely divisible law and \(L\) is completely monotone on \([0,1]\), then \(L^t\) is completely monotone on \([0,1]\) for every \(t>0\).
