# Definition: q-monotone finite-difference convention

## Statement

In the Abel--Leviatan--Rasa source convention, a continuous function on \([0,1]\) is \(q\)-monotone when its \(q\)-th finite differences are nonnegative on the admitted intervals; in particular, \(2\)-monotone is equivalent to convexity on \([0,1]\).
