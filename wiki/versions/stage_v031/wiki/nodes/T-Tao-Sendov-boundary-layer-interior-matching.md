# Theorem: T-Tao-Sendov-boundary-layer-interior-matching

## Statement

In both boundary charts q=r+lambda/d and q=1-lambda/d, for fixed 0<r<1 with lambda -> infinity and lambda=o(d), the tangent-cluster large-root defect satisfies 1-|y_L| ~ 1-|C(r,q)| ~ kappa_r lambda/d.

## Scope

- Asymptotic matching between boundary-layer and interior tangent-cluster estimates.
