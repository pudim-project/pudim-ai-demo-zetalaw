# Theorem: Erdos 536 diagnostic sparse predecessor center positive mass construction route

## Statement

Diagnostic sparse predecessor-center construction route: attempt to construct a positive-mass fork-free coherent moving-star family whose occupied tops lie in a central linear window, whose predecessor closures avoid the full-window fork lemma and the partial predecessor-window fork hypothesis by sparse center/rank selection, and whose rank support is not thin enough for the sparse mass-collapse theorem. If no construction works, record whether the failure is caused by close-rank forks, rank-block anti-concentration, center-profile synchronization, or loss of local predecessor closure.
