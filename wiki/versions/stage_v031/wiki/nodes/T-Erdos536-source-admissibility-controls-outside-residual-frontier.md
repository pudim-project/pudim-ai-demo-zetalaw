# Theorem: T Erdos536 source admissibility controls outside residual frontier

## Statement

Source-admissibility control outside the residual frontier: prove that every high-support non-rank-thin lower-trace-poor admissible branch that is not a residual moving-base high-multiplicity configuration supplies either an \(o(\sqrt{V_k})\)-antichain cover after negligible discards or subcritical lower-trace multiplicity relative to deletion weights; otherwise identify the exact missing admissibility-to-control implication.
