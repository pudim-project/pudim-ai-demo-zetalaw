# Negative_Result: T-not-Popa-Tao-Mn-trace-neutral-selfcommutator-spectral-radius

## Statement

The formula inf{||V||^2 : H=VV^*-V^*V}=max(lambda_max(H),-lambda_min(H)) for trace-zero Hermitian H is false in general finite dimension.

## Scope

- Finite trace-neutral self-commutator model; not a solution of Tao's infinite B(H) model.
