# Theorem: Erdos 536 cross top star center collisions force occupied union fork

## Statement

Cross-top star-center collision theorem: in the prime-biased moving-center setup, assume there are two nonnegligible families of occupied tops \(C_1,C_2\) in close central ranks whose threshold-sharp star centers \(q(C_1)\) and \(q(C_2)\) are asymptotically incompatible. If both families carry nonnegligible lower-trace star-fiber mass, then there exist distinct \(A,B,C\in\mathcal F_k\) with \(A\subsetneq C_1\), \(B\subsetneq C_2\), and \(A\cup B=C\). In particular incompatible moving star centers force the occupied-union fork required by `T-Erdos536-occupied-union-coverage-from-close-rank-density`.
