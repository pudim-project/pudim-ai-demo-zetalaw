# Theorem: Wu Yu Guo Tsallis heat-flow complete monotonicity conjecture for all alpha in (1,2)

## Statement

For every one-dimensional heat-flow density \(p(x,t)=T_t f(x)\) in the source convention and every \(\alpha\in(1,2)\), the Tsallis entropy \(\hat h_\alpha(p(\cdot,t))\) is completely monotone in the sense that \((-1)^{k-1}\partial_t^k\hat h_\alpha(p(\cdot,t))\ge0\) for all \(k\ge1\) and \(t>0\).
