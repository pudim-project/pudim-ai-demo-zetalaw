# Theorem: Qi h_lambda degree four conjecture for h_lambda lambda<=0 and -h_mu mu>=4

## Statement

For \(\Psi(x)=[\psi'(x)]^2+\psi''(x)\) and \(h_\lambda(x)=\Psi(x)-(x^2+\lambda x+12)/(12x^4(x+1)^2)\), Qi's Remark 7.4 conjectures that \(\deg_x^{\rm cm}h_\lambda=\deg_x^{\rm cm}(-h_\mu)=4\) if and only if \(\lambda\le0\) and \(\mu\ge4\).
