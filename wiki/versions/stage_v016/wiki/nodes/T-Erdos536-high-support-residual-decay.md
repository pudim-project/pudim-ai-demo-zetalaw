# Theorem: Erdos 536 high support prefix rank residual decays along growing prime sets

## Statement

There are finite prime sets \(P_j\) with \(S_{P_j}=\sum_{p\in P_j}1/p\to\infty\) and a fixed \(0\le\theta<1\) such that the high-support prefix-rank residual \(R_{P_j}(\theta)=\delta_{P_j}\int_1^\infty (g_{P_j}(t)-L_{P_j,\theta}(t))_+t^{-2}\,dt\) tends to \(0\).
