# Theorem: Erdos 536 occupied union coverage follows from close rank lower trace density and incompatible moving centers

## Statement

For the prime-biased setup \(P_k=\{p_1,\ldots,p_k\}\), \(S_k=\sum_{i\le k}1/p_i\), \(\nu_k(p_i\in S)=1/p_i\), let \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) be a coherent intersecting-shadow moving-center system with nonsparse close-rank lower-trace density on central ranks \(r<s\le2r\). If two center profiles are asymptotically incompatible and each has nonnegligible lower-trace mass below occupied tops, then there exist lower traces \(A,B\in\mathcal F_k\) and an occupied top \(C\in\mathcal F_k\), all distinct, with \(A\cup B=C\). Equivalently, close-rank density plus incompatible moving centers forces occupied union coverage.
