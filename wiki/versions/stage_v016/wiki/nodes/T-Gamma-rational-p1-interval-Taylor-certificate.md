# Theorem: interval Taylor certificate brackets Gamma rational p1 maximizer and certifies derivative signs

## Statement

A certified log-Gamma Taylor and interval-arithmetic proof brackets the unique maximizer of \(H(x)=x(\Gamma(1+x)-x)/(1-\Gamma(1+x))\) in \([0.19277765,0.19277767]\), proves the required derivative signs outside that bracket, and certifies the corresponding optimal upper parameter \(p_1^*\).
