# Theorem: Chiu Yin Sparre Andersen ladder height complete monotonicity converse remains open

## Statement

Chiu--Yin Remark 3.5 conjectures that in the Sparre Andersen model, if the ascending ladder-height distribution \(F^+\) has a completely monotone density, then the claim-size distribution \(H\) has a completely monotone density; consequently the converses of their ruin-probability, ruin-time, and deficit-at-ruin complete-monotonicity theorems should hold.
