# Theorem: Erdos 536 fixed center coherent intersecting shadow systems collapse to rank thin mass

## Statement

For every fixed \(0<\theta<1\), every fork-free family \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) whose occupied-top defect shadows are coherent and center-stable is rank-thin up to \(o(1)\) mass. Here center-stable means that, after discarding \(o(1)\) mass, the occupied tops can be partitioned into \(o(\sqrt{S_k})\) blocks, each with a coordinate \(q\) such that every nonempty defect in every top shadow of that block contains \(q\). Consequently such fixed-center coherent systems have \(\nu_k(\mathcal F_k)\to0\).
