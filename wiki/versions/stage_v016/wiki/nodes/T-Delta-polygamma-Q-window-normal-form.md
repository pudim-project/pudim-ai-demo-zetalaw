# Theorem: Q_Delta window 1<Q<6/5 with endpoint limits proves Qi Agarwal sharp exponent theorem

## Statement

Let \(D(x)=x^4((\psi'(x))^2+\psi''(x))\), \(A(x)=(x^2+4x+12)/(12(x+1)^2)\), and \(Q_\Delta(x)=\log D(x)/\log A(x)\). Then \(1<Q_\Delta(x)<6/5\) on \((0,\infty)\), with endpoint limits \(6/5\) at \(0^+\) and \(1\) at \(\infty\); this proves the Qi--Agarwal sharp exponent window.
