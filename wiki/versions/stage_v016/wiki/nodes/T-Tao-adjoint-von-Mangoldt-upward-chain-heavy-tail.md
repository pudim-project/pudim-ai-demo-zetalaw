# Theorem: Heavy tail of Tao's adjoint upward von Mangoldt chain

## Statement

For n > 1, the adjoint upward von Mangoldt jump law K_n(q) is normalized, is comparable to log(n)Lambda(q)/(q log(nq)^2), has infinite expected log-multiplier, and has fixed-n tail asymptotic 1/(n nu_Lambda(n) log(nQ)).
\[
K_n(q)=nu_Lambda(nq)Lambda(q)/(nu_Lambda(n)log(nq)); sum_q K_n(q)=1; K_n(q) asymp log(n)Lambda(q)/(qlog(nq)^2); sum_q K_n(q)log(q)=infinity; sum_{q>=Q}K_n(q)~1/(n nu_Lambda(n)log(nQ)) for fixed n.
\]
