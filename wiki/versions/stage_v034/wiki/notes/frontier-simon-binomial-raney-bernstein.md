# Frontier Note: Simon Binomial/Raney Bernstein Moment Sequences

Source: Thomas Simon, *Moment problems related to Bernstein functions*, Annales de la Faculté des sciences de Toulouse 29(3), 577--594, 2020.

Remark 3.4 records the open problem of characterizing the Bernstein property of binomial and Raney moment sequences. The local bridge verifies one source-contained binomial slice:
\[
\mu_n=\binom{2n+1}{n}.
\]
The ratio is
\[
\frac{\mu_n}{\mu_{n-1}}=4-\frac2{n+1},
\]
and the interpolating function
\[
\Phi(x)=4-\frac2{x+1}=2+2\frac{x}{x+1}
\]
is complete Bernstein because \(x/(x+1)=\int_0^\infty(1-e^{-xt})e^{-t}\,dt\).

Status: bridge only. The full binomial/Raney characterization remains open.
