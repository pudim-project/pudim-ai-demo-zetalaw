Oracle forage `ORACLE-FI-20260529T-next-loop-025-force` selected Berg--Mateu--Porcu's Dagum auxiliary family
\[
f_{\alpha,\beta}(x)=x^{-\alpha}(1+x^\beta)^{-1}.
\]

The bounded admitted theorem proves:
\[
f_{\alpha,\beta}\in CM
\quad(\alpha\ge0,\ 0\le\beta\le1),
\]
and
\[
f_{\alpha,\beta}\in CM
\quad(1<\beta\le2,\ \alpha\ge\beta/2).
\]
At \(\beta=2\), the endpoint is exact:
\[
f_{\alpha,2}\in CM\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \alpha\ge1.
\]

The source threshold is
\[
c(\beta)=\inf\{\alpha\ge0:f_{\alpha,\beta}\in CM\}.
\]
The local theorem gives \(c(1)=0\), \(c(2)=1\), and \(c(\beta)\le\beta/2\) for \(1<\beta\le2\). The source conjecture that \(c\) is continuous remains open.
