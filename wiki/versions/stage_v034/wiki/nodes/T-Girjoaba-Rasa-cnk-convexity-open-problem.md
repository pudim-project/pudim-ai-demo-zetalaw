# Theorem: Girjoaba Rasa c(n,k) convexity open problem

## Statement

For \(c(n,k)=\binom{2n}{k}^{-2}\sum_{j=0}^k\binom{n}{j}^2\binom{n}{k-j}^2\), prove that \(k\mapsto c(n,k)\) is convex for every \(n\).
