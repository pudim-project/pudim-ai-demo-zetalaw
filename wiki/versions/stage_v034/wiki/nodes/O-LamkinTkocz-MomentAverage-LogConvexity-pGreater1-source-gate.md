# Open_Problem: Lamkin-Tkocz moment-average log-convexity conjecture for p greater than 1 source gate

## Statement

Lamkin and Tkocz ask whether, for every real \(p>1\) and every i.i.d. sequence of nonnegative random variables \((X_i)\), the moment-average sequence \(b_n=\mathbb E[((X_1+\cdots+X_n)/n)^p]\) is log-convex in \(n\).
