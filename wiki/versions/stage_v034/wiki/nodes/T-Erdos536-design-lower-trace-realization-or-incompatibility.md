# Theorem: Erdos536 design lower trace realization or incompatibility

## Statement

Spread-design lower-trace realization test: under the prime-biased high-support admissibility schema, either realize projective-plane-like pairwise-intersecting defect systems as actual lower traces below a positive-mass non-rank-thin family, or prove incompatibility by showing that such incidence designs force rank-thinness, vanish in \(\nu_k\)-mass, violate actual lower-trace membership, or descend to a smaller admissible model. The statement treats spread designs as their own branch, not as sparse matching or core/shield concentration.
