# Definition: Antimonotone positive pair

## Statement

A positive pair \((A,B)\) is antimonotone, in Bourin's source sense, if \(A=f(C)\) and \(B=g(C)\) for one Hermitian \(C\), with one of \(f,g\) nondecreasing and the other nonincreasing; equivalently, in a common eigenbasis the eigenvalues can be ordered oppositely.
