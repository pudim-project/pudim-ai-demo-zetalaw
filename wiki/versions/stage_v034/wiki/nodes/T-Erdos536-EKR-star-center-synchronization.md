# Theorem: Erdos 536 EKR star centers synchronize or yield incompatible dense profiles for occupied coverage

## Statement

Suppose \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) is a positive-mass fork-free coherent moving-center system with nonsparse close-rank lower-trace density, and suppose every relevant occupied top is below the local EKR trace threshold. Then, after discarding \(o(1)\) \(\nu_k\)-mass, the near-extremal intersecting defect-complement traces have synchronized EKR-star centers on \(o(\sqrt{S_k})\) center/rank blocks, or else two unsynchronized center profiles satisfy the occupied-union coverage hypotheses of `T-Erdos536-occupied-union-coverage-from-close-rank-density`.
