# Definition: Modular residue distribution and successor entropy

## Statement

For \(q\ge2\), define the residue distribution of the zeta law modulo \(q\) by
\[
\mu_{q,\beta}(a)=\sum_{\substack{n\ge1\\ n\equiv a\pmod q}}\rho_\beta(n),
\qquad a\in\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z.
\]
Writing \(a+1\) cyclically in \(\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z\), define
\[
B_q(\beta)=
\sum_{a\in\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z}
\mu_{q,\beta}(a)\log\frac{\mu_{q,\beta}(a)}{\mu_{q,\beta}(a+1)}.
\]

## Proof

This is a definition. The quantities are finite because \(\mu_{q,\beta}\) is a probability distribution on the finite group \(\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z\).
