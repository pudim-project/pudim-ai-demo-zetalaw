# Problem: Alzer-Kwong convexity and concavity problem

## Statement

Alzer and Kwong conjectured the reciprocal zeta function \(F(x)=1/\zeta(x)\) has the sign pattern
\[
F''(x)>0\quad\text{on }(-4n,-4n+2),
\]
and
\[
F''(x)<0\quad\text{on }(-4n-2,-4n),
\]
for every integer \(n\ge1\).

## Evidence

The PDF imports this as Conjecture 1.12 from Alzer and Kwong and gives a proof using the zeta functional equation plus a positive-axis curvature bound.
