# Theorem: Alzer-Kwong convexity and concavity pattern for reciprocal zeta

## Statement

Let \(F(x)=1/\zeta(x)\). For every integer \(n\ge1\),
\[
F''(x)>0\quad\text{on }(-4n,-4n+2),
\]
and
\[
F''(x)<0\quad\text{on }(-4n-2,-4n).
\]

## Proof

Put \(x=-u\), where \(u>2\). The functional equation gives
\[
\zeta(-u)
=-2^{-u}\pi^{-u-1}\sin\left(\frac{\pi u}{2}\right)\Gamma(u+1)\zeta(u+1),
\]
and hence
\[
\frac1{\zeta(-u)}
=-\frac{2^u\pi^{u+1}}
{\Gamma(u+1)\zeta(u+1)\sin(\pi u/2)}.
\]
Define
\[
G(u)=
\frac{2^u\pi^{u+1}}
{\Gamma(u+1)\zeta(u+1)|\sin(\pi u/2)|}.
\]
On \(u\in(4n-2,4n)\), \(\sin(\pi u/2)<0\), so \(F(-u)=G(u)\). On \(u\in(4n,4n+2)\), \(\sin(\pi u/2)>0\), so \(F(-u)=-G(u)\). Since \(x=-u\), second derivatives are unchanged by this change of variable. It is enough to prove \(G''(u)>0\) for \(u>2\).

Let \(\ell(u)=\log G(u)\). Then
\[
\ell(u)
=u\log2+(u+1)\log\pi-\log\Gamma(u+1)-\log\zeta(u+1)
-\log\left|\sin\left(\frac{\pi u}{2}\right)\right|.
\]
Differentiating twice gives
\[
\ell''(u)
=-\psi'(u+1)-(\log\zeta)''(u+1)
+\frac{\pi^2}{4}\csc^2\left(\frac{\pi u}{2}\right),
\]
where \(\psi'\) is the trigamma function. Put \(s=u+1>3\). Then
\[
\psi'(s)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac1{(s+k)^2}
<
\int_{s-1}^{\infty}\frac{dt}{t^2}
=\frac1{s-1}\le\frac12.
\]
By the positive-axis curvature bound,
\[
(\log\zeta)''(s)<\frac13,
\]
and also \(\csc^2(\pi u/2)\ge1\). Therefore
\[
\ell''(u)>
\frac{\pi^2}{4}-\frac12-\frac13
=\frac{\pi^2}{4}-\frac56>0.
\]
Since \(G>0\),
\[
G''(u)=G(u)(\ell''(u)+\ell'(u)^2)>0.
\]

Returning to \(x=-u\), if \(x\in(-4n,-4n+2)\), then \(u\in(4n-2,4n)\) and \(F(x)=G(u)\), so \(F''(x)=G''(u)>0\). If \(x\in(-4n-2,-4n)\), then \(u\in(4n,4n+2)\) and \(F(x)=-G(u)\), so \(F''(x)=-G''(u)<0\). This proves the asserted convexity and concavity pattern.
