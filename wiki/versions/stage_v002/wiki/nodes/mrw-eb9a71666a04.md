# Problem: Nantomah zeta positivity problem

## Statement

Determine whether, for every \(n\in\mathbb N\),
\[
(n+2)\zeta(n+1)\zeta(n+3)
-(n+1)\zeta(n+2)^2
-\zeta(n+1)\zeta(n+2)>0.
\]

## Evidence

The PDF attributes this question to Kwara Nantomah and gives an affirmative solution.
