# Frontier Note: Arsinh-Square Thorin-Bernstein Log-Derivative

Source: Jedidi and Vakeroudis, *Windings of planar processes, Exponential Functionals and Asian options*, arXiv:1610.07030.

First-contact records the exact source target: for
\[
\varphi(x)=\operatorname{arsinh}^2\sqrt{x},
\]
the paper says the Stieltjes status of \(\varphi'(x)/\varphi(x)\) is not achieved there. If it were Stieltjes, a Thorin-Bernstein composition conclusion for \(\log(1+t\varphi(x))\) would follow.

The Student pass gives a negative answer to the Stieltjes target. With
\[
h(x)=\frac{\varphi'(x)}{\varphi(x)}
=\frac{1}{\sqrt{x}\sqrt{1+x}\operatorname{arsinh}\sqrt{x}},
\]
the upper boundary value at \(-t+i0\), \(t>1\), has strictly positive imaginary part. Stieltjes transforms of positive measures have nonpositive imaginary part in the upper half-plane, so \(h\) is not Stieltjes.

The same \(h\) is nevertheless completely monotone. This separates CM from Stieltjes for the source kernel and blocks the stated log-derivative route to the downstream Thorin-Bernstein composition claim.
