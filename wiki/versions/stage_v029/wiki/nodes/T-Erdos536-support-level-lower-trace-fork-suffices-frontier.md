# Theorem: Erdos536 support level lower trace fork suffices frontier

## Statement

Support-level pivot theorem: for every fixed \(0<\theta<1\) and \(\eta>0\), every admissible non-rank-thin family \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) with \(\nu_k(\mathcal F_k)\ge\eta\) contains an occupied top \(C\in\mathcal F_k\) and two proper lower traces \(A,B\in\mathcal F_k\), \(A,B\subsetneq C\), such that \(A\cup B=C\). Consequently a union-free family in \(H_{k,	heta}\) is rank-thin and hence has vanishing \(\nu_k\)-mass.
