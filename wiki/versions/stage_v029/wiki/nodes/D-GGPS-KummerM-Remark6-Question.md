# Definition: GGPS Remark 6 Kummer M global modulus question

## Statement

Garrappa--Gerhold--Popolizio--Simon Remark 6 asks whether, for all \(\alpha,\beta>0\), \(|{\rm M}(\alpha,\alpha-\beta,z)|\ge {\rm M}(\alpha,\alpha-\beta,\operatorname{Re}z)\) holds for every \(z\in\mathbb C\), and states that this global question seems still open.
