# Theorem: Bazhlekova Bazhlekov alpha-alpha_m<=1 relaxation necessity frontier for propagation positivity package

## Statement

Bazhlekova--Bazhlekov leave open whether, and to what extent, the condition \(\alpha-\alpha_m\le1\) is necessary for the propagation-function properties \(w\ge0\), \(w_t\ge0\), and \(-w_x\ge0\) in the multi-term time-fractional diffusion-wave equation.
