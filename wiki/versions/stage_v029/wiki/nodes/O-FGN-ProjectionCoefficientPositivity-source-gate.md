# Open_Problem: Fractional Gaussian noise projection-coefficient positivity source gate

## Statement

Verify exact source wording and later-literature novelty for the conjecture that, for fractional Gaussian noise with Hurst parameter \(H>1/2\), all coefficients in the projection of one increment onto finitely many subsequent increments are strictly positive. The source reduces the problem to positivity of the solution of a Toeplitz covariance linear system and proves complete monotonicity of the covariance function; determine whether a full proof, a structural reduction, or an exact counterexample would resolve the primary source conjecture.
