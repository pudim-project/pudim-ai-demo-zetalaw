# Theorem: Erdos536 residual union free moving base high multiplicity frontier

## Statement

Residual union-free moving-base high-multiplicity frontier: the remaining lower-trace-poor obstruction is a positive-mass high-support non-rank-thin admissible family that is not coverable by \(o(\sqrt{V_k})\) antichains after negligible discards, lacks subcritical lower-trace multiplicity relative to deletion weights, avoids finite-core shielding, and uses moving bases to evade the product-measure antichain and deletion sinks. Constructing or excluding this residual condition remains open.
