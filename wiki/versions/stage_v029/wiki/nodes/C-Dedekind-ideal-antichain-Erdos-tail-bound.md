# Corollary: Ideal-antichain Erdos tail bound over a fixed number field

## Statement

For every fixed number field K, ideal antichains satisfy the tail Erdos-type bound with constant kappa_K.
\[
limsup_{X->infty} sup_A sum_{a in A, Na>=X} 1/(Na log Na) <= kappa_K.
\]

## Scope

- A ranges over antichains of nonzero integral ideals under ideal divisibility
- kappa_K is the residue of zeta_K at s=1
- upper bound only; no optimal lower bound claimed
