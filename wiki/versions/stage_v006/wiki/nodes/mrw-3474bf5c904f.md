# Problem: Union-aware weighted compression problem for prime-biased union-free families

## Statement

Let \(P_k=\{p_1,\ldots,p_k\}\), with prime-biased product law
\[
\nu_k(p_i\in S)=\frac1{p_i}.
\]
Find a replacement for ordinary weight-increasing shifting that is strong
enough to support the weighted union-free route [[mrw-55a8d9eddd2e]].

One possible formulation is:

For every fixed \(0<\theta<1\), every \(\eta>0\), and every union-free
\(\mathcal F\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) with \(\nu_k(\mathcal F)\ge\eta\), prove
that either

1. a union-free, \(\nu_k\)-measure-nondecreasing normalization transforms
   \(\mathcal F\) into a family satisfying a structural condition that implies
   the subcritical fiber-width criterion [[mrw-265ec9f57561]], or
2. the failed normalization produces an explicit local obstruction that itself
   forces a union triple or a squarefree pair-link triple.

Equivalently, construct a broad-fiber positive-mass counterexample that defeats
every such union-aware compression and then test whether it also avoids the full
pair-link shadow [[mrw-3c39ca3d1973]].

## Evidence

Ordinary shifting is blocked by [[mrw-8fcc1c2c5cda]].  Thus the familiar
weighted compression path cannot start by repeatedly shifting larger-prime
coordinates toward smaller-prime coordinates.  The next admissible route must
be property-preserving from the start, or it must use a different mechanism
such as a product-measure container or a direct max-fiber decomposition.

## Depends on

- [[mrw-8fcc1c2c5cda]] Ordinary shifts do not preserve union-free families
- [[mrw-55a8d9eddd2e]] Prime-biased weighted union-free theorem
- [[mrw-265ec9f57561]] Subcritical max-fiber antichain width forces vanishing high-support mass

## Used by

- Next #536 loop: replace the invalid ordinary-compression route with an
  admissible compression, ad-extremis shift, container, or decomposition route.

## Notes

The ordinary shifted-family route is blocked by [[mrw-8fcc1c2c5cda]].  The
external shifting literature suggests that some properties require
``ad extremis'' or property-preserving shifts rather than unrestricted shifts.
For the present route, any such modification must be weighted: shifting a
larger prime coordinate toward a smaller prime coordinate is useful only if it
does not reduce \(\nu_k\)-mass or if the lost mass is quantitatively controlled.

The focused Oracle audit recommended treating ad-extremis shifting as an
auxiliary diagnostic rather than the main route: failed shifts should be mined
for shift-resistance certificates that force a union triple, a pair-link triple,
or the broad max-fiber obstruction measured by [[mrw-265ec9f57561]].
