# Proposition: Incompatible near-pure inherited signatures have small side overlap

## Statement

Use the ancestor complete-bipartite slice notation of
[[mrw-36595780824f]].  Thus \(P\) is finite,
\(\mathcal F\subseteq2^P\) is pair-link-free, \(Q\subseteq R_i\subseteq P\)
for \(1\le i\le m\), and the upper slice graphs \(G_{R_i}^{\mathcal F}\)
contain nondegenerate complete bipartite graphs \(K_{X_i,Y_i}\) on a common
vertex set
\[
V=X_i\sqcup Y_i .
\]
Let \(V_\tau\) denote the resulting ancestor-signature classes, with
\(\bar\tau=\mathbf 1-\tau\).

Let \(w_v\ge0\) be a common weight function on \(V\).  For \(j=1,2\), let
\[
U_j,W_j\subseteq V
\]
be nonempty disjoint sets with positive side masses
\[
A_j=\sum_{u\in U_j}w_u>0,\qquad
B_j=\sum_{v\in W_j}w_v>0 .
\]
Assume that for some \(1/2\le\lambda_j\le1\),
\[
M_Q(U_j,W_j)
=
\sum_{\substack{u\in U_j,\ v\in W_j\\
\{u,v\}\in E_Q^{\mathcal F}}}w_uw_v
\ge \lambda_j A_jB_j .
\tag{1}
\]
For each \(j\), choose an ancestor signature \(\tau_j\) supplied by
[[mrw-36595780824f]], so that
\[
\sum_{u\in U_j\cap V_{\tau_j}}w_u\ge\lambda_jA_j,
\qquad
\sum_{v\in W_j\cap V_{\bar\tau_j}}w_v\ge\lambda_jB_j .
\tag{2}
\]

Then incompatible chosen signatures force small overlap:
\[
\tau_1\ne\tau_2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\sum_{x\in U_1\cap U_2}w_x
\le
(1-\lambda_1)A_1+(1-\lambda_2)A_2,
\tag{3}
\]
and
\[
\tau_1\ne\tau_2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\sum_{x\in W_1\cap W_2}w_x
\le
(1-\lambda_1)B_1+(1-\lambda_2)B_2.
\tag{4}
\]
The corresponding cross-side forms are
\[
\tau_1\ne\bar\tau_2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\sum_{x\in U_1\cap W_2}w_x
\le
(1-\lambda_1)A_1+(1-\lambda_2)B_2,
\tag{5}
\]
and
\[
\bar\tau_1\ne\tau_2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\sum_{x\in W_1\cap U_2}w_x
\le
(1-\lambda_1)B_1+(1-\lambda_2)A_2.
\tag{6}
\]

Equivalently, two near-complete inherited-signature corridors can share
substantial common side weight only when their chosen near-pure signatures
are compatible on that shared side.  In the complete case
\(\lambda_1=\lambda_2=1\), any positive weighted same-side overlap forces
\(\tau_1=\tau_2\), and any positive weighted cross-side overlap forces
complementary signatures.

## Proof

Only (3) needs proof; the other estimates are the same argument with the
appropriate pure side classes.

By (2),
\[
\sum_{u\in U_j\setminus V_{\tau_j}}w_u
\le
(1-\lambda_j)A_j
\qquad(j=1,2).
\tag{7}
\]
If \(\tau_1\ne\tau_2\), then the signature classes \(V_{\tau_1}\) and
\(V_{\tau_2}\) are disjoint.  Hence every point of \(U_1\cap U_2\) lies
outside at least one of these two pure classes:
\[
U_1\cap U_2
\subseteq
\bigl(U_1\setminus V_{\tau_1}\bigr)
\cup
\bigl(U_2\setminus V_{\tau_2}\bigr).
\tag{8}
\]
Summing the nonnegative weights over (8) and using (7) gives
\[
\sum_{x\in U_1\cap U_2}w_x
\le
\sum_{x\in U_1\setminus V_{\tau_1}}w_x
+
\sum_{x\in U_2\setminus V_{\tau_2}}w_x
\le
(1-\lambda_1)A_1+(1-\lambda_2)A_2,
\]
which proves (3).

For (4), use the pure classes \(V_{\bar\tau_1}\) and
\(V_{\bar\tau_2}\), which are disjoint exactly when
\(\tau_1\ne\tau_2\).  For (5), use \(V_{\tau_1}\) on \(U_1\) and
\(V_{\bar\tau_2}\) on \(W_2\); these classes are disjoint exactly when
\(\tau_1\ne\bar\tau_2\).  For (6), use \(V_{\bar\tau_1}\) on \(W_1\) and
\(V_{\tau_2}\) on \(U_2\).  The same union-bound calculation gives the
displayed estimates.

When \(\lambda_1=\lambda_2=1\), the right side of each applicable estimate is
zero.  Since weights are nonnegative, any positive weighted overlap on the
indicated sides is possible only when the displayed incompatibility condition
fails, which is exactly the stated compatibility conclusion.

## Notes

- This proposition is a packing tool for the global coherent-corridor assembly
  problem.  It does not prove that many heavy corridors exist, nor that their
  selected signatures must be incompatible.
- The result gives a concrete way to audit a proposed coherent signature-tree
  assembly: if different near-complete corridors reuse the same weighted
  vertices on a side while assigning incompatible inherited signatures, then
  the reused mass must be charged to the near-purity defects
  \(1-\lambda_j\).
- No \(M_{P_k}(\theta)\), \(U_k(\theta)\), or \(R_P(\theta)\) vanishing theorem
  is claimed.
