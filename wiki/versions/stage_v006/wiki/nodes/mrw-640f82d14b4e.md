# Proposition: Endpoint-tower residual plus occupancy defect bounds high-support mass

## Statement

Let
\[
P=P_0\supseteq P_1\supseteq\cdots\supseteq P_r
\]
be a finite endpoint tower, with
\[
P_{j-1}=P_j\sqcup X_j\sqcup Y_j,\qquad X_j,Y_j\ne\varnothing
\qquad(1\le j\le r),
\tag{1}
\]
and all endpoint classes disjoint from the terminal core \(P_r\).  Let
\(\nu_P\) be a product law on \(2^P\) with coordinate probabilities
\(q_p\in(0,1)\), and let \(\Gamma_r\) be the endpoint one-from-each
probability product defined in [[mrw-b52df00c958c]].  For a real threshold
\(L\), write
\[
H_L=\{S\subseteq P:|S|>L\}.
\tag{2}
\]
Let
\[
E_{\mathrm{tw}}
=
\{S\subseteq P: |S\cap X_j|=|S\cap Y_j|=1
\text{ for every }1\le j\le r\}
\tag{3}
\]
be the exact tower occupancy event, and let
\[
D_{\mathrm{tw}}=2^P\setminus E_{\mathrm{tw}}
\tag{4}
\]
be the occupancy-defect event.

If \(\mathcal F\subseteq2^P\) is pair-link-free, then
\[
\nu_P(\mathcal F\cap H_L)
\le
\Gamma_r\,\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r)
+
\nu_P(\mathcal F\cap D_{\mathrm{tw}}\cap H_L),
\tag{5}
\]
where \(\mathfrak M_{P_r}\) is the shifted terminal pair-link-free residual
from [[mrw-b52df00c958c]] under the restricted product law on \(P_r\).
Consequently, if for some \(\delta\ge0\),
\[
\nu_P(\mathcal F\cap H_L)
\ge
\Gamma_r\,\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r)+\delta,
\tag{6}
\]
then
\[
\nu_P(\mathcal F\cap D_{\mathrm{tw}}\cap H_L)\ge\delta.
\tag{7}
\]

In the one-level case \(P=Z\sqcup X\sqcup Y\), this says that, with
\[
E_{X,Y}=\{S:|S\cap X|=|S\cap Y|=1\}
\tag{8}
\]
and \(D_{X,Y}=2^P\setminus E_{X,Y}\),
\[
\nu_P(\mathcal F\cap H_L)
\le
\alpha_X\beta_Y\,\mathfrak M_Z(L-2)
+
\nu_P(\mathcal F\cap D_{X,Y}\cap H_L),
\tag{9}
\]
using the one-from-\(X\) and one-from-\(Y\) probabilities of
[[mrw-fe13472e08c8]].

For the prime-biased law \(\nu_P(p\in S)=1/p\), the same statement applies to
\[
H_{P,\theta}=\{S\subseteq P:|S|>\theta S_P\},
\qquad
S_P=\sum_{p\in P}\frac1p,
\tag{10}
\]
by taking \(L=\theta S_P\).  Thus any high-support mass beyond the exact
endpoint-tower residual must be carried by sets that select zero, two, or more
points from at least one endpoint class.  The proposition does not bound that
defect mass; it isolates it as the next necessary target.

## Proof

Decompose
\[
\mathcal F_{\mathrm{ex}}=\mathcal F\cap E_{\mathrm{tw}},
\qquad
\mathcal F_{\mathrm{def}}=\mathcal F\cap D_{\mathrm{tw}}.
\tag{11}
\]
These two subfamilies are disjoint and cover \(\mathcal F\).  Hence
\[
\nu_P(\mathcal F\cap H_L)
=
\nu_P(\mathcal F_{\mathrm{ex}}\cap H_L)
+
\nu_P(\mathcal F_{\mathrm{def}}\cap H_L).
\tag{12}
\]

Because \(\mathcal F\) is pair-link-free, every subfamily of
\(\mathcal F\) is pair-link-free.  In particular
\(\mathcal F_{\mathrm{ex}}\) is pair-link-free.  Since every member of
\(\mathcal F_{\mathrm{ex}}\) satisfies the exact tower occupancy condition
(3), it has a unique endpoint transcript and therefore a terminal-fiber tower
representation of the form used in [[mrw-b52df00c958c]].  The terminal fibers
in that representation are pair-link-free by the pair-link criterion in
[[mrw-b52df00c958c]].

Therefore \(\mathcal F_{\mathrm{ex}}\) belongs to the exact endpoint-tower
class whose high-support value is bounded in [[mrw-b52df00c958c]].  Applying
that proposition gives
\[
\nu_P(\mathcal F_{\mathrm{ex}}\cap H_L)
\le
\Gamma_r\,\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r).
\tag{13}
\]
Substituting (13) into the disjoint decomposition (12) proves (5), since
\(\mathcal F_{\mathrm{def}}=\mathcal F\cap D_{\mathrm{tw}}\).  Inequality
(7) is just (5) rearranged under hypothesis (6).

The one-level formula (9) is the specialization \(r=1\), with
\(P_1=Z\), \(X_1=X\), \(Y_1=Y\), and
\(\Gamma_1=\alpha_X\beta_Y\).  This is also the variational value isolated in
[[mrw-fe13472e08c8]], whose endpoint-pair decoupling rests on
[[mrw-d7b3299d3813]].  The prime-biased formulation follows by substituting
\(L=\theta S_P\) in the already proved threshold statement.

## Notes

- This is an accounting proposition, not a terminal bound.  It proves that the
  exact zero-\(\Xi\) endpoint-tower branch contributes only the self-similar
  residual already audited in [[mrw-b52df00c958c]].
- Contextual ancestry: [[mrw-23227179a350]] classifies the clean zero-\(\Xi\)
  exact-occupancy alternative, [[mrw-d7b3299d3813]] proves the one-level
  endpoint-pair interval decoupling, and [[mrw-fe13472e08c8]] records the
  one-level variational value.  The direct proof of this residual-plus-error
  decomposition only needs [[mrw-b52df00c958c]].
- The remaining live branch is precisely
  \(\mathcal F\cap D_{\mathrm{tw}}\cap H_L\): sets selecting zero, two, or
  more points from at least one normalized support class.
- To finish the current corridor route, a future theorem must either bound
  this occupancy-defect mass directly, force positive pointwise mixed
  incidence in the sense of [[mrw-7f0eb8d1648c]], or reduce the defect slice to
  a smaller terminal core residual.
- No \(M_{P_k}(\theta)\), \(U_k(\theta)\), or \(R_P(\theta)\) theorem follows
  from this node alone.
