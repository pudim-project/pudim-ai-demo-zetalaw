# Corollary: Sharper compact maximum bracket for the n=2 lower envelope

## Statement

Let
\[
C_2(x)=\psi''(x)+x\psi^{(3)}(x),
\qquad
P_2(x)=\psi''(x)\psi''(1/x),
\]
and
\[
Q_2(x)=\frac{\log(P_2(x)/C_2(x))}{\log x}
\qquad(0<x<1).
\]
Then
\[
Q_2(7/25)<\frac{4629}{2000}
<Q_2(23/80),
\qquad
Q_2(3/10)<\frac{4629}{2000}
<Q_2(23/80).
\]
Consequently \(Q_2\) attains its maximum on the compact interval
\[
\left[\,\frac7{25},\frac3{10}\,\right]
\]
at some point
\[
\xi\in\left(\frac7{25},\frac3{10}\right),
\]
and
\[
Q_2(\xi)>\frac{4629}{2000}.
\]
In particular, the \(n=2\) lower envelope satisfies
\[
L_2:=\sup_{0<x<1}Q_2(x)>\frac{4629}{2000},
\]
so every admissible \(n=2\) beta parameter satisfies
\[
\beta>\frac{4629}{2000}.
\]

## Proof

Put
\[
Z_s(a)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(a+k)^{-s}.
\]
For \(n=2\), the Hurwitz-zeta formula gives
\[
C_2(x)=2(3xZ_4(x)-Z_3(x)),
\qquad
P_2(x)=4Z_3(x)Z_3(1/x),
\]
and hence
\[
R(x):=\frac{P_2(x)}{C_2(x)}
=
\frac{2Z_3(x)Z_3(1/x)}
{3xZ_4(x)-Z_3(x)}.
\]
For \(s>1\), \(a>0\), and \(N\ge1\), define
\[
S_{s,N}(a)=\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}(a+k)^{-s},
\]
\[
L_{s,N}(a)=S_{s,N}(a)+\frac{(a+N)^{1-s}}{s-1},
\qquad
U_{s,N}(a)=S_{s,N}(a)+\frac{(a+N-1)^{1-s}}{s-1}.
\]
Monotone integral comparison gives
\[
L_{s,N}(a)<Z_s(a)<U_{s,N}(a).
\]
Using \(N=80\), exact rational substitution into the displayed formula for \(R(x)\) gives
\[
R(7/25)>\frac{6567}{125000},
\]
\[
R(23/80)<\frac{55847}{1000000},
\]
and
\[
R(3/10)>\frac{1541}{25000}.
\]
The required power comparisons are exact:
\[
\left(\frac{6567}{125000}\right)^{2000}
>
\left(\frac7{25}\right)^{4629},
\]
\[
\left(\frac{55847}{1000000}\right)^{2000}
<
\left(\frac{23}{80}\right)^{4629},
\]
and
\[
\left(\frac{1541}{25000}\right)^{2000}
>
\left(\frac3{10}\right)^{4629}.
\]
Therefore
\[
R(7/25)>(7/25)^{4629/2000},
\qquad
R(23/80)<(23/80)^{4629/2000},
\qquad
R(3/10)>(3/10)^{4629/2000}.
\]
Since \(\log x<0\) on \(0<x<1\), \(Q_2(x)>r\) is equivalent to \(R(x)<x^r\), and \(Q_2(x)<r\) is equivalent to \(R(x)>x^r\).  This proves the three threshold comparisons.

The function \(Q_2\) is continuous on \([7/25,3/10]\), because \(C_2\) and \(P_2\) are positive there by [[wiki/nodes/mrw-0241ab931d33|Even-order envelope reduction for polygamma beta windows]].  Both endpoints lie below \(4629/2000\), while the interior point \(23/80\) lies above \(4629/2000\).  Hence a compact-interval maximizer lies in \((7/25,3/10)\) and has value above \(4629/2000\).  The lower-envelope and beta-obstruction conclusions follow from the same envelope reduction.

## Notes

- This refines the earlier bracket \([1/4,1/3]\) to \([7/25,3/10]\).
- The later localization [[wiki/nodes/mrw-3712cf1c88d8|Refined compact localization for the n=2 lower-envelope maximum]] narrows this bracket to \([1409/5000,293/1000]\) at the same rational level \(4629/2000\).
- Numerically, \(Q_2(23/80)\approx2.31454736320\), but the promoted threshold here is the rational level \(4629/2000=2.3145\).
- This is still not a uniqueness theorem or a global upper bound for \(Q_2\) on \((0,1)\).  The next useful target is to certify derivative signs on subintervals of \([7/25,3/10]\), or prove a global ceiling outside that bracket.
