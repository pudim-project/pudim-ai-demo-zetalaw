# Corollary: Small distinct-cover probability is terminal cover-atom rarity

## Statement

Let \(U\) be a nonempty finite terminal block carrying a product law with
coordinate probabilities
\[
0<q_u\le \frac12
\qquad (u\in U).
\]
Write
\[
a_U=\prod_{u\in U}q_u,
\qquad
C_U=\prod_{u\in U}q_u(2-q_u).
\]
Let
\[
\operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_U
=
\prod_{u\in U}(2q_u-q_u^2)
-
\left(\prod_{u\in U}q_u\right)^2
\]
be the distinct-cover probability from `mrw-9077aa1c34bc`: the probability
that two independent \(U\)-traces cover \(U\) by distinct sets.  Then
\[
\operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_U
=
C_U-a_U^2
=
a_U\left(\prod_{u\in U}(2-q_u)-a_U\right).
\]

For every nonempty \(U\),
\[
a_U
\le
\operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_U
\le
C_U
\le
2^{|U|}a_U,
\]
and also
\[
(1-3^{-|U|})C_U
\le
\operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_U
\le
C_U.
\]
In particular,
\[
\frac23 C_U\le \operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_U\le C_U.
\]

Thus if
\[
\operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_U<\varepsilon
\]
for \(0<\varepsilon<1\), then
\[
C_U<\frac32\varepsilon,
\qquad
a_U<\varepsilon,
\qquad
\sum_{u\in U}-\log q_u>\log\frac1\varepsilon.
\]
Small distinct-cover probability in the half-biased terminal regime is
therefore a cover-atom/all-present-atom rarity branch, not a new source of
terminal decay.

Moreover, if \(|U|\le K\), then
\[
\operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_U<\varepsilon
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\min_{u\in U}q_u<\varepsilon^{1/K}.
\]
Equivalently, if \(q_u\ge\alpha>0\) for all \(u\in U\) and \(|U|\le K\), then
\[
\operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_U\ge \alpha^K.
\]
The bounded-size hypothesis is essential: with \(q_u=1/2\) for many
coordinates, the cover probability tends to zero even though no coordinate
probability is small.

Finally, combine this with `mrw-9077aa1c34bc`.  If \(U\ne\emptyset\) is a
lower terminal parent in the comparable upper mixed-shadow branch, and every
\(U\)-section of an upper terminal family is top-union-free, then every
subfamily \(\mathcal W\) of that upper family satisfies
\[
\mu_T(\mathcal W)\le \sqrt{1-\operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_U}.
\]
Hence:

- if \(C_U\ge\varepsilon\), then
  \[
  \mu_T(\mathcal W)\le \sqrt{1-\frac23\varepsilon};
  \]
- if \(a_U\ge\varepsilon\), then
  \[
  \mu_T(\mathcal W)\le \sqrt{1-\varepsilon}
  \le
  1-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}.
  \]

Consequently an upper mixed-shadow branch with very large upper-fiber mass can
avoid this cover cap only when the active lower parents have tiny terminal
cover atom, and in particular tiny all-present atom.

## Proof

The identity
\[
\operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_U=C_U-a_U^2
\]
is the formula from `mrw-9077aa1c34bc`: \(C_U\) is the probability that two
independent traces cover every coordinate in \(U\), and \(a_U^2\) is the
probability of the unique equal covering pair \(X=Y=U\).  Pulling out
\(a_U\) gives
\[
\operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_U
=
a_U\left(\prod_{u\in U}(2-q_u)-a_U\right).
\]

Let \(m=|U|\ge1\).  Since \(q_u\le1/2\),
\[
\prod_{u\in U}(2-q_u)\ge\left(\frac32\right)^m,
\qquad
a_U\le\left(\frac12\right)^m.
\]
Therefore
\[
\prod_{u\in U}(2-q_u)-a_U
\ge
\left(\frac32\right)^m-\left(\frac12\right)^m
\ge1.
\]
Multiplying by \(a_U\) proves
\[
a_U\le\operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_U.
\]
The upper bounds
\[
\operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_U\le C_U\le2^m a_U
\]
are immediate from \(C_U=a_U\prod_{u\in U}(2-q_u)\) and \(2-q_u\le2\).

For the comparison with \(C_U\), note that
\[
\frac{a_U^2}{C_U}
=
\prod_{u\in U}\frac{q_u}{2-q_u}.
\]
Because \(q_u\le1/2\), each factor is at most \(1/3\).  Hence
\[
a_U^2\le3^{-m}C_U,
\]
and so
\[
\operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_U
=
C_U-a_U^2
\ge
(1-3^{-m})C_U.
\]
Since \(m\ge1\), this gives the displayed \((2/3)C_U\)-bound.

The small-cover consequences follow immediately.  If
\(\operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_U<\varepsilon\), then
\[
a_U<\varepsilon
\]
and
\[
C_U\le\frac32\operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_U<\frac32\varepsilon.
\]
Taking logarithms of \(a_U<\varepsilon\) gives
\[
\sum_{u\in U}-\log q_u>\log\frac1\varepsilon.
\]
If \(|U|\le K\) and every \(q_u\ge\varepsilon^{1/K}\), then
\[
a_U\ge (\varepsilon^{1/K})^{|U|}
\ge
\varepsilon,
\]
contradicting \(a_U<\varepsilon\).  The \(\alpha\)-form is the same argument:
if \(q_u\ge\alpha\) and \(|U|\le K\), then
\[
a_U\ge\alpha^{|U|}\ge\alpha^K,
\]
so
\[
\operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_U\ge a_U\ge\alpha^K.
\]

The fiber consequences are just `mrw-9077aa1c34bc` plus the estimates above.
If \(C_U\ge\varepsilon\), then
\[
\operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_U\ge\frac23 C_U\ge\frac23\varepsilon.
\]
If \(a_U\ge\varepsilon\), then
\[
\operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_U\ge a_U\ge\varepsilon.
\]
Substituting these lower bounds into
\[
\mu_T(\mathcal W)\le \sqrt{1-\operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_U}
\]
proves the two caps.  Finally,
\[
\sqrt{1-\varepsilon}\le1-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}
\]
for \(0\le\varepsilon\le1\).

## Depends on

- `mrw-9077aa1c34bc`: top-union-free sections have the distinct-cover mass cap.
- `mrw-dda277c43571`: comparable upper mixed-shadow exclusions are exactly
  sectionwise top-union-freeness.

## Used by

- Future small-cover branch classification: small cover probability is now
  quarantined as tiny terminal cover atom/all-present atom, except for the
  explicitly excluded empty-block boundary.
- Future aggregation attempts: lower parents with non-negligible \(C_U\) or
  \(a_U\) impose a uniform cap on the upper fiber; avoiding the cap requires
  lower parents with large multiplicative surprise \(\sum-\log q_u\) or
  growing support.

## Notes

- The empty block is excluded.  For \(U=\emptyset\), one has
  \(\operatorname{cov}^{\ne}_\emptyset=0\) but \(a_\emptyset=C_\emptyset=1\).
- Oracle suggested the sharper cover-atom formulation and warned against any
  conclusion that small cover probability forces a small coordinate without a
  support-size bound.  The statement above keeps the bounded-size conversion
  explicit.
- This corollary is not terminal Erdos 536 evidence.  It only identifies the
  escape from the top-union-free cover cap as a tiny terminal atom/product
  obstruction that still needs residual or aggregate classification.
