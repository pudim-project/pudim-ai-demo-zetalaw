# Corollary: Support-light or fiber-sparse branches have direct mass loss

## Statement
Work in the endpoint-fiber setting of `mrw-2b75fd587224`. Let \(H\) be the terminal high window with \(\tau=\nu_T(H)>0\), let \(\mathcal E\) be the active endpoint support with
\[
\Lambda=\pi_B(\mathcal E),
\]
and let
\[
M=\sum_{e\in\mathcal E}\pi_B(e)\nu_T(\mathcal R_e\cap H).
\]
Define
\[
\rho=
\begin{cases}
M/(\Lambda\tau),&\Lambda>0,\\
0,&\Lambda=0.
\end{cases}
\]
Then, including the \(\Lambda=0\) boundary,
\[
0\le \frac{M}{\tau}\le \Lambda,\qquad
0\le \frac{M}{\tau}\le \rho.
\]
Equivalently,
\[
0\le \frac{M}{\tau}\le \min\{\Lambda,\rho\}.
\]

Consequently, for any \(\lambda,r>0\),
\[
\Lambda<\lambda\Rightarrow M<\lambda\tau,\qquad
\rho<r\Rightarrow M<r\tau,
\]
and
\[
\Lambda<\lambda\ \text{or}\ \rho<r
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
M<\max\{\lambda,r\}\tau.
\]
Conversely, for every \(\eta>0\),
\[
M\ge \eta\tau\quad\Rightarrow\quad \Lambda\ge\eta\ \text{and}\ \rho\ge\eta.
\]

If, in addition, the empty-atom branch estimate from `mrw-4a7cdb250fd4`
\[
\frac{M}{\tau}\le \frac{e^{-Q(B)}}{\varepsilon}
\]
is available, then
\[
Q(B)>\log\frac{1}{\varepsilon\lambda r}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\Lambda<\lambda\ \text{or}\ \rho<r,
\]
and therefore
\[
M<\max\{\lambda,r\}\tau.
\]
In particular, with \(\lambda=r=\eta>0\),
\[
Q(B)>\log\frac{1}{\varepsilon\eta^2}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
M<\eta\tau.
\]

## Proof
For every endpoint pattern \(e\),
\[
0\le \nu_T(\mathcal R_e\cap H)\le \nu_T(H)=\tau.
\]
Hence
\[
0\le M\le \tau\sum_{e\in\mathcal E}\pi_B(e)=\Lambda\tau,
\]
so \(0\le M/\tau\le \Lambda\).

If \(\Lambda=0\), the preceding inequality gives \(M=0\), while \(\rho=0\) by convention. Thus \(M/\tau=0\le \rho\). If \(\Lambda>0\), then by definition
\[
\frac{M}{\tau}=\Lambda\rho.
\]
Since \(\Lambda\le 1\), this gives \(M/\tau\le \rho\). This proves the two envelope inequalities. The support-light, fiber-sparse, disjunctive, and converse implications are immediate from them.

For the endpoint-intensity addendum, assume the empty-atom estimate. If
\[
Q(B)>\log\frac{1}{\varepsilon\lambda r},
\]
then \(e^{-Q(B)}/\varepsilon<\lambda r\), so
\[
\Lambda\rho=\frac{M}{\tau}<\lambda r.
\]
If both \(\Lambda\ge\lambda\) and \(\rho\ge r\) held, then \(\Lambda\rho\ge\lambda r\), a contradiction. Therefore \(\Lambda<\lambda\) or \(\rho<r\). The disjunctive mass-loss envelope gives \(M<\max\{\lambda,r\}\tau\), and the specialization \(\lambda=r=\eta\) follows.
