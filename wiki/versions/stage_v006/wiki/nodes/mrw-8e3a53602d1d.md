# Proposition: Complete prime-biased product towers grow or absorb

## Statement

Let \(P=B\sqcup T\) be a finite disjoint decomposition with product law, and
assume all endpoint weights satisfy
\[
0<q_b\le\frac12.
\]
Let
\[
B=X_1\sqcup\cdots\sqcup X_K
\]
be a complete multipartite transversal endpoint tower with nonempty endpoint
parts.  For each part, let
\[
a(X_i)=\sum_{x\in X_i}q_x\prod_{u\in X_i\setminus\{x\}}(1-q_u)
\]
be the probability of selecting exactly one point from \(X_i\), and put
\[
\Gamma=\prod_{i=1}^K a(X_i).
\]
Then
\[
a(X_i)\le\frac12
\qquad\text{for every }i,
\]
and therefore
\[
\Gamma\le2^{-K}.
\]

Consequently, the exact complete product-tower residual
\[
\mathcal R(L)=\Gamma\,\mathfrak M_T(L-K)
\]
satisfies the absolute endpoint-depth bound
\[
\mathcal R(L)\le2^{-K}
\]
for every real threshold \(L\).

Now let
\[
\mu_X=\sum_{x\in X}q_x,\qquad L=\theta\mu_P=\theta(\mu_B+\mu_T),
\]
with \(\mu_T>0\) and \(0\le\theta'<\theta<1\).  Then the finite
grow-or-absorb alternative is:

- if
  \[
  K-\theta\mu_B\le(\theta-\theta')\mu_T,
  \]
  then
  \[
  \mathcal R(\theta\mu_P)
  \le
  \Gamma\,\mathfrak M_T(\theta'\mu_T);
  \]
- otherwise
  \[
  K>\theta\mu_B+(\theta-\theta')\mu_T
  \]
  and hence
  \[
  \mathcal R(\theta\mu_P)
  \le
  2^{-K}
  <
  2^{-(\theta-\theta')\mu_T}.
  \]

Thus a complete prime-biased product tower either absorbs into a degraded
terminal residual, or its endpoint depth gives absolute exponential decay in
the available terminal threshold slack.  This is a theorem for complete
multipartite transversal endpoint towers only; it is not a theorem for
arbitrary interval-shielded endpoint supports.

## Proof

First prove the one-hit bound.  For a finite nonempty endpoint part \(X\), set
\[
r_x=\frac{q_x}{1-q_x}.
\]
Since \(0<q_x\le1/2\), one has \(0<r_x\le1\).  Let
\[
R=\sum_{x\in X}r_x.
\]
Then
\[
a(X)
=
\left(\prod_{x\in X}(1-q_x)\right)
\sum_{x\in X}\frac{q_x}{1-q_x}
=
\frac{R}{\prod_{x\in X}(1+r_x)}.
\]
Write \(R=m+t\), where \(m=\lfloor R\rfloor\) and \(0\le t<1\).  Over the
compact closure \(0\le r_x\le1\) with fixed sum \(R\), the function
\[
\sum_x\log(1+r_x)
\]
is concave.  Hence its minimum over the fixed-sum polytope occurs at an
extreme point.  Such an extreme point has \(m\) coordinates equal to \(1\), one
coordinate equal to \(t\) if \(t>0\), and all remaining coordinates equal to
\(0\).  Therefore
\[
\prod_x(1+r_x)\ge 2^m(1+t),
\]
and so
\[
a(X)\le\frac{m+t}{2^m(1+t)}.
\]
If \(m=0\), this upper bound is \(t/(1+t)<1/2\).  If \(m=1\), it is exactly
\(1/2\).  If \(m\ge2\), then
\[
\frac{m+t}{2^m(1+t)}
\le
\frac{m}{2^m}
\le
\frac12,
\]
because \(m\le2^{m-1}\) for \(m\ge2\).  Thus \(a(X)\le1/2\).

For the complete multipartite transversal tower, the endpoint coefficient is
the product of the one-hit probabilities over its \(K\) endpoint parts:
\[
\Gamma=\prod_{i=1}^K a(X_i).
\]
The one-hit bound gives
\[
\Gamma\le2^{-K}.
\]
Since every residual supremum is at most \(1\),
\[
\mathcal R(L)=\Gamma\,\mathfrak M_T(L-K)\le\Gamma\le2^{-K}.
\]

Finally set \(L=\theta\mu_P\).  If
\[
K-\theta\mu_B\le(\theta-\theta')\mu_T,
\]
then the threshold-degraded absorption proposition
[[mrw-7f4dbc4882f4]] gives
\[
\mathcal R(\theta\mu_P)\le\Gamma\,\mathfrak M_T(\theta'\mu_T).
\]
If the displayed inequality fails, then
\[
K>\theta\mu_B+(\theta-\theta')\mu_T.
\]
Since \(\theta\mu_B\ge0\), this implies \(K>(\theta-\theta')\mu_T\).  Combining
with \(\mathcal R(\theta\mu_P)\le2^{-K}\) gives
\[
\mathcal R(\theta\mu_P)
\le2^{-K}
<
2^{-(\theta-\theta')\mu_T}.
\]

## Notes

- The hypothesis \(q_b\le1/2\) is essential.  Without it, a singleton endpoint
  part with \(q_b>1/2\) violates the one-hit bound.
- The result is finite and does not require a diffuse limit.  In the
  prime-biased squarefree model \(q_b=1/p\), the condition \(q_b\le1/2\) holds
  automatically.
- This closes the complete-product-tower branch at positive threshold
  parameter in the following conditional sense: if the terminal residual
  theorem is proved for all \(0\le\theta'<1\), complete product towers add no
  further obstruction.  The remaining endpoint obstruction must come from
  arbitrary interval-shielded supports, non-complete products, or mass outside
  exact product towers.
- The \(\theta=0\) boundary is not handled by degraded-threshold absorption;
  there is no positive lower parameter \(\theta'<\theta\) in that case.
