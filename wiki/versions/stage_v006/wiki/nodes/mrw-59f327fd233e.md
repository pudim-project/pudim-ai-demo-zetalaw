# Proposition: Endpoint defect residual is terminal-or-overfull

## Statement

Use the endpoint-tower notation and the defect-pattern residual budget of
[[mrw-05f82d03b190]].  Thus
\[
P=P_0\supseteq P_1\supseteq\cdots\supseteq P_r,
\qquad
P_{j-1}=P_j\sqcup X_j\sqcup Y_j,
\]
and
\[
\mathcal R_{\mathrm{def}}(L)
=
\sum_{\omega\in\Omega_{\mathrm{def}}}
\pi(\omega)\,\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-|E(\omega)|).
\]
Let the overfull endpoint-pattern event be
\[
\Omega_{\mathrm{ov}}
=
\{\omega:\exists j,\ |A_j|\ge2\text{ or }|B_j|\ge2\},
\]
and set
\[
\pi_{\mathrm{ov}}=\sum_{\omega\in\Omega_{\mathrm{ov}}}\pi(\omega).
\]
Then, for every real threshold \(L\),
\[
\mathcal R_{\mathrm{def}}(L)
\le
\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r)+\pi_{\mathrm{ov}}.
\]
Moreover,
\[
\pi_{\mathrm{ov}}
\le
\sum_{j=1}^r
\left(
\sum_{\{p,q\}\subseteq X_j}q_pq_q
+
\sum_{\{p,q\}\subseteq Y_j}q_pq_q
\right)
\le
\frac12\sum_{j=1}^r
\bigl(Q(X_j)^2+Q(Y_j)^2\bigr),
\]
where \(Q(C)=\sum_{p\in C}q_p\).

Consequently, if \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{def}}(L)\ge\eta>0\), then either
\[
\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r)\ge \eta/2
\]
or
\[
\pi_{\mathrm{ov}}\ge \eta/2.
\]
Thus, after the endpoint-pattern residual reduction, the defect budget is
large only through a large exact-shift terminal residual or through overfull
selection inside at least one endpoint class.

## Proof

The terminal residual value \(\mathfrak M_{P_r}(t)\) is nonincreasing in
\(t\), because increasing \(t\) shrinks the high-support constraint
\(\{R:|R|>t\}\).  Also \(0\le \mathfrak M_{P_r}(t)\le1\).

Split the sum defining \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{def}}(L)\) into defect patterns
with \(|E(\omega)|\le2r\) and those with \(|E(\omega)|>2r\).  If
\(|E(\omega)|\le2r\), then
\[
L-|E(\omega)|\ge L-2r,
\]
so monotonicity gives
\[
\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-|E(\omega)|)
\le
\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r).
\]
Therefore the total contribution of all defect patterns with
\(|E(\omega)|\le2r\) is at most
\[
\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r)
\sum_{\substack{\omega\in\Omega_{\mathrm{def}}\\ |E(\omega)|\le2r}}
\pi(\omega)
\le
\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r).
\]

If \(|E(\omega)|>2r\), then among the \(2r\) endpoint classes
\[
X_1,Y_1,\ldots,X_r,Y_r
\]
some class contains at least two selected endpoint coordinates.  Hence
\(\omega\in\Omega_{\mathrm{ov}}\).  Since
\(\mathfrak M_{P_r}(\cdot)\le1\), the contribution of this part is at most
\[
\sum_{\substack{\omega\in\Omega_{\mathrm{def}}\\ |E(\omega)|>2r}}\pi(\omega)
\le
\pi_{\mathrm{ov}}.
\]
Combining the two estimates proves
\[
\mathcal R_{\mathrm{def}}(L)
\le
\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r)+\pi_{\mathrm{ov}}.
\]

For a single endpoint class \(C\), the event \(|S\cap C|\ge2\) is contained
in the union over unordered pairs \(\{p,q\}\subseteq C\) of the event
\(\{p,q\}\subseteq S\).  By the product law,
\[
\Pr(\{p,q\}\subseteq S)=q_pq_q,
\]
so the union bound gives
\[
\Pr(|S\cap C|\ge2)
\le
\sum_{\{p,q\}\subseteq C}q_pq_q
\le
\frac12\left(\sum_{p\in C}q_p\right)^2.
\]
Summing this over the endpoint classes \(X_j\) and \(Y_j\) gives the claimed
bound for \(\pi_{\mathrm{ov}}\).

The final alternative is the elementary implication
\[
a+b\ge\eta,\qquad a,b\ge0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
a\ge\eta/2\text{ or }b\ge\eta/2,
\]
with \(a=\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r)\) and \(b=\pi_{\mathrm{ov}}\).

## Notes

- This proposition is the first cross-pattern improvement over the raw
  endpoint-pattern residual budget: balanced underfilled or missing endpoint
  patterns are absorbed by the exact-shift terminal residual
  \(\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r)\).
- A sharper intermediate form is also available.  If
  \[
  \Pi_{\le 2r}^{\mathrm{def}}
  =
  \sum_{\substack{\omega\in\Omega_{\mathrm{def}}\\ |E(\omega)|\le2r}}
  \pi(\omega),
  \qquad
  \Pi_{>2r}^{\mathrm{def}}
  =
  \sum_{\substack{\omega\in\Omega_{\mathrm{def}}\\ |E(\omega)|>2r}}
  \pi(\omega),
  \]
  then
  \[
  \mathcal R_{\mathrm{def}}(L)
  \le
  \Pi_{\le 2r}^{\mathrm{def}}\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r)
  +
  \Pi_{>2r}^{\mathrm{def}},
  \]
  and the displayed bound follows from
  \(\Pi_{\le 2r}^{\mathrm{def}}\le1\) and
  \(\Pi_{>2r}^{\mathrm{def}}\le\pi_{\mathrm{ov}}\).
- The only remaining nonterminal defect branch in this estimate is overfull
  endpoint incidence, where some endpoint class contributes at least two
  selected coordinates.  Turning that overfull incidence into pointwise mixed
  incidence or a full pair-link interval contradiction remains open.
- The result is not a terminal residual decay theorem, not a mixed-incidence
  production theorem, and not an \(R_P(\theta)\) lift.
