# Proposition: Persistent heavy corridors have coherent refined normal forms

## Statement

Fix one coarse corridor, and use the notation and hypotheses of
[[mrw-a9efecc818c7]] and
[[mrw-8a0c228a0166]].  Thus \(m\ge1\) nested upper complete bipartite slice
blocks select a coarse complementary corridor
\[
A=V_\tau,\qquad B=V_{\bar\tau},
\]
and the next \(\ell\ge1\) nested upper cuts refine the corridor into classes
\[
A_\omega,\qquad B_\omega
\qquad(\omega\in\{0,1\}^{\ell}).
\]
Let \(w_v\ge0\) be weights on \(A\cup B\), and put
\[
\alpha_\omega=\sum_{a\in A_\omega}w_a,\qquad
\beta_\omega=\sum_{b\in B_\omega}w_b,
\]
\[
W_A=\sum_\omega\alpha_\omega,\qquad
W_B=\sum_\omega\beta_\omega.
\]
Assume \(W_AW_B>0\).  For the lower edge mass across the coarse corridor,
\[
M_Q(A,B)
=
\sum_{\substack{\{u,v\}\in E_Q^\mathcal F\\
|\{u,v\}\cap A|=|\{u,v\}\cap B|=1}}w_uw_v,
\]
suppose that \(1/2\le\lambda\le1\) and
\[
M_Q(A,B)\ge\lambda W_AW_B.
\tag{1}
\]

Then there is a full refined signature \(\omega^*\in\{0,1\}^{\ell}\) such
that
\[
\alpha_{\omega^*}\ge\lambda W_A,
\qquad
\beta_{\bar\omega^*}\ge\lambda W_B.
\tag{2}
\]

For \(0\le t\le\ell\), write \(\omega^*_{\le t}\) for the length-\(t\) prefix
of \(\omega^*\), and define prefix-class weights by
\[
\alpha^{(t)}_\xi=\sum_{\omega_{\le t}=\xi}\alpha_\omega,
\qquad
\beta^{(t)}_\xi=\sum_{\omega_{\le t}=\xi}\beta_\omega
\qquad(\xi\in\{0,1\}^{t}).
\]
Then the same coherent complementary signature chain carries at least the same
side-weight fractions at every intermediate depth:
\[
\alpha^{(t)}_{\omega^*_{\le t}}\ge\lambda W_A,
\qquad
\beta^{(t)}_{\overline{\omega^*_{\le t}}}\ge\lambda W_B
\qquad(0\le t\le\ell).
\tag{3}
\]
Equivalently, outside the full normal-form cells \(A_{\omega^*}\) and
\(B_{\bar\omega^*}\), at most a \(1-\lambda\) fraction of each side weight
remains.

In the equality case
\[
M_Q(A,B)=W_AW_B,
\tag{4}
\]
all positive \(A\)-weight lies in \(A_{\omega^*}\), all positive \(B\)-weight
lies in \(B_{\bar\omega^*}\), and the lower graph between the two
positive-weight supports is complete up to zero-weight pairs.  Thus a complete
persistent corridor is a single coherent refined-signature corridor: for each
added cut \(j\), the positive-weight support on \(A\) lies on one side of that
cut, while the positive-weight support on \(B\) lies on the opposite side.

## Proof

By [[mrw-8a0c228a0166]], hypothesis (1) gives a refined signature
\(\omega^*\in\{0,1\}^{\ell}\) satisfying (2).  The prefix assertion is then
immediate: for each \(t\), the full class \(A_{\omega^*}\) is contained in the
prefix class with signature \(\omega^*_{\le t}\), and the full class
\(B_{\bar\omega^*}\) is contained in the prefix class with signature
\(\overline{\omega^*_{\le t}}\).  Since all weights are nonnegative,
\[
\alpha^{(t)}_{\omega^*_{\le t}}
\ge
\alpha_{\omega^*}
\ge
\lambda W_A,
\qquad
\beta^{(t)}_{\overline{\omega^*_{\le t}}}
\ge
\beta_{\bar\omega^*}
\ge
\lambda W_B,
\]
which proves (3).

The near-normal-form statement is just the complementary form of (2):
\[
W_A-\alpha_{\omega^*}\le(1-\lambda)W_A,
\qquad
W_B-\beta_{\bar\omega^*}\le(1-\lambda)W_B.
\]

For equality, apply (2) with \(\lambda=1\).  Then
\[
\alpha_{\omega^*}=W_A,\qquad
\beta_{\bar\omega^*}=W_B,
\]
and all other refined classes have zero weight.  Since \(M_Q(A,B)=W_AW_B\) is
also the total weight of all possible cross pairs between \(A\) and \(B\), no
positive-weight cross pair can be missing from the lower edge set.  Otherwise
the missing pair would subtract a positive product from the edge mass.  This
proves completeness on the positive-weight supports and gives the stated
coherent normal form.

## Notes

- This proposition packages the previous near-full concentration result as a
  persistent-corridor normal form.  It is still local to one coarse corridor
  and its nested refinements.
- The result does not prove that such heavy corridors occur globally, nor does
  it prove \(M_{P_k}(\theta)\to0\), \(U_k(\theta)\to0\), or an
  \(R_P(\theta)\) lift.
- The global obstruction is now sharper: a positive-mass high-support
  candidate must either lose heavy-corridor capacity somewhere, or repeatedly
  assemble corridors that are already coherent at all refined depths.  The
  next theorem must rule out that global assembly or construct and test it
  against every full pair-link interval.
