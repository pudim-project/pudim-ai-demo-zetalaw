# Lemma: Squarefree cosunflower criterion for equal pairwise lcm triples

## Statement

Let \(a,b,c\) be squarefree positive integers, and write \(A,B,C\) for their prime-support sets.  Then
\[
[a,b]=[a,c]=[b,c]
\]
if and only if
\[
A\cup B=A\cup C=B\cup C.
\]
Equivalently, the three supports form a cosunflower: no prime belongs to exactly one of \(A,B,C\).

## Proof

For squarefree integers, \(v_p(a),v_p(b),v_p(c)\in\{0,1\}\).  By [[wiki/nodes/mrw-2e217726536f|Prime-valuation criterion for equal pairwise lcm triples]], equal pairwise lcm is equivalent to saying that the maximum in every coordinate is not uniquely attained.  In the \(0/1\) case this means that, for every prime \(p\), either \(p\) belongs to none of \(A,B,C\), or it belongs to at least two of them.

The equality
\[
A\cup B=A\cup C=B\cup C
\]
has exactly the same coordinatewise meaning.  A prime contained in exactly one support belongs to exactly two of the pairwise unions and not the third; a prime contained in none or at least two supports belongs to either none of the pairwise unions or all of them.  Hence the equal-union condition is equivalent to absence of a uniquely supported prime, and therefore to equal pairwise lcm.
