# Counterexample: Ambient endpoint-moment budgets do not force pointwise mixed incidence

## Statement

Let \(P=Z\sqcup X\sqcup Y\) be a finite disjoint decomposition with
\(X,Y\ne\varnothing\), and let \(\nu_P\) be a product law with point
probabilities \(q_p\in(0,1)\).  For every \((x,y)\in X\times Y\), let
\(\mathcal R_{xy}\subseteq2^Z\) be pair-link-free and put
\[
\mathcal A
=
\{R\cup\{x,y\}:x\in X,\ y\in Y,\ R\in\mathcal R_{xy}\}.
\]
Then \(\mathcal A\) is pair-link-free and has zero pointwise mixed incidence
for the exact endpoint supports \(C_0=X\), \(C_1=Y\):
\[
\Xi=w(C_0\cap C_1)=0.
\]

Nevertheless, the ambient endpoint-pair budget from [[mrw-2a765ca2676f]]
\[
R_2
=
\sum_{\{p,q\}\subseteq X}q_pq_q
+
\sum_{\{p,q\}\subseteq Y}q_pq_q
\]
can be positive.  If \(X=\{x_1,x_2\}\) and \(|Y|\ge3\), then the collapsed
same-class deletion \(e=\{x_1,x_2\}\) also has positive absorbed third-order
ambient tail
\[
q_{x_1}q_{x_2}\,\nu_Y(\{B\subseteq Y:|B|\ge3\})>0.
\]

Consequently, ambient endpoint-moment budgets alone do not force
\(\Xi>0\).  Any positive-incidence charging theorem after
[[mrw-2a765ca2676f]] must be phrased using realized high-support mass outside
exact one-from-each occupancy, realized overfull endpoint slices, or another
non-ambient witness.

## Proof

The pair-link claim is exactly [[mrw-d7b3299d3813]]: the assembly
\(\mathcal A\) is pair-link-free if and only if every endpoint fiber
\(\mathcal R_{xy}\) is pair-link-free.  The same result also gives the
high-support mass decomposition by endpoint pair.

By construction, every member of \(\mathcal A\) contains exactly one point of
\(X\) and exactly one point of \(Y\).  Hence the zero-\(\Xi\) exact-occupancy
classification [[mrw-23227179a350]] applies with \(C_0=X\) and \(C_1=Y\).
Since the decomposition of \(P\) is disjoint,
\[
C_0\cap C_1=X\cap Y=\varnothing,
\]
so \(w(C_0\cap C_1)=0\) for every point weight \(w\).

The endpoint-pair budget \(R_2\) is computed from the ambient endpoint
classes, not from sets of \(\mathcal A\) that actually choose two points from
one endpoint class.  Thus \(R_2>0\) whenever \(X\) or \(Y\) contains a
two-point subset with positive coordinate weights.  In particular, if
\(X=\{x_1,x_2\}\), then \(q_{x_1}q_{x_2}>0\).  If also \(|Y|\ge3\), then the
product event \(|B|\ge3\) in \(Y\) has positive \(\nu_Y\)-measure, so
\[
q_{x_1}q_{x_2}\,\nu_Y(\{B:|B|\ge3\})>0.
\]
This is precisely the kind of collapsed-class absorbed endpoint slack that
appears in [[mrw-2a765ca2676f]].

For a completely explicit positive-mass high-support instance, take
\(Z=\varnothing\) and \(\mathcal R_{xy}=\{\varnothing\}\) for every
\((x,y)\).  Then
\[
\mathcal A=\{\{x,y\}:x\in X,\ y\in Y\}
\]
is pair-link-free, has \(\Xi=0\), and satisfies
\(\mathcal A\subseteq\{S:|S|>L\}\) for every real \(L<2\).  Its measure is
\[
\nu_P(\mathcal A)
=
\left(\sum_{x\in X}q_x\prod_{u\in X\setminus\{x\}}(1-q_u)\right)
\left(\sum_{y\in Y}q_y\prod_{v\in Y\setminus\{y\}}(1-q_v)\right)>0.
\]
At the same time \(R_2>0\), and for \(X=\{x_1,x_2\}\), \(|Y|\ge3\), the
absorbed third-order ambient tail above is positive.

Therefore the proposed inference
\[
\text{large ambient endpoint moment budget}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \Xi>0
\]
is false.  The defect mechanism [[mrw-7f0eb8d1648c]] can still be used, but
only after producing positive pointwise mixed incidence from realized escaped
mass rather than from ambient slack.

## Notes

- This counterexample does not refute [[mrw-2a765ca2676f]].  It only separates
  the ambient endpoint-moment error terms in that corollary from any realized
  mass of overfull endpoint selections inside the family.
- The explicit \(Z=\varnothing\) instance is a high-support example for
  thresholds \(L<2\).  For larger thresholds, the same obstruction persists by
  taking terminal-core fibers \(\mathcal R_{xy}\) with positive high-support
  mass above \(L-2\), whenever such fibers are available.
- The next viable target should replace \(R_2\) and the absorbed cubic tail by
  a realized quantity, such as \(\nu_P(\mathcal F\cap H_L\cap D_{\mathrm{ov}})\),
  mass outside exact endpoint occupancy, or a certified escaped slice from the
  strict-deletion decomposition.
