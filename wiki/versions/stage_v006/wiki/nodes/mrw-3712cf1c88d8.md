# Corollary: Refined compact localization for the n=2 lower-envelope maximum

## Statement

Let
\[
C_2(x)=\psi''(x)+x\psi^{(3)}(x),
\qquad
P_2(x)=\psi''(x)\psi''(1/x),
\]
and
\[
Q_2(x)=\frac{\log(P_2(x)/C_2(x))}{\log x}
\qquad(0<x<1).
\]
Then
\[
Q_2(1409/5000)<\frac{4629}{2000}<Q_2(23/80),
\]
and
\[
Q_2(293/1000)<\frac{4629}{2000}<Q_2(23/80).
\]
Consequently \(Q_2\) attains its maximum on
\[
\left[\,\frac{1409}{5000},\frac{293}{1000}\,\right]
\]
at an interior point, and the maximum value on this interval is greater than \(4629/2000\).  In particular,
\[
L_2:=\sup_{0<x<1}Q_2(x)>\frac{4629}{2000},
\]
so every admissible \(n=2\) beta parameter satisfies
\[
\beta>\frac{4629}{2000}.
\]

## Proof

As in [[wiki/nodes/mrw-8c1324a498bf|Sharper compact maximum bracket for the n=2 lower envelope]], put
\[
Z_s(a)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(a+k)^{-s}
\]
and
\[
R(x):=\frac{P_2(x)}{C_2(x)}
=
\frac{2Z_3(x)Z_3(1/x)}
{3xZ_4(x)-Z_3(x)}.
\]
For \(s>1\), \(a>0\), and \(N\ge1\), define
\[
S_{s,N}(a)=\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}(a+k)^{-s},
\]
\[
L_{s,N}(a)=S_{s,N}(a)+\frac{(a+N)^{1-s}}{s-1},
\qquad
U_{s,N}(a)=S_{s,N}(a)+\frac{(a+N-1)^{1-s}}{s-1}.
\]
Monotone integral comparison gives \(L_{s,N}(a)<Z_s(a)<U_{s,N}(a)\).

Using these rational Hurwitz-zeta bounds gives the following exact enclosures:
\[
R(1409/5000)>\frac{5331983}{100000000},
\]
\[
R(23/80)<\frac{55847}{1000000},
\]
and
\[
R(293/1000)>\frac{583533}{10000000}.
\]
The endpoint lower bounds use \(N=200\), while the middle upper bound is already certified by the \(N=80\) enclosure from [[wiki/nodes/mrw-8c1324a498bf|Sharper compact maximum bracket for the n=2 lower envelope]].

The needed power comparisons are exact:
\[
\left(\frac{5331983}{100000000}\right)^{2000}
>
\left(\frac{1409}{5000}\right)^{4629},
\]
\[
\left(\frac{55847}{1000000}\right)^{2000}
<
\left(\frac{23}{80}\right)^{4629},
\]
and
\[
\left(\frac{583533}{10000000}\right)^{2000}
>
\left(\frac{293}{1000}\right)^{4629}.
\]
Therefore
\[
R(1409/5000)>(1409/5000)^{4629/2000},
\]
\[
R(23/80)<(23/80)^{4629/2000},
\]
and
\[
R(293/1000)>(293/1000)^{4629/2000}.
\]
Since \(\log x<0\) on \(0<x<1\), \(Q_2(x)>r\) is equivalent to \(R(x)<x^r\), and \(Q_2(x)<r\) is equivalent to \(R(x)>x^r\).  This proves both displayed threshold comparisons.

The function \(Q_2\) is continuous on the compact interval because \(C_2\) and \(P_2\) are positive there by [[wiki/nodes/mrw-0241ab931d33|Even-order envelope reduction for polygamma beta windows]].  Both endpoints lie below \(4629/2000\), while the interior point \(23/80\) lies above it.  Hence a compact-interval maximizer lies in the open interval and has value above \(4629/2000\).  The lower-envelope and beta-obstruction conclusions follow from the envelope reduction.

## Notes

- This refines the prior localization \([7/25,3/10]\) to \([1409/5000,293/1000]\) without increasing the rational lower level \(4629/2000\).
- The certificate remains interval-localization evidence, not a derivative-sign theorem, uniqueness theorem, or global upper bound for \(Q_2\) on \((0,1)\).
- The next useful target is to turn the rational-enclosure machinery into a derivative-sign certificate on subintervals around this localized maximum.
- The later critical-point equation node converts the interior maximizer into an explicit Hurwitz-zeta derivative equation.
