# Corollary: Empty-atom diagonal quarantine bounds high-window mass

## Statement

Let \(B\) be finite and let \(\pi_B\) be the product law on \(2^B\),
\[
\pi_B(e)=\prod_{b\in e}q_b\prod_{b\notin e}(1-q_b),
\qquad
0<q_b\le\frac12.
\]
Set
\[
P_0(B)=\prod_{b\in B}(1-q_b),
\qquad
Q(B)=\sum_{b\in B}q_b.
\]
Let \(T\) carry a probability law \(\nu_T\), let \(H\subseteq2^T\) satisfy
\[
\tau=\nu_T(H)>0,
\]
and let \(\mathcal E\subseteq2^B\).  For each \(e\in\mathcal E\), let
\(\mathcal R_e\subseteq2^T\).  Define
\[
M=\sum_{e\in\mathcal E}\pi_B(e)\,\nu_T(\mathcal R_e\cap H)
\]
and
\[
\Delta
=
\sum_{e\in\mathcal E}\pi_B(e)^2\,\nu_T(\mathcal R_e\cap H).
\]
Then
\[
\Delta\le P_0(B)M\le e^{-Q(B)}M.
\]
If \(M>0\), then
\[
\frac{\Delta}{M^2/\tau}
\le
\frac{P_0(B)\tau}{M}
\le
\frac{e^{-Q(B)}\tau}{M}
\le
\frac{e^{-Q(B)}}{M}.
\]

Now assume additionally the endpoint-fiber overlap accounting from
`mrw-ad1f6f41665a` and `mrw-90be6f9a7f88`: namely,
\[
\Omega_{\mathrm{tot}}
\ge
\frac{M^2}{\tau},
\qquad
\Omega_{\mathrm{tot}}
=
\Omega_{\mathcal C}+\Delta+\Omega_{\mathcal N},
\]
where \(\Omega_{\mathcal C}\) is chargeable ordered-pair energy and
\(\Omega_{\mathcal N}\) is distinct nonchargeable ordered-pair energy.  If
\[
\Omega_{\mathcal C}\le\gamma\frac{M^2}{\tau}
\]
and
\[
c:=1-\gamma-\frac{P_0(B)\tau}{M}>0,
\]
then
\[
\Omega_{\mathcal N}\ge c\frac{M^2}{\tau}.
\]
If
\[
\Lambda=\sum_{e\in\mathcal E}\pi_B(e)>0,
\qquad
\rho=\frac{M}{\Lambda\tau},
\]
then there exists a distinct positive-weight nonchargeable endpoint pair
\((e,f)\) such that
\[
\frac{\nu_T(\mathcal R_e\cap\mathcal R_f\cap H)}{\tau}
\ge
c\rho^2
\]
and
\[
\mathcal E\cap I_B(e,f)\subseteq\{e,f\}.
\]

Conversely, if \(\delta>0\) and
\[
\Delta\ge\delta\frac{M^2}{\tau},
\]
then
\[
M\le\frac{P_0(B)\tau}{\delta}
\le
\frac{e^{-Q(B)}}{\delta}.
\]
Thus diagonal-heavy energy at fixed positive high-window mass can occur only
at the empty-atom scale.

## Proof

For every \(e\subseteq B\),
\[
\pi_B(e)
=
P_0(B)\prod_{b\in e}\frac{q_b}{1-q_b}.
\]
Because \(0<q_b\le1/2\), each factor satisfies
\[
\frac{q_b}{1-q_b}\le1.
\]
Hence
\[
\pi_B(e)\le P_0(B)
\]
for all \(e\subseteq B\).  Therefore
\[
\Delta
=
\sum_{e\in\mathcal E}\pi_B(e)^2\,\nu_T(\mathcal R_e\cap H)
\le
P_0(B)
\sum_{e\in\mathcal E}\pi_B(e)\,\nu_T(\mathcal R_e\cap H)
=
P_0(B)M.
\]
Also,
\[
\log P_0(B)
=
\sum_{b\in B}\log(1-q_b)
\le
-\sum_{b\in B}q_b
=
-Q(B),
\]
so
\[
P_0(B)\le e^{-Q(B)}.
\]
This proves
\[
\Delta\le P_0(B)M\le e^{-Q(B)}M.
\]

If \(M>0\), division by \(M^2/\tau\) gives
\[
\frac{\Delta}{M^2/\tau}
\le
\frac{P_0(B)M}{M^2/\tau}
=
\frac{P_0(B)\tau}{M}
\le
\frac{e^{-Q(B)}\tau}{M}
\le
\frac{e^{-Q(B)}}{M},
\]
because \(\tau\le1\).

For the combined branch, use the total-energy lower bound and decomposition:
\[
\frac{M^2}{\tau}
\le
\Omega_{\mathrm{tot}}
=
\Omega_{\mathcal C}+\Delta+\Omega_{\mathcal N}.
\]
Together with
\[
\Omega_{\mathcal C}\le\gamma\frac{M^2}{\tau}
\]
and
\[
\Delta
\le
\frac{P_0(B)\tau}{M}\frac{M^2}{\tau},
\]
this gives
\[
\Omega_{\mathcal N}
\ge
\left(1-\gamma-\frac{P_0(B)\tau}{M}\right)\frac{M^2}{\tau}
=
c\frac{M^2}{\tau}.
\]

Let \(\mathcal N\) be the distinct nonchargeable ordered-pair set and write
\[
b_{ef}
=
\frac{\nu_T(\mathcal R_e\cap\mathcal R_f\cap H)}{\tau}.
\]
Then
\[
\Omega_{\mathcal N}
=
\tau
\sum_{(e,f)\in\mathcal N}
\pi_B(e)\pi_B(f)b_{ef}.
\]
Since
\[
\sum_{(e,f)\in\mathcal N}\pi_B(e)\pi_B(f)\le\Lambda^2
\]
and
\[
c\frac{M^2}{\tau}
=
c\rho^2\Lambda^2\tau,
\]
weighted averaging gives a distinct nonchargeable pair \((e,f)\) with
\(\pi_B(e)\pi_B(f)>0\) and
\[
b_{ef}\ge c\rho^2.
\]
By nonchargeability,
\[
\mathcal E\cap I_B(e,f)\subseteq\{e,f\}.
\]

Finally, suppose
\[
\Delta\ge\delta\frac{M^2}{\tau}.
\]
If \(M=0\), then \(\Delta=0\) and the displayed conclusion is trivial.  If
\(M>0\), combine the lower bound with \(\Delta\le P_0(B)M\):
\[
\delta\frac{M^2}{\tau}
\le
P_0(B)M.
\]
Dividing by \(M\) gives
\[
M\le\frac{P_0(B)\tau}{\delta}
\le
\frac{e^{-Q(B)}}{\delta}.
\]

## Used by

- Future mass-quarantine arguments: if the desired high-window mass \(M\) is
  substantially larger than \(P_0(B)\), then diagonal energy cannot carry a
  positive share of the overlap budget.
- Future support-mass arguments: this removes the need to separately control
  \(\Lambda\rho\), since \(\Lambda\rho=M/\tau\).
- Future nonchargeable-pair reductions: if chargeable energy is also small and
  \(M\gg P_0(B)\tau\), the distinct nonchargeable branch is forced.

## Notes

- This is not terminal Erdos 536 evidence.  It does not prove that \(M\) is
  bounded below in the active model.
- The estimate uses only the endpoint product law and the maximum-atom bound;
  no product structure on \(T\) is needed.
- The assumption \(q_b\le1/2\) is essential for \(P_0(B)\) to be a maximum
  endpoint atom.  Without it, replace \(P_0(B)\) by
  \(\prod_b\max\{q_b,1-q_b\}\).
- Pair-link-freeness and interval structure enter only through the inherited
  combined branch, not through the diagonal bound \(\Delta\le P_0(B)M\).
