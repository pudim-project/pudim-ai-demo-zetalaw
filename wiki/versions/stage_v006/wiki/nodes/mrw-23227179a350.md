# Corollary: Zero-Xi exact occupancy alternatives are endpoint-fiber towers

## Statement

Let
\[
P=Z\sqcup X\sqcup Y
\]
be a finite disjoint decomposition with \(X,Y\ne\varnothing\).  Let
\(\mathcal A\subseteq2^P\) satisfy the exact two-class occupancy condition
\[
|A\cap X|=|A\cap Y|=1
\qquad(A\in\mathcal A).
\tag{1}
\]
Set
\[
C_0=X,\qquad C_1=Y.
\tag{2}
\]
Then
\[
w(C_0\cap C_1)=0
\tag{3}
\]
for every weight \(w\), so this exact two-class model has zero pointwise
mixed incidence in the sense isolated by [[mrw-7f0eb8d1648c]].

Moreover \(\mathcal A\) has a unique endpoint-fiber representation
\[
\mathcal A
=
\{R\cup\{x,y\}:x\in X,\ y\in Y,\ R\in\mathcal R_{xy}\},
\tag{4}
\]
where
\[
\mathcal R_{xy}
=
\{A\cap Z:A\in\mathcal A,\ A\cap X=\{x\},\ A\cap Y=\{y\}\}
\subseteq2^Z.
\tag{5}
\]
Consequently, by [[mrw-d7b3299d3813]], \(\mathcal A\) is pair-link-free in
\(2^P\) if and only if every fiber \(\mathcal R_{xy}\) is pair-link-free in
\(2^Z\).  For every product law \(\nu_P\), its mass and high-support mass
decompose by endpoint pair exactly as in [[mrw-d7b3299d3813]].

More generally, let
\[
P=P_0\supseteq P_1\supseteq\cdots\supseteq P_r,\qquad
P_{j-1}=P_j\sqcup X_j\sqcup Y_j,\qquad X_j,Y_j\ne\varnothing
\tag{6}
\]
be a finite endpoint tower with mutually disjoint endpoint classes, and let
\(\mathcal A\subseteq2^P\) satisfy
\[
|A\cap X_j|=|A\cap Y_j|=1
\qquad(A\in\mathcal A,\ 1\le j\le r).
\tag{7}
\]
Then \(\mathcal A\) has a unique terminal-fiber tower representation
\[
\mathcal A
=
\{R\cup E(\mathbf e):
\mathbf e\in\prod_{j=1}^r(X_j\times Y_j),\
R\in\mathcal R_{\mathbf e}\},
\tag{8}
\]
where, for
\(\mathbf e=((x_1,y_1),\ldots,(x_r,y_r))\),
\[
E(\mathbf e)=\{x_1,y_1,\ldots,x_r,y_r\}.
\tag{9}
\]
The terminal fibers satisfy \(\mathcal R_{\mathbf e}\subseteq2^{P_r}\).  For each
level \(j\), the associated endpoint point supports
\[
C_{j,0}=X_j,\qquad C_{j,1}=Y_j
\tag{10}
\]
are disjoint, hence have zero pointwise mixed incidence.  By
[[mrw-b52df00c958c]], \(\mathcal A\) is pair-link-free if and only if every
terminal fiber \(\mathcal R_{\mathbf e}\) is pair-link-free, and the optimal
high-support mass inside this exact tower class is
\[
\Gamma_r\,\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r)
\tag{11}
\]
for threshold \(L\), where \(\Gamma_r\) and \(\mathfrak M_{P_r}\) are the
endpoint probability product and terminal residual value defined in
[[mrw-b52df00c958c]].

Thus a global theorem forcing positive pointwise mixed incidence cannot hold
on the whole exact one-from-each endpoint-fiber or endpoint-tower class.  It
must either assume that positive mass leaves exact occupancy, prove a
terminal-core residual estimate after the reduction (10), or charge only the
escaped mass to the defect mechanism of [[mrw-7f0eb8d1648c]].

## Proof

For the two-class case, (3) follows immediately from the disjoint
decomposition \(P=Z\sqcup X\sqcup Y\).  For each endpoint pair \((x,y)\), the
definition (5) collects exactly the \(Z\)-sections of members of
\(\mathcal A\) whose endpoint section is \(\{x,y\}\).  Because every
\(A\in\mathcal A\) satisfies (1), it has a unique endpoint pair
\((x,y)\in X\times Y\) and can be written uniquely as
\[
A=(A\cap Z)\cup\{x,y\}.
\]
This proves the representation (4), and uniqueness of the fibers is forced by
(5).

The pair-link criterion and the product-measure identities in the two-class
case are exactly [[mrw-d7b3299d3813]] applied to the uniquely recovered
fibers \(\mathcal R_{xy}\).

For the iterated tower, condition (7) gives every member \(A\in\mathcal A\) a
unique endpoint transcript
\[
\mathbf e(A)=((x_1,y_1),\ldots,(x_r,y_r))
\in\prod_{j=1}^r(X_j\times Y_j),
\]
where \(A\cap X_j=\{x_j\}\) and \(A\cap Y_j=\{y_j\}\).  Define
\[
\mathcal R_{\mathbf e}
=
\{A\cap P_r:A\in\mathcal A,\ \mathbf e(A)=\mathbf e\}.
\]
Then every \(A\in\mathcal A\) has the unique form
\[
A=(A\cap P_r)\cup E(\mathbf e(A)),
\]
which proves (8).  The supports \(C_{j,0}=X_j\) and \(C_{j,1}=Y_j\) are
disjoint by (6), proving zero pointwise mixed incidence at every endpoint
level.  The pair-link criterion and high-support value are then exactly
[[mrw-b52df00c958c]] applied to these terminal fibers.

The final strategic conclusion is the contrapositive reading of the proved
classification: exact one-from-each endpoint occupancy is a zero-incidence
model that survives the full pair-link interval precisely by terminal-fiber
decoupling.  Therefore a future positive-\(\Xi\) theorem must exclude this
exact model, measure the mass outside it, or continue the residual problem on
the terminal core.

## Notes

- This corollary is a conditional classification of the zero-\(\Xi\) branch:
  zero pointwise mixed incidence plus exact one-from-each occupancy is not a
  new object; it is exactly the endpoint-fiber/tower model already audited by
  [[mrw-d7b3299d3813]] and [[mrw-b52df00c958c]].
- The result does not say that \(\Xi=0\) alone forces exact occupancy.  A
  future global proof must still handle sets with zero, two, or more points in
  one of the two supports, or prove that such deviations pay defect or lose
  high-support mass.
- This is why the next target should be an occupancy-forcing or
  occupancy-defect theorem: positive high-support mass must either create
  pointwise mixed incidence, leave exact one-from-each occupancy, or reduce to
  the terminal residual in (11).
- No \(M_{P_k}(\theta)\), \(U_k(\theta)\), or \(R_P(\theta)\) theorem follows
  from this node.
