# Note: Escaping first-hit mass obstruction for finite-shadow decrement control

## Statement

Fixed individual logarithmic density data does not by itself imply continuity from above for logarithmic density.  More precisely, for every \(0<\alpha<1\), there are pairwise disjoint sets \(S_j\subseteq\mathbb N\) such that every \(S_j\) has logarithmic density \(0\), but
\[
\limsup_{x\to\infty}
\mu_x\Bigl(\bigcup_{j>N}S_j\Bigr)\ge\alpha
\qquad\text{for every }N.
\]
Consequently, in the Erdos #25 route, the finite-shadow decrement list \(h_i=\delta_{i-1}-\delta_i\) cannot be the only input to a proof of tail continuity.  A proof must also use uniformity, scale localization, residue-overlap structure, or another mechanism that rules out escaping first-hit mass.

This is not a counterexample to Erdos Problem #25.  It is a route obstruction to arguments that try to pass from fixed first-hit density limits alone to
\[
\lim_{N\to\infty}\limsup_{x\to\infty}\mu_x(B_N\setminus B)=0.
\]

## Proof

Fix \(0<\alpha<1\), and choose \(A>1\) with
\[
\frac{A-1}{A}\ge\alpha.
\]
Choose integers \(Y_j\to\infty\) so fast that
\[
Y_{j+1}>Y_j^A.
\]
Define
\[
S_j=\{m\in\mathbb N:\ Y_j\le m<Y_j^A\}.
\]
The sets \(S_j\) are pairwise disjoint and finite, so each \(S_j\) has logarithmic density \(0\).

However, for \(x=Y_j^A\),
\[
\mu_x(S_j)
=
\frac{1}{A\log Y_j}
\sum_{Y_j\le m<Y_j^A}\frac1m
=
\frac{A-1}{A}+o(1)
\]
as \(j\to\infty\).  For any fixed \(N\), taking \(j>N\) and passing along the subsequence \(x=Y_j^A\) gives
\[
\limsup_{x\to\infty}
\mu_x\Bigl(\bigcup_{k>N}S_k\Bigr)
\ge
\frac{A-1}{A}
\ge
\alpha.
\]
This proves the obstruction.

The scope matters.  In the actual residue first-hit setting, [[wiki/nodes/mrw-171478aeed08|Essential-index tail criterion for Erdos residue-class logarithmic density]] proves \(h_i=0\Rightarrow H_i=\varnothing\), so the sets \(S_j\) are not being asserted as possible \(H_i\).  The conclusion is instead that the scalar fixed-index limits \(h_i\), even when summable by telescoping, do not carry the uniformity needed to control moving-index mass.

## Notes

- This note quarantines h-only decrement chasing.  Future progress should prove a residue-specific block-uniform estimate or construct a residue-specific obstruction.
- The note does not change the open status of Erdos Problem #25.
- The CRT prefix-dispersion certificate identifies two finite defects, \(P_I\) and \(R_I\), that must be controlled in place of h-only data.
- The small-residue obstruction shows that \(R_I\) itself may be a threshold artifact; future certificate work should use the threshold-aware \(Q_I\) defect or actual first-hit mass.
- The activation-scale \(Q_I\) bound gives one scale-localized sufficient condition, but it is too crude when \(L_B/(n_B\log n_B)\) is large.  Future work must add overlap, entropy, or a true residue-specific obstruction.
- The first-cycle entropy bound gives the best possible estimate using only \(n_B\) and \(|D_I|\).  It does not remove the need for residue-overlap structure.
