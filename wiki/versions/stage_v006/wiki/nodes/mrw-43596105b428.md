# Definition: Riemann zeta probability law

## Statement

For \(\beta>1\), the Riemann zeta probability law on \(\mathbb N\) is
\[
\rho_\beta(n)=\frac{n^{-\beta}}{\zeta(\beta)},\qquad n\in\mathbb N.
\]
Equivalently, it is the Gibbs law
\[
\rho_\beta(n)=\frac{e^{-\beta E(n)}}{Z(\beta)},\qquad E(n)=\log n,\qquad Z(\beta)=\zeta(\beta).
\]

## Proof

This is a definition. Normalization follows from \(Z(\beta)=\sum_{n\ge 1}n^{-\beta}=\zeta(\beta)\) for \(\beta>1\).
