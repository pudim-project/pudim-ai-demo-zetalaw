# Theorem: Exact inverse-tail floor formula at s=8

## Statement

For \(n\ge1\), put
\[
T_8(n)=\zeta_n(8)=\sum_{k=n}^{\infty}\frac1{k^8}.
\]
Define
\[
\begin{aligned}
A_8(n)
={}&7n^7-\frac{49}{2}n^6+\frac{637}{12}n^5-\frac{1715}{24}n^4
+\frac{7399}{144}n^3-\frac{1715}{96}n^2+\frac{69685}{1728}n-\frac{65611}{3456}\\
&-\frac{23764069}{103680n}
-\frac{23764069}{207360n^2}
+\frac{2102131409}{1244160n^3}
+\frac{2149659547}{829440n^4}\\
&-\frac{203290800013}{14929920n^5}
-\frac{1146003910541}{29859840n^6}
+\frac{21587525169497}{179159040n^7}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Then, for every \(n\ge6\),
\[
\left\lfloor T_8(n)^{-1}\right\rfloor=\left\lfloor A_8(n)\right\rfloor.
\]
For \(1\le n\le5\), the exact values are
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrr}
n&1&2&3&4&5\\
\hline
\lfloor T_8(n)^{-1}\rfloor&0&245&5844&53503&291407.
\end{array}
\]

## Proof

Let
\[
B_8(n)=A_8(n)+\frac{200335112892761}{358318080n^8}.
\]
For \(C=A_8\) or \(B_8\), write \(R_C(k)=1/C(k)\).  Direct expansion gives two rational telescoping inequalities:
\[
R_{A_8}(k)-R_{A_8}(k+1)-\frac1{k^8}>0\qquad(k\ge3)
\]
and
\[
\frac1{k^8}-\left(R_{B_8}(k)-R_{B_8}(k+1)\right)>0\qquad(k\ge1).
\]
The exact integer certificate is recorded in `calculations/verify_s8_inverse_tail.py`: after clearing denominators, the first numerator becomes a polynomial in \(k-3\) with all coefficients positive, and the second becomes a polynomial in \(k-1\) with all coefficients positive.

Summing the first inequality over \(k\ge n\) gives
\[
T_8(n)<R_{A_8}(n)=\frac1{A_8(n)}
\]
for \(n\ge3\), hence \(A_8(n)<T_8(n)^{-1}\).  Summing the second gives
\[
T_8(n)>R_{B_8}(n)=\frac1{B_8(n)}
\]
for \(n\ge1\), hence \(T_8(n)^{-1}<B_8(n)\).  Therefore, for \(n\ge6\),
\[
A_8(n)<T_8(n)^{-1}<B_8(n).
\]

It remains to check that the interval \((A_8(n),B_8(n))\) does not cross an integer.  The same certificate verifies
\[
\lfloor A_8(n)\rfloor=\lfloor B_8(n)\rfloor
\]
for every \(6\le n\le10^6\) by exact integer arithmetic.  For \(n>10^6\), let \(P_8(n)\) be the polynomial part of \(A_8(n)\), through the constant term.  The numerator of \(P_8(n)\) modulo \(3456\) runs through the odd residue classes, so \(P_8(n)\) is at distance at least \(1/3456\) from the next integer.  The tail correction \(A_8(n)-P_8(n)\) is negative and has absolute value \(<1/3456\), while \(B_8(n)-A_8(n)<1/3456\).  Hence \(B_8(n)\) remains below the next integer after \(A_8(n)\), proving the floor equality for all \(n\ge6\).

For \(1\le n\le5\), the displayed values are certified by exact rational partial sums.  With cutoff \(N=300\),
\[
\sum_{k=n}^{N}\frac1{k^8}>\frac1{M_n+1}
\]
and
\[
\sum_{k=n}^{N}\frac1{k^8}+\frac1{7N^7}<\frac1{M_n}
\]
for the listed \(M_n\) and \(2\le n\le5\); the monotone integral bound
\[
\sum_{k=N+1}^{\infty}\frac1{k^8}<\int_N^\infty x^{-8}\,dx=\frac1{7N^7}
\]
then gives \(M_n<T_8(n)^{-1}<M_n+1\).  For \(n=1\), \(T_8(1)>1\), so the floor is \(0\).
