# Proposition: Endpoint occupancy patterns reduce defect mass to terminal core residuals

## Statement

Let
\[
P=P_0\supseteq P_1\supseteq\cdots\supseteq P_r
\]
be a finite endpoint tower, with
\[
P_{j-1}=P_j\sqcup X_j\sqcup Y_j,\qquad X_j,Y_j\ne\varnothing
\qquad(1\le j\le r).
\tag{1}
\]
Let \(\nu_P\) be a product law on \(2^P\) with coordinate probabilities
\(q_p\in(0,1)\), and let \(\nu_{P_r}\) be its restriction to \(P_r\).  Put
\[
\mathcal B=P\setminus P_r=\bigsqcup_{j=1}^r(X_j\sqcup Y_j).
\tag{2}
\]
An endpoint occupancy pattern is an element
\[
\omega=((A_1,B_1),\ldots,(A_r,B_r))
\in
\Omega:=\prod_{j=1}^r(2^{X_j}\times2^{Y_j}).
\tag{3}
\]
Write
\[
E(\omega)=\bigcup_{j=1}^r(A_j\cup B_j)\subseteq \mathcal B
\tag{4}
\]
and
\[
\pi(\omega)
=
\prod_{p\in E(\omega)}q_p
\prod_{p\in \mathcal B\setminus E(\omega)}(1-q_p).
\tag{5}
\]
Let
\[
\Omega_{\mathrm{ex}}
=
\{\omega\in\Omega: |A_j|=|B_j|=1\text{ for every }j\},
\qquad
\Omega_{\mathrm{def}}=\Omega\setminus\Omega_{\mathrm{ex}}.
\tag{6}
\]

For a pair-link-free family \(\mathcal F\subseteq2^P\), define the terminal
fiber over \(\omega\) by
\[
\mathcal F_\omega
=
\{R\subseteq P_r:R\cup E(\omega)\in\mathcal F\}.
\tag{7}
\]
Then each \(\mathcal F_\omega\) is pair-link-free in \(2^{P_r}\), and for
every real threshold \(L\),
\[
\nu_P(\mathcal F\cap\{S:|S|>L\})
=
\sum_{\omega\in\Omega}
\pi(\omega)\,
\nu_{P_r}\bigl(\mathcal F_\omega\cap\{R:|R|>L-|E(\omega)|\}\bigr).
\tag{8}
\]
Consequently,
\[
\nu_P(\mathcal F\cap\{S:|S|>L\})
\le
\sum_{\omega\in\Omega}
\pi(\omega)\,\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-|E(\omega)|),
\tag{9}
\]
where \(\mathfrak M_{P_r}\) is the terminal pair-link-free residual value of
[[mrw-b52df00c958c]].

Moreover, if \(D_{\mathrm{tw}}\) is the occupancy-defect event of
[[mrw-640f82d14b4e]], then
\[
\nu_P(\mathcal F\cap D_{\mathrm{tw}}\cap\{S:|S|>L\})
\le
\mathcal R_{\mathrm{def}}(L),
\tag{10}
\]
where
\[
\mathcal R_{\mathrm{def}}(L)
=
\sum_{\omega\in\Omega_{\mathrm{def}}}
\pi(\omega)\,\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-|E(\omega)|).
\tag{11}
\]
Equivalently, if
\[
\Pi_m^{\mathrm{def}}
=
\sum_{\substack{\omega\in\Omega_{\mathrm{def}}\\ |E(\omega)|=m}}\pi(\omega),
\tag{12}
\]
then
\[
\mathcal R_{\mathrm{def}}(L)
=
\sum_{m=0}^{|\mathcal B|}
\Pi_m^{\mathrm{def}}\,\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-m).
\tag{13}
\]
In particular, since
\[
\mathfrak M_{P_r}(t)\le\nu_{P_r}(\{R:|R|>t\}),
\tag{14}
\]
one has the ambient tail bound
\[
\mathcal R_{\mathrm{def}}(L)
\le
\nu_P(D_{\mathrm{tw}}\cap\{S:|S|>L\}).
\tag{15}
\]
The exact-occupancy part of (9) is precisely the residual from
[[mrw-b52df00c958c]]:
\[
\sum_{\omega\in\Omega_{\mathrm{ex}}}
\pi(\omega)\,\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-|E(\omega)|)
=
\Gamma_r\,\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r).
\tag{16}
\]
Thus
\[
\nu_P(\mathcal F\cap\{S:|S|>L\})
\le
\Gamma_r\,\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r)
+
\mathcal R_{\mathrm{def}}(L).
\tag{17}
\]
In particular, any excess
\[
\nu_P(\mathcal F\cap\{S:|S|>L\})
\ge
\Gamma_r\,\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r)+\delta
\tag{18}
\]
forces the endpoint-pattern defect residual budget
\[
\mathcal R_{\mathrm{def}}(L)\ge\delta.
\tag{19}
\]

For the prime-biased law \(\nu_P(p\in S)=1/p\), the same formulas apply to
\[
H_{P,\theta}=\{S\subseteq P:|S|>\theta S_P\},
\qquad
S_P=\sum_{p\in P}\frac1p,
\tag{20}
\]
by substituting \(L=\theta S_P\).

## Proof

Fix \(\omega\in\Omega\).  Suppose, toward a contradiction, that
\(\mathcal F_\omega\) is not pair-link-free in \(2^{P_r}\).  Then there are
pairwise distinct \(R_1,R_2,R_3\in\mathcal F_\omega\) such that
\[
R_3\in I_{P_r}(R_1,R_2).
\tag{21}
\]
Adjoining a fixed endpoint set is the common-coordinate stability of the
pair-link interval.  Set
\[
S_i=R_i\cup E(\omega)\qquad(i=1,2,3).
\tag{22}
\]
The sets \(S_i\) are pairwise distinct members of \(\mathcal F\).  Since the
endpoint part \(E(\omega)\) is common to all three,
\[
S_1\triangle S_2=R_1\triangle R_2
\tag{23}
\]
and
\[
S_1\cup S_2=E(\omega)\cup R_1\cup R_2.
\tag{24}
\]
Therefore (21) implies
\[
S_3\in I_P(S_1,S_2),
\tag{25}
\]
contradicting pair-link-freeness of \(\mathcal F\).  Hence every
\(\mathcal F_\omega\) is pair-link-free.

The endpoint events
\[
\{S\subseteq P:S\cap \mathcal B=E(\omega)\}
\qquad(\omega\in\Omega)
\tag{26}
\]
are disjoint and cover \(2^P\).  On the event (26), the endpoint-section
probability is \(\pi(\omega)\), independent of the terminal \(P_r\)-section,
which is distributed according to \(\nu_{P_r}\).  A member
\[
S=R\cup E(\omega)
\tag{27}
\]
satisfies \(|S|>L\) exactly when
\[
|R|>L-|E(\omega)|.
\tag{28}
\]
Summing over the disjoint endpoint patterns gives the identity (8).

Since each \(\mathcal F_\omega\) is pair-link-free, the truncated fiber in
(8) is pair-link-free and lies inside
\[
\{R\subseteq P_r:|R|>L-|E(\omega)|\}.
\]
By the definition of \(\mathfrak M_{P_r}\), its measure is at most
\[
\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-|E(\omega)|),
\]
which proves (9).  Restricting the same sum to
\(\Omega_{\mathrm{def}}\) proves (10) and (11).

Formula (13) is just (11) regrouped by the integer \(m=|E(\omega)|\).  The
ambient tail estimate (15) follows from (14), because after replacing every
\(\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-|E(\omega)|)\) in (11) by the full terminal tail
\(\nu_{P_r}(\{R:|R|>L-|E(\omega)|\})\), the product-measure decomposition
over \(\Omega_{\mathrm{def}}\) is exactly
\(\nu_P(D_{\mathrm{tw}}\cap\{S:|S|>L\})\).

For \(\omega\in\Omega_{\mathrm{ex}}\), one has \(|E(\omega)|=2r\).  Also
\[
\sum_{\omega\in\Omega_{\mathrm{ex}}}\pi(\omega)
=
\prod_{j=1}^r
\left(
\sum_{x\in X_j}q_x\prod_{u\in X_j\setminus\{x\}}(1-q_u)
\right)
\left(
\sum_{y\in Y_j}q_y\prod_{v\in Y_j\setminus\{y\}}(1-q_v)
\right)
=
\Gamma_r.
\tag{29}
\]
This proves (16).  Combining the exact and defect parts of (9) gives (17),
and (19) is the rearrangement of (17) under the lower bound (18).  The
prime-biased specialization is the substitution \(L=\theta S_P\).

## Notes

- This proposition replaces the unknown defect mass in [[mrw-640f82d14b4e]]
  by an explicit endpoint-pattern residual budget on the same terminal core
  \(P_r\), with the cutoff shifted by the number of selected endpoint
  coordinates.
- The grouped form (13) separates endpoint-pattern probability from terminal
  residual strength.  The ambient estimate (15) is only a tail benchmark: the
  useful content is that \(\mathfrak M_{P_r}\) may be much smaller than the
  unrestricted terminal tail if the terminal pair-link-free problem is
  nontrivial.
- The estimate is intentionally one-sided and may be loose: it ignores
  pair-link constraints between different endpoint occupancy patterns.  Any
  future improvement must use cross-pattern interactions, a better terminal
  residual theorem, or a mixed-incidence defect mechanism.
- The exact endpoint-tower branch is recovered from the special patterns in
  \(\Omega_{\mathrm{ex}}\).  All non-exact patterns are now isolated in
  \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{def}}(L)\).
- This is not a proof of \(M_{P_k}(\theta)\to0\), \(U_k(\theta)\to0\), or an
  \(R_P(\theta)\) lift.  It is a terminal-core residual reduction for the
  occupancy-defect slice.
