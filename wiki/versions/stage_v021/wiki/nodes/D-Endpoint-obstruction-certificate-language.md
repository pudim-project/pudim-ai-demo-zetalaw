# Definition: Endpoint and pointwise obstruction certificates

## Statement

A pointwise obstruction certificate is an explicit admissible parameter value at which a proposed universal sign, monotonicity, convexity, or complete-monotonicity claim fails after reduction to a pointwise inequality. An endpoint obstruction is the same kind of certificate obtained from a boundary or asymptotic regime of the source domain.
