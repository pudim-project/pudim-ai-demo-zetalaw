# Theorem: Baskakov alpha one even line inverse Laplace density has nonnegative Fejer type factorization

## Statement

For every integer \(m\ge2\), the inverse-Laplace density of \(f^{[2m]}_1\) can be written as \(e^{-t/2}\) times a nonnegative Fejer-type sine-square or positive-kernel trigonometric factor.
