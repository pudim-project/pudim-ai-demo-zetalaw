# Theorem: Erdos 536 LCM-triangle-free maximum density open problem

## Statement

Let \(f(N)\) be the largest size of an LCM-triangle-free subset of \([N]\), where an LCM-triangle is a triple of distinct integers \(a,b,c\) with \([a,b]=[a,c]=[b,c]\). Erdos Problem 536 asks whether \(f(N)=o(N)\).
