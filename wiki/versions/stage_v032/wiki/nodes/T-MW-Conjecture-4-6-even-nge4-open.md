# Theorem: Ma Weigert Conjecture 4.6 even degrees n at least 4 remain open

## Statement

The even-degree part of Ma--Weigert Conjecture 4.6 remains open after odd-degree deflation; in particular, the first nondeflated case is to prove or refute \(D_k^{(4)}\supseteq D_{k+1}^{(4)}\) for all \(k\ge0\) in the one-variable log-function family.
