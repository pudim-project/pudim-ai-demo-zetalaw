# Theorem: T-Tao-Sendov-boundary-layer-q1-large-root-defect

## Statement

For fixed 0<r<1 in the tangent-cluster quadratic, with q=1-lambda/d and (1+lambda)/d sufficiently small, the large root y_L satisfies 1-|y_L| = kappa_r lambda/d + O_r((1+lambda)^2/d^2), where kappa_r=(1-r)(2+r)/2.

## Scope

- Fixed-r tangent-cluster quadratic asymptotic; the formal lambda=0 endpoint has |y_L|=1.
