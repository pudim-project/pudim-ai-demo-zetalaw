## Definition

A Mellin-Planck partition function is a Mellin moment \(M(s)=\int_0^\infty t^s\,d\mu(t)\) of a positive measure, on an interval where the integral is finite. The tilted law \(\nu_s\) has density proportional to \(t^s\,d\mu(t)\), so logarithmic derivatives of \(M\) are cumulants of \(\log t\) under \(\nu_s\).
