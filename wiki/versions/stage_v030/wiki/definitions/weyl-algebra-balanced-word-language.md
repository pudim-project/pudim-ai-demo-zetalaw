## Definition

The real Weyl algebra \(A_1\) is generated by \(X\) and \(D\) with relation \(DX-XD=1\). A balanced word of degree \((n,n)\) is a word containing exactly \(n\) copies of \(D\) and \(n\) copies of \(X\). The coefficient \(\ell^1\)-norm of a finite word expansion \(\sum_w a_w w\) is \(\sum_w |a_w|\).
