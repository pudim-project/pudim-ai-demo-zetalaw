# Theorem: Tao-Sendov product far-zero alternative

## Statement

If Sendov fails at a simple zero a of a degree n polynomial with all zeros in the closed unit disk, then for every 1<R<2 the number of other zeros at distance at least R from a has an explicit lower bound.
\[
M_R\ge\max\{0,\lfloor(\log n-(n-1)\log R)/\log(2/R)\rfloor+1\}.
\]
