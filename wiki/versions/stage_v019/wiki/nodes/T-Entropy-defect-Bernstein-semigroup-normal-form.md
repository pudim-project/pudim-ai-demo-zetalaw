# Theorem: Yu entropy defect CM equivalent to positive Laplace transform normal form

## Statement

For Yu-type entropy-defect problems, complete monotonicity of a standardized Gaussian entropy defect is equivalent to a positive Laplace-transform representation in the convolution or shape parameter, giving a Bernstein--Widder normal form for future source-family attacks.
