# Theorem: Karp Sitnik Gauss beta shifted ratio complete monotonicity for general sigma remains open

## Statement

For \(b>a>0\) and \(\sigma>0\), determine whether \({}_2F_1(\sigma,a+1;b+1;-x)/{}_2F_1(\sigma,a;b;-x)\) is completely monotone, at least for \(0<\sigma\le1\).
