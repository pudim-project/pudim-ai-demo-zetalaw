# Theorem: atanh log outside cover plus compact root bracket proves terminal endpoint

## Statement

There is a finite rational cover of \((0,1)\setminus[1409/5000,293/1000]\) and the compact-root bracket \(J=[287345/1000000,287346/1000000]\) such that exact Hurwitz-zeta bounds and the true atanh rational logarithm-enclosure lemma prove every outside interval has \(Q_2(x)<q_J\), where \(q_J\) is a certified lower witness below \(Q_2(\xi)\) for \(\xi\in J\). Together with the one-zero compact certificate, this yields \(L_2=Q_2(\xi)\) and \(\mathcal I_2=(Q_2(\xi),3]\).
