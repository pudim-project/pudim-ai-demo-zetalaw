# Theorem: Erdos536 lower trace poor comparable sink or nonrankthin construction

## Statement

Lower-trace-poor comparable-pair sink or non-rank-thin construction: in the lower-trace-poor branch of the two-gate schema, prove a weighted comparable-pair, entropy, chain, or LYM sink forcing \(\nu_k(\mathcal F_k)\to0\) for admissible high-support non-rank-thin families, or construct an explicit positive-mass non-rank-thin lower-trace-poor family. The proof or construction must use the prime-biased product law and must not introduce defect laws, since lower traces are unavailable or negligible.
