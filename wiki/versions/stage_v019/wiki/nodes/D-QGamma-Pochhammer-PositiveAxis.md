# Definition: q-gamma and q-Pochhammer notation on the positive axis

## Statement

For \(0<q<1\), the q-Pochhammer symbols are \((a;q)_n=\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}(1-aq^j)\) and \((a;q)_\infty=\prod_{j=0}^{\infty}(1-aq^j)\). The Jackson q-gamma function on \((0,\infty)\) is \(\Gamma_q(z)=(1-q)^{1-z}(q;q)_\infty/(q^z;q)_\infty\).
