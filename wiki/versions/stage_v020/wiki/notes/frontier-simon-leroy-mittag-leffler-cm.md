# Simon Le Roy-Type Mittag-Leffler Frontier

Simon studies complete monotonicity of
\[
F^{(\gamma)}_{\alpha,\beta}(-x),\qquad
F^{(\gamma)}_{\alpha,\beta}(z)=\sum_{n\ge0}\frac{z^n}{\Gamma(\beta+\alpha n)^\gamma}.
\]

The source leaves the strip
\[
\gamma>1,\qquad \alpha\gamma<1,\qquad
\alpha<\beta<\frac{\alpha(1+\gamma)}2
\]
as the remaining open region.

The bounded Pudim v2 bridge is the moment property
\[
{\bf E}[X^s]=\Gamma(1+s)\left(\frac{\Gamma(\beta)}{\Gamma(\beta+\alpha s)}\right)^\gamma,
\qquad s>0,
\]
which yields the Laplace representation
\[
F^{(\gamma)}_{\alpha,\beta}(-x)
=\Gamma(\beta)^{-\gamma}{\bf E}[e^{-xX}].
\]

This pass should not grind individual parameter triples in the open strip. The LID/Levy-kernel necessity question remains an open frontier.
