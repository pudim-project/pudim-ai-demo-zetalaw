# Open_Problem: Heat-flow Fisher convexity D-functional negativity source gate

## Statement

Verify the exact source status and later-literature novelty for the heat-flow Fisher-information convexity problem: for an admissible smooth, strictly positive, sufficiently decaying density \(f=e^u\) on \(\mathbb R^d\), with \(H_f=-\nabla^2u\) and \(D[f]=\int_{\mathbb R^d}(|\nabla H_f|^2+2\operatorname{tr}(H_f^3))f\,dx\), determine whether the primary source explicitly asks whether \(D[f]\) can be negative, equivalently whether Fisher information can fail ordinary convexity along heat flow.
