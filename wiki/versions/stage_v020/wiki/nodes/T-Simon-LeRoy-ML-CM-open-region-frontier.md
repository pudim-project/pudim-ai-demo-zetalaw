# Theorem: Simon Le Roy Mittag-Leffler CM open strip gamma>1 alpha gamma<1 alpha<beta<alpha(1+gamma)/2

## Statement

For Simon's Le Roy-type Mittag-Leffler function \(F^{(\gamma)}_{\alpha,\beta}(z)=\sum_{n\ge0}z^n/\Gamma(\beta+\alpha n)^\gamma\), the remaining source frontier is to characterize complete monotonicity of \(x\mapsto F^{(\gamma)}_{\alpha,\beta}(-x)\) in the strip \(\gamma>1\), \(\alpha\gamma<1\), and \(\alpha<\beta<\alpha(1+\gamma)/2\).
