# Theorem: Gaussian heat-flow entropy complete monotonicity conjecture for all one-dimensional probability measures

## Statement

For every probability measure \(\mu\) on \(\mathbb R\) for which the heat-flow entropy \(H_\mu(t)=\int_{\mathbb R}(p_t*\mu)(x)\log(p_t*\mu)(x)\,dx\) is finite, one has \((-1)^mH_\mu^{(m)}(t)\ge0\) for every \(m\ge1\) and \(t>0\).
