# Theorem: Ressel two-center fixed-radii minimizer is not always collinear

## Statement

The proof route claiming that fixed-radii minimizers of the Ressel two-center defect are always collinear is false: there are fixed-radii families where a non-collinear angular configuration has strictly smaller defect than the same-radii collinear same-ray configuration.
