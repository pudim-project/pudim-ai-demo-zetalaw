# Theorem: Erdos536 moving base high multiplicity obstruction schema

## Statement

Moving-base high-multiplicity obstruction schema: after the weighted antichain sink and finite-core conditional sink, a genuine uncontrolled-multiplicity obstruction must use moving bases, positive product mass, high support, non-rank-thinness, source admissibility, and lower-trace-poor behavior while avoiding bounded antichain decompositions and finite-core shields. Constructing or excluding such a model remains open.
