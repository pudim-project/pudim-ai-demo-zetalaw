# Theorem: Erdos 536 locally unique moving star union pushforward hits occupied top code

## Statement

Top-code hitting lemma for the union push-forward: in the prime-biased setup, let \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) be a positive-mass threshold-sharp moving-star system with nonsparse close central ranks and with locally unique full star centers in the sense forced by `T-Erdos536-same-top-two-star-centers-force-fork`. For two nonnegligible incompatible center-profile classes, form the push-forward of the lower-star pair measure under \((A,B)\mapsto A\cup B\). Then this union push-forward assigns nonnegligible mass to the occupied top code \(\mathcal F_k\), unless \(\nu_k(\mathcal F_k)\to0\). Consequently the occupied-top density conclusion of `T-Erdos536-star-fiber-union-occupied-top-density` holds.
