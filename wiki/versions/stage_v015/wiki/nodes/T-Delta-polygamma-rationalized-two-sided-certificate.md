# Theorem: rationalized certificate D<A and D^5>A^6 with sharp endpoint asymptotics

## Statement

For all \(x>0\), with \(D(x)=x^4((\psi'(x))^2+\psi''(x))\) and \(A(x)=(x^2+4x+12)/(12(x+1)^2)\), one has \(D(x)<A(x)\) and \(D(x)^5>A(x)^6\), with endpoint asymptotics proving the exponents \(1\) and \(6/5\) are sharp.
