# Theorem: Erdos 536 supercritical broad maximum fibers force two scale union collision

## Statement

For every fixed \(0<\theta<1\), every positive-mass high-support union-free family with supercritical maximum-fiber antichain-cover width \(B_k(\theta)\not=o(\sqrt{S_k})\) along a subsequence has a two-scale lower-shadow collision: for infinitely many \(k\), there exist \(A,B,C\in\mathcal F_k\) with \(A\cup B=C\).
