# Theorem: Tao-Sendov root-adaptive boundary lower bound

## Statement

At the root-adaptive contour rho_*=(1+s)/2, the normalized logarithmic-derivative boundary minimum is bounded below by d(1-s)^2 over the explicit denominator.
\[
\mu_{\rho_*}\ge d(1-s)^2/[4(1+r+\rho_*)(1+\rho_*)].
\]
