# Theorem: exists nu >= 0 and u > 0 such that q^2+(2nu+1)q/u+(nu+1/2)/u^2 <= 1

## Statement

not(Let \(q_\nu(u)=I_{\nu+1}(u)/I_\nu(u)\). For every \(\nu\ge0\) and \(u>0\), \(q_\nu(u)^2+(2\nu+1)q_\nu(u)/u+(\nu+1/2)/u^2>1\).)
