# Theorem: Chen Choi Conjecture 1 Gurland ratio solved

## Statement

Chen-Choi Conjecture 1 holds: \((-1)^n(\log F(x))^{(n)}>0\) for \(x>0\) and \(n\in\mathbb N_0\), where \(F(x)=\Gamma(1/x)\Gamma(3/x)/\Gamma(2/x)^2\).
