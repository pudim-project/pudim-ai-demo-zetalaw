# Theorem: Qi Guo Open Problem 3 solved as supremum not maximum

## Statement

Qi--Guo Open Problem 3 is solved in supremum-corrected form: the requested finite maximum of \(\tau(s,t)\) does not exist, and its sharp supremum is \(a_*/(1+a_*+a_*^2)\), where \(e^{a_*}=1+a_*+a_*^2\).
