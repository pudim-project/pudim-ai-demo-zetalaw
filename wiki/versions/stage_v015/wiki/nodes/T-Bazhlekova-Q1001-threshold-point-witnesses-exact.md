# Theorem: Bazhlekova Q1001 threshold point witnesses exact rational audit

## Statement

Exact rational recurrence evaluation gives the two Bazhlekova threshold point witnesses \(Q_{1001}^{3/2,63/250}(2876)<0\) and \(Q_{1001}^{11/10,13/500}(447)<0\). Since \(1001\) is odd, these are direct odd-derivative sign obstructions at the two rational exponent pairs.
