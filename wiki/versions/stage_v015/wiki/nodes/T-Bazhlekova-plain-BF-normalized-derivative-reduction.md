# Theorem: Bazhlekova plain BF normalized derivative complete monotonicity reduction

## Statement

For the normalized Bazhlekova two-term symbol \(h(s)=s^{b/2}(1+s^{a-b})^{1/2}\), plain Bernstein status is equivalent to complete monotonicity of \(h'(s)=s^{b/2-1}(1+s^{a-b})^{-1/2}\bigl(b/2+(a/2)s^{a-b}\bigr)\). Failure of this complete monotonicity gives a finite-\(x\) complete-monotonicity obstruction for \(e^{-x h(s)}\) by the leading small-\(x\) term.
