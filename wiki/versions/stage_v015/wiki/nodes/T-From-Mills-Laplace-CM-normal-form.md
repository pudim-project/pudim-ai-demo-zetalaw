# Theorem: Mills ratio positive Laplace representation and complete monotonicity

## Statement

For \(t>0\), the standard normal Mills ratio satisfies \(r(t)=\int_0^\infty e^{-tu-u^2/2}\,du\). Hence \(r\) is completely monotone on \((0,\infty)\).
