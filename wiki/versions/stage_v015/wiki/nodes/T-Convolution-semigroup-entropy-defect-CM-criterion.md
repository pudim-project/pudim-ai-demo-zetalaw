# Theorem: positive Laplace kernel criterion proves entropy defect CM for a new convolution semigroup family

## Statement

There is a reusable sufficient criterion for a standardized convolution semigroup whose entropy defect has a positive Laplace kernel in semigroup time, and the criterion applies to at least one family beyond the gamma and inverse-Gaussian examples in Yu's source paper.
