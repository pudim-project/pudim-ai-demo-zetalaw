# Theorem: Exact l1 minimum in the Tao-Sawin balanced Weyl algebra model

## Statement

In the Weyl algebra DX-XD equals one, the least l1 coefficient norm of a balanced degree (n,n) word combination equal to one is two to the n divided by n factorial.
\[
L_n=\min\{\sum_w |a_w|:\sum_w a_w w=1,\ w\hbox{ has }n\hbox{ copies of }D\hbox{ and }n\hbox{ copies of }X\}=\frac{2^n}{n!}.
\]
