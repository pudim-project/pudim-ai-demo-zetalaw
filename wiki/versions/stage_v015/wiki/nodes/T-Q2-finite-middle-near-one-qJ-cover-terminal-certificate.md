# Theorem: finite middle and near one Q2 cover below qJ plus compact and near zero covers proves terminal endpoint

## Statement

There is a finite rational cover of the finite-middle and near-one regions of \((0,1)\setminus J\), excluding a separately handled near-zero interval, such that the atanh logarithm and Hurwitz-zeta enclosure lemmas certify \(Q_2(x)<q_J\) on every interval using the inner witness \(q_J\) from the endpoint run. Combined with a compact one-crossing certificate for \(G\) and a near-zero scaled cover, this determines \(L_2=Q_2(\xi)\) and \(\mathcal I_2=(Q_2(\xi),3]\).
