# Proposition: Absorbed terminal classes give window-averaged residuals

## Statement

Let \(C,D\) be disjoint finite coordinate sets with product law
\(\nu_{C\sqcup D}=\nu_C\otimes\nu_D\).  For a finite set \(A\), write
\[
\mathfrak M_A(T)
=
\sup\{\nu_A(\mathcal H):
\mathcal H\subseteq2^A,\ 
\mathcal H\text{ is pair-link-free},\
\mathcal H\subseteq\{S\subseteq A:|S|>T\}\}.
\]
Then for every real threshold \(T\),
\[
\mathfrak M_{C\sqcup D}(T)
\le
\mathbb E_{B\sim\nu_D}
\mathfrak M_C(T-|B|).
\]
Consequently, for every real \(b\),
\[
\mathfrak M_{C\sqcup D}(T)
\le
\mathfrak M_C(T-b)+\nu_D(\{B:|B|>b\}).
\]

In particular, let \(e=C_j\) be a collapsed same-class strict deletion in the
notation of [[mrw-3dde1053699f]], and let \(D_j\) be the opposite endpoint
class absorbed into the induced terminal core.  Then for every real \(L\),
\[
\mathfrak M_{P_r\cup D_j}(L-2r)
\le
\mathbb E_{B\sim\nu_{D_j}}
\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r-|B|)
\le
\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r-b)
+
\nu_{D_j}(\{B:|B|>b\}).
\]
Thus the collapsed-core terminal residuals in [[mrw-5df7f8135e2c]] are
ordinary terminal-core residuals averaged over the absorbed opposite endpoint
class, up to an optional product-tail window loss.

In particular, taking \(b=2\) gives
\[
\mathfrak M_{P_r\cup D_j}(L-2r)
\le
\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r-2)
+
\nu_{D_j}(\{B:|B|\ge3\}).
\]
The absorbed-class tail has the crude product-union estimate
\[
\nu_{D_j}(\{B:|B|\ge3\})
\le
\sum_{\{a,b,c\}\subseteq D_j}q_aq_bq_c
\le
\frac{Q(D_j)^3}{6},
\qquad
Q(D_j)=\sum_{a\in D_j}q_a.
\]

## Proof

Let \(\mathcal A\subseteq2^{C\sqcup D}\) be pair-link-free and contained in
\(\{S:|S|>T\}\).  For each \(B\subseteq D\), define the \(B\)-slice
\[
\mathcal A_B
=
\{A\subseteq C:A\sqcup B\in\mathcal A\}.
\]
The slice \(\mathcal A_B\) is pair-link-free in \(2^C\).  Indeed, if
\(A_1,A_2,A_3\in\mathcal A_B\) were pairwise distinct and
\[
A_3\in I_C(A_1,A_2),
\]
then adjoining the same \(B\) to all three sets would give pairwise distinct
members \(A_i\sqcup B\in\mathcal A\) with
\[
A_3\sqcup B\in I_{C\sqcup D}(A_1\sqcup B,A_2\sqcup B),
\]
contradicting pair-link-freeness of \(\mathcal A\).

Also, if \(A\in\mathcal A_B\), then
\[
|A|+|B|=|A\sqcup B|>T,
\]
so
\[
\mathcal A_B\subseteq\{A\subseteq C:|A|>T-|B|\}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\nu_C(\mathcal A_B)\le \mathfrak M_C(T-|B|).
\]
Product disintegration gives
\[
\nu_{C\sqcup D}(\mathcal A)
=
\sum_{B\subseteq D}\nu_D(B)\nu_C(\mathcal A_B)
\le
\sum_{B\subseteq D}\nu_D(B)\mathfrak M_C(T-|B|).
\]
Taking the supremum over all such \(\mathcal A\) proves
\[
\mathfrak M_{C\sqcup D}(T)
\le
\mathbb E_{B\sim\nu_D}\mathfrak M_C(T-|B|).
\]

For the deterministic window bound, split the expectation into
\(|B|\le b\) and \(|B|>b\).  Since \(T\mapsto\mathfrak M_C(T)\) is
nonincreasing and \(\mathfrak M_C(T)\le1\),
\[
\mathbb E_B\mathfrak M_C(T-|B|)
\le
\mathfrak M_C(T-b)+\nu_D(\{B:|B|>b\}).
\]

For a collapsed strict deletion \(e=C_j\), [[mrw-3dde1053699f]] identifies the
induced terminal core as \(P_r\cup D_j\).  Apply the first part with
\(C=P_r\), \(D=D_j\), and \(T=L-2r\), then apply the deterministic window
bound.  This proves the displayed collapsed-core estimates.

The \(b=2\) specialization is the same window estimate with
\(\{B:|B|>2\}=\{B:|B|\ge3\}\).  The final tail bound follows by covering the
event \(|B|\ge3\) by the union of triple-selection events
\(\{a,b,c\}\subseteq B\), then applying the product law and
\[
\sum_{\{a,b,c\}\subseteq D_j}q_aq_bq_c
\le
\frac{1}{6}\left(\sum_{a\in D_j}q_a\right)^3.
\]

## Notes

- The first two displayed inequalities are standalone consequences of product
  disintegration and common-coordinate stability of pair-link intervals.  The
  final displayed inequalities apply this standalone lemma to the collapsed
  terminal core \(P_r\cup D_j\) identified in [[mrw-3dde1053699f]], refining
  the collapsed branch appearing in [[mrw-5df7f8135e2c]].
- This proposition was suggested by the Scout response for the
  strict-deletion residual iteration loop, but the proof above is a local
  audit using only product disintegration and common-coordinate stability of
  pair-link intervals.
- It improves the collapsed-class branch of [[mrw-5df7f8135e2c]]: the
  enlarged terminal core \(P_r\cup D_j\) is not an opaque new residual object;
  it is controlled by a window average of residuals on the original terminal
  core \(P_r\).
- This is still nonterminal.  The next theorem must show that the window
  average is small in the relevant high-support regime, or else classify a
  near-extremal absorbed-class assembly.
- Focused Oracle passed the proof and recommended the dependency cleanup plus
  the \(b=2\) third-order-tail specialization; both are patched here.
