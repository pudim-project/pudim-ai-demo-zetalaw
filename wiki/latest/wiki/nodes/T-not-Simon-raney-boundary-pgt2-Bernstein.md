# Theorem: Simon Raney boundary obstruction for p greater than 2

## Statement

For Simon's Raney boundary family \(\mu_n=\frac{p}{pn+p}\binom{pn+p}{n}\), the Bernstein product property fails for every \(p>2\). In particular, the residual \(Q_p^R=L_p-R_n\) is not completely monotone.
