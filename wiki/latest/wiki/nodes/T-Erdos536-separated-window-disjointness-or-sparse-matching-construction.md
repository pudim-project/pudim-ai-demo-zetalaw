# Theorem: Erdos536 separated window disjointness or sparse matching construction

## Statement

Separated-window disjointness versus sparse-matching construction: under a fixed admissible lower-trace law \(\lambda_C\), if defect mass occupies two separated size/weight windows and no core/shield concentration from the terminating-descent node is present, then \(\lambda_C^{\otimes2}\{D\cap E=\varnothing\}\) is bounded below; otherwise construct an actual lower-trace family whose defect graph is sparse matching-like, has vanishing disjoint-pair mass, and preserves positive prime-biased mass, high support, and non-rank-thinness.
