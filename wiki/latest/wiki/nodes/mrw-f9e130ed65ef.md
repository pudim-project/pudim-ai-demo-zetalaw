# Theorem: Affirmative solution of Nantomah zeta positivity problem

## Statement

The imported PDF claims that for every \(n\in\mathbb N\),
\[
(n+2)\zeta(n+1)\zeta(n+3)
-(n+1)\zeta(n+2)^2
-\zeta(n+1)\zeta(n+2)>0.
\]

## Evidence

The PDF sets \(s=n+1\), rewrites the expression using moments of \(X(m)=1/m\) under \(\rho_s\), splits the expression into a positive linear part and a bounded quadratic correction, proves the case \(s\ge4\), and handles \(s=2,3\) by elementary zeta bounds.
