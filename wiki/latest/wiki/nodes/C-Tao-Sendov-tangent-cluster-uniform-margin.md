# Corollary: Tao-Sendov tangent-cluster uniform margin

## Statement

In the tangent-cluster normal form, the free critical point can be chosen within distance sqrt((1+r)/(m+N+1)) from r, hence within sqrt(2/3) uniformly.
\[
\min_{\zeta\ free}|\zeta-r|\le\sqrt{(1+r)/(m+N+1)}\le\sqrt{2/3}.
\]
