# Proposition: Block-uniform first-hit criterion for Erdos residue-class logarithmic density

## Statement

Use the notation of [[wiki/nodes/mrw-ba29cdf1fd30|Finite-shadow reduction for Erdos residue-class logarithmic density]].  Put
\[
B_0=\mathbb N,\qquad H_i=B_{i-1}\setminus B_i,\qquad h_i=\delta_{i-1}-\delta_i.
\]
Let
\[
0=N_0<N_1<N_2<\cdots
\]
be cutpoints, and for each \(r\ge1\) define the finite block
\[
I_r=\{N_{r-1}+1,\ldots,N_r\},
\]
the first-hit block
\[
G_r=\bigcup_{i\in I_r}H_i,
\]
and its finite-shadow decrement
\[
\eta_r=\sum_{i\in I_r}h_i=\delta_{N_{r-1}}-\delta_{N_r}.
\]
Assume that there are a constant \(C<\infty\) and nonnegative errors \(\varepsilon_r\) with
\[
\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}\varepsilon_r<\infty
\]
such that every block satisfies the uniform logarithmic mass bound
\[
\sup_{x\ge2}\mu_x(G_r)\le C\eta_r+\varepsilon_r.
\]
Then \(B\) has logarithmic density
\[
\delta=\lim_{N\to\infty}\delta_N.
\]

## Proof

The first-hit sets are disjoint and satisfy
\[
B_{N_R}\setminus B=\bigcup_{r>R}G_r.
\]
For a fixed \(x\), only finitely many terms in this union can meet \([1,x)\), because \(H_i\subseteq\{m:m\ge n_i\}\) and \(n_i\to\infty\).  Hence summing the block bounds below \(x\) is legitimate:
\[
\mu_x(B_{N_R}\setminus B)
\le
\sum_{r>R}\mu_x(G_r)
\le
C\sum_{r>R}\eta_r+\sum_{r>R}\varepsilon_r.
\]
The first sum telescopes:
\[
\sum_{r>R}\eta_r
=
\sum_{i>N_R}(\delta_{i-1}-\delta_i)
=
\delta_{N_R}-\delta.
\]
Therefore
\[
\limsup_{x\to\infty}\mu_x(B_{N_R}\setminus B)
\le
C(\delta_{N_R}-\delta)+\sum_{r>R}\varepsilon_r.
\]
The right-hand side tends to \(0\) as \(R\to\infty\).

Finally, if \(N_R\le N<N_{R+1}\), then \(B_N\subseteq B_{N_R}\), so
\[
B_N\setminus B\subseteq B_{N_R}\setminus B.
\]
Thus the tail defect tends to \(0\) for all \(N\to\infty\).  The tail-defect criterion in [[wiki/nodes/mrw-ba29cdf1fd30|Finite-shadow reduction for Erdos residue-class logarithmic density]] applies, and \(B\) has logarithmic density \(\delta\).

## Notes

- This is a sufficient criterion, not a complete solution of Erdos Problem #25.
- The condition controls the actual first-hit blocks \(G_r\), not the raw residue progressions.  It can therefore encode partial redundancy among indices with \(h_i>0\), but only when a separate uniform block estimate is available.
- The next target is to derive such block-uniform estimates from explicit residue-overlap or CRT structure, or to prove that they can fail in a residue-specific construction.
- The CRT prefix-dispersion certificate gives one finite way to verify the required uniform block estimate.
- The threshold-aware CRT certificate gives the current preferred finite verification route after the unshifted \(R_I\) defect was obstructed.
- The activation-scale \(Q_I\) bound is a sufficient verification subcase; it leaves open whether overlap or first-cycle entropy can control the activation-scale ratio in the full problem.
