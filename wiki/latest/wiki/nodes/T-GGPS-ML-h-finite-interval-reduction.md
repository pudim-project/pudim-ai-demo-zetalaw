# Theorem: GGPS h finite interval reduction to global convexity

## Statement

There is an explicit \(\varepsilon\in(0,1)\) and a certified finite-interval proof of \(h''(x)>0\) on \([\varepsilon,1]\); combined with the small-\(x\) slice and the source's convexity on \([1,\infty)\), this proves global strict convexity of \(h\).
