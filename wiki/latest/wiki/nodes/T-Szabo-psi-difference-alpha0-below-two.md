# Theorem: Szabo psi difference sharp threshold is strictly below 2

## Statement

In Szabo Open Problem 1.5, for every \(0<b-a<1\), the endpoint \(\alpha=2\) is inadmissible; hence any sharp complete-monotonicity threshold satisfies \(\alpha_0<2\).
