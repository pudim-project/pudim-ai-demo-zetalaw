# Lemma: Prime-valuation criterion for equal pairwise lcm triples

## Statement

Let \(a,b,c\) be positive integers.  Then
\[
[a,b]=[a,c]=[b,c]
\]
if and only if, for every prime \(p\), the maximum of
\[
v_p(a),\qquad v_p(b),\qquad v_p(c)
\]
is attained at least twice.

## Proof

For every prime \(p\),
\[
v_p([x,y])=\max(v_p(x),v_p(y)).
\]
Thus the equality of the three pairwise least common multiples is equivalent, prime by prime, to
\[
\max(\alpha,\beta)=\max(\alpha,\gamma)=\max(\beta,\gamma),
\]
where \(\alpha=v_p(a)\), \(\beta=v_p(b)\), and \(\gamma=v_p(c)\).

If the largest of \(\alpha,\beta,\gamma\) is attained at least twice, then each pair contains a largest entry, so all three pairwise maxima are equal to that common largest value.  Conversely, if the largest value is attained uniquely, say \(\alpha>\beta,\gamma\), then
\[
\max(\alpha,\beta)=\max(\alpha,\gamma)=\alpha
\]
but
\[
\max(\beta,\gamma)<\alpha,
\]
so the three pairwise maxima cannot be equal.  This proves the criterion for each prime, hence for the integers.
