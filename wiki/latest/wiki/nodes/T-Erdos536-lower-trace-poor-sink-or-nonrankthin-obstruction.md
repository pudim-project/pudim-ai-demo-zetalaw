# Theorem: Erdos536 lower trace poor sink or nonrankthin obstruction

## Statement

Lower-trace-poor sink/obstruction theorem: for fixed \(0<\theta<1\) and \(\eta>0\), every admissible positive-mass high-support family \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) in the lower-trace-poor branch either has vanishing \(\nu_k\)-mass by an entropy/LYM/comparable-pair sink estimate, or yields an explicit positive-mass non-rank-thin obstruction to the current Erdos536 lower-trace route. The obstruction must have few internal comparable pairs and must not be merely an exact-rank or already rank-thin antichain.
