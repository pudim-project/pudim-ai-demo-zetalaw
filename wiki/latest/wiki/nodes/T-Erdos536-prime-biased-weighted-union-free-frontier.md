# Theorem: Erdos 536 prime biased high support union free theorem frontier

## Statement

For \(P_k=\{p_1,\ldots,p_k\}\), the product law \(\nu_k(p_i\in S)=1/p_i\), and \(H_{k,\theta}=\{S\subseteq P_k: |S|>\theta\sum_{i\le k}1/p_i\}\), every union-free family \(\mathcal F\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) has \(\nu_k(\mathcal F)\to0\) for each fixed \(0\le\theta<1\).
