# Proposition: Upper mixed-shadow exclusion is sectionwise top-union-free

## Statement
Let \(A\subseteq T\), let \(\mathcal V\subseteq2^T\), and for
\(D\subseteq T\setminus A\) define the \(A\)-section
\[
\mathcal V_A(D)
=
\{X\subseteq A:D\cup X\in\mathcal V\}.
\]
Then the following are equivalent:

1. For every \(B\in\mathcal V\),
   \[
   \mathcal V\cap I_T(A,B)\subseteq\{B\}.
   \]

2. For every \(D\subseteq T\setminus A\), the section \(\mathcal V_A(D)\) is
   top-union-free on \(A\): there are no distinct
   \(X,Y\in\mathcal V_A(D)\) with
   \[
   X\cup Y=A.
   \]

Equivalently, if
\[
B=D\cup X,\qquad C=D'\cup Y,
\]
where \(D,D'\subseteq T\setminus A\) and \(X,Y\subseteq A\), then
\[
C\in I_T(A,B)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
D'=D\ \text{ and }\ X\cup Y=A.
\]

Consequently, if \(f\subsetneq u\) is a comparable endpoint pair in a
pair-link-free nonempty star assembly and the upper mixed-shadow exclusion from
`mrw-740b9e5c6cff` holds, namely
\[
\mathcal R_u\cap I_T(A,B)\subseteq\{B\}
\qquad(A\in\mathcal R_f,\ B\in\mathcal R_u),
\]
then for each fixed \(A\in\mathcal R_f\) and every
\(D\subseteq T\setminus A\), the section
\[
\{X\subseteq A:D\cup X\in\mathcal R_u\}
\]
is top-union-free on \(A\).

## Proof
Write
\[
B=D\cup X,\qquad C=D'\cup Y,
\]
with \(D,D'\subseteq T\setminus A\) and \(X,Y\subseteq A\).  Then
\[
A\triangle B=(A\setminus X)\cup D,
\qquad
A\cup B=A\cup D.
\]
Thus the interval condition
\[
A\triangle B\subseteq C\subseteq A\cup B
\]
splits into two coordinate conditions.

Outside \(A\), it gives
\[
D\subseteq D'\subseteq D,
\]
so \(D'=D\).  Inside \(A\), it gives
\[
A\setminus X\subseteq Y\subseteq A.
\]
Since \(Y\subseteq A\), this is equivalent to
\[
X\cup Y=A.
\]
This proves the displayed fixed-\(A\) characterization of \(I_T(A,B)\).

Condition 1 fails exactly when there exist \(B,C\in\mathcal V\) with
\[
C\in\mathcal V\cap I_T(A,B)\setminus\{B\}.
\]
By the fixed-\(A\) characterization, this is equivalent to the existence of
some \(D\subseteq T\setminus A\) and distinct
\[
X,Y\in\mathcal V_A(D)
\]
with \(X\cup Y=A\).  This is precisely the failure of sectionwise
top-union-freeness.  Hence conditions 1 and 2 are equivalent.

For the star-assembly consequence, fix \(A\in\mathcal R_f\) and apply the
equivalence with \(\mathcal V=\mathcal R_u\).  The upper mixed-shadow exclusion
from `mrw-740b9e5c6cff` supplies condition 1 for every \(B\in\mathcal R_u\),
so every \(A\)-section of \(\mathcal R_u\) is top-union-free.

## Notes
- The statement is valid when \(A=\emptyset\).  Then every section has at most
  one element and \(I_T(\emptyset,B)=\{B\}\).
- The point \(B\) need not always lie in \(I_T(A,B)\); it does so exactly when
  \(A\subseteq B\).  This is why the proof phrases failure as a point in
  \(\mathcal V\cap I_T(A,B)\setminus\{B\}\), not as a second point after \(B\).
- This is a structural localization, not terminal Erdos 536 evidence.  It
  reduces the upper mixed-shadow scheme to a product-section problem: bound or
  classify families with no two distinct section members whose union is the
  fixed lower-parent set \(A\).
- Oracle accepted the result with the wording correction about \(B\notin
  I_T(A,B)\) in general.  Scout returned only a scaffold response and was
  ingested raw-only.
