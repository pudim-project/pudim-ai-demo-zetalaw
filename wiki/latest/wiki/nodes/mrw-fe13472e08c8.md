# Proposition: Endpoint-fiber mixtures have exact core-residual variational value

## Statement

Let \(P=Z\sqcup X\sqcup Y\) be a finite disjoint decomposition with
\(X,Y\ne\varnothing\), and let \(\nu_P\) be a product law on \(2^P\) with
coordinate probabilities \(q_p\in(0,1)\).  Let \(\nu_Z\) be the restricted
product law on \(2^Z\).  Put
\[
\alpha_x=q_x\prod_{u\in X\setminus\{x\}}(1-q_u),
\qquad
\beta_y=q_y\prod_{v\in Y\setminus\{y\}}(1-q_v),
\tag{1}
\]
and
\[
\alpha_X=\sum_{x\in X}\alpha_x,\qquad
\beta_Y=\sum_{y\in Y}\beta_y.
\tag{2}
\]

For a real threshold \(L\), define the core pair-link residual value
\[
\mathfrak M_Z(L-2)
=
\sup\{\nu_Z(\mathcal R):
\mathcal R\subseteq2^Z,\ \mathcal R\text{ is pair-link-free},\
\mathcal R\subseteq\{R:|R|>L-2\}\}.
\tag{3}
\]
Let \(\mathfrak A(P;Z,X,Y,L)\) be the class of all exact one-from-each
assemblies
\[
\mathcal A=
\{R\cup\{x,y\}:x\in X,\ y\in Y,\ R\in\mathcal R_{xy}\}
\tag{4}
\]
such that every endpoint fiber \(\mathcal R_{xy}\subseteq2^Z\) is
pair-link-free.  Equivalently, by [[mrw-d7b3299d3813]], every
\(\mathcal A\in\mathfrak A(P;Z,X,Y,L)\) is pair-link-free.

Then the endpoint-fiber mixture has the exact variational value
\[
\sup_{\mathcal A\in\mathfrak A(P;Z,X,Y,L)}
\nu_P(\mathcal A\cap\{S:|S|>L\})
=
\alpha_X\beta_Y\,\mathfrak M_Z(L-2).
\tag{5}
\]

In particular, for the prime-biased law \(\nu_P(p\in S)=1/p\) and the
high-support event
\[
H_{P,\theta}=\{S\subseteq P:|S|>\theta S_P\},
\qquad
S_P=\sum_{p\in P}\frac1p,
\]
one has
\[
\sup_{\mathcal A\in\mathfrak A(P;Z,X,Y,\theta S_P)}
\nu_P(\mathcal A\cap H_{P,\theta})
=
\alpha_X\beta_Y\,\mathfrak M_Z(\theta S_P-2).
\tag{6}
\]
Thus heterogeneous endpoint fibers give no larger high-support mass than the
equal-fiber construction using one core family that is extremal, or
near-extremal, for the shifted core residual.

## Proof

Let \(\mathcal A\in\mathfrak A(P;Z,X,Y,L)\), with endpoint fibers
\(\mathcal R_{xy}\).  By [[mrw-d7b3299d3813]],
\[
\nu_P(\mathcal A\cap\{S:|S|>L\})
=
\sum_{x\in X}\sum_{y\in Y}
\alpha_x\beta_y\,
\nu_Z(\{R\in\mathcal R_{xy}:|R|+2>L\}).
\tag{7}
\]
For each \((x,y)\), the family
\[
\mathcal R_{xy}^{>L-2}
=
\{R\in\mathcal R_{xy}:|R|>L-2\}
\]
is pair-link-free as a subfamily of the pair-link-free family
\(\mathcal R_{xy}\), and it is contained in \(\{R:|R|>L-2\}\).  Hence
\[
\nu_Z(\mathcal R_{xy}^{>L-2})\le\mathfrak M_Z(L-2).
\tag{8}
\]
Substituting (8) into (7) gives
\[
\nu_P(\mathcal A\cap\{S:|S|>L\})
\le
\mathfrak M_Z(L-2)\sum_{x\in X}\sum_{y\in Y}\alpha_x\beta_y
=
\alpha_X\beta_Y\,\mathfrak M_Z(L-2).
\]
Taking the supremum over \(\mathcal A\) proves the upper bound in (5).

For the reverse inequality, fix \(\varepsilon>0\).  Choose a pair-link-free
core family \(\mathcal R_\varepsilon\subseteq\{R:|R|>L-2\}\) such that
\[
\nu_Z(\mathcal R_\varepsilon)>
\mathfrak M_Z(L-2)-\varepsilon.
\]
Use the same core family in every endpoint fiber:
\[
\mathcal R_{xy}=\mathcal R_\varepsilon
\qquad(x\in X,\ y\in Y).
\]
By [[mrw-d7b3299d3813]], the resulting assembly is pair-link-free; equivalently
it is the equal-fiber complete bipartite blow-up from [[mrw-c7c76faed872]].
Since every \(R\in\mathcal R_\varepsilon\) satisfies \(|R|+2>L\), the measure
identity gives
\[
\nu_P(\mathcal A_\varepsilon\cap\{S:|S|>L\})
=
\alpha_X\beta_Y\,\nu_Z(\mathcal R_\varepsilon)
>
\alpha_X\beta_Y(\mathfrak M_Z(L-2)-\varepsilon).
\]
Letting \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\) proves the reverse inequality and hence
(5).  Since \(Z\) is finite, the supremum is in fact a maximum; the
\(\varepsilon\)-argument only avoids a separate attainment discussion.  The
prime-biased statement (6) is the specialization \(L=\theta S_P\).

## Notes

- This proposition quarantines the endpoint-fiber mixture branch.  Within the
  exact one-from-each two-class normal form, varying the core fiber with the
  endpoint pair cannot improve the high-support mass beyond the shifted
  one-core residual multiplied by the one-from-\(X\) and one-from-\(Y\)
  probabilities.
- The lower bound is achieved by the equal-fiber construction from
  [[mrw-c7c76faed872]], up to the usual supremum/epsilon issue.  This does not
  promote the fixed blow-up as terminal evidence; it shows that the entire
  exact endpoint-fiber model is self-similar.
- [[mrw-c7c76faed872]] is a sharpness comparison for the equal-fiber
  construction, and [[mrw-3c39ca3d1973]] is background for pair-link
  vocabulary.  The direct proof only needs [[mrw-d7b3299d3813]].
- Therefore a terminal proof of \(M_{P_k}(\theta)\to0\) along this branch must
  either prove the shifted core residual vanishes in the relevant regimes,
  make the endpoint factor \(\alpha_X\beta_Y\) vanish, or prove that a
  positive-mass candidate cannot stay in the exact one-from-each form and must
  pay the mixed-overlap defect budget from [[mrw-bc27191b14d4]].
- No \(U_k(\theta)\) theorem or \(R_P(\theta)\) lift follows from this node.
