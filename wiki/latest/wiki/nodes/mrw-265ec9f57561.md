# Proposition: Subcritical max-fiber antichain width forces vanishing high-support mass

## Statement

Let \(P_k=\{p_1,\ldots,p_k\}\), \(q_i=1/p_i\),
\[
S_k=\sum_{i\le k}q_i,
\qquad
\nu_k(i\in S)=q_i,
\]
and let \(H_{k,\theta}=\{S\subseteq [k]: |S|>\theta S_k\}\).  For a family
\(\mathcal F_k\subseteq 2^{[k]}\), write its maximum fibers as
\[
\mathcal F_{k,m}
=\{X\subseteq [m-1]: X\cup\{m\}\in\mathcal F_k\},
\]
and put
\[
T_m=\sum_{i<m}q_i,\qquad
V_m=\sum_{i<m}q_i(1-q_i),\qquad
w_{m,k}=q_m\prod_{i>m}(1-q_i).
\]
For each \(m\), let \(a_{m,k}\) be the least number of antichains whose union
covers \(\mathcal F_{k,m}\).  Assume the same product-measure antichain estimate
used in [[mrw-54968b07a069]]:
\[
\mu(\mathcal A)\le C\left(1+\sum_i r_i(1-r_i)\right)^{-1/2}
\tag{1}
\]
for every antichain \(\mathcal A\) under every Bernoulli product law with
coordinate probabilities \(0<r_i\le 1/2\).

Fix \(0<\theta<1\).  If
\[
B_k(\theta)
:=\sum_{\substack{m\le k\\ T_m>\theta S_k/2}} w_{m,k}a_{m,k}
=o(\sqrt{S_k}),
\tag{2}
\]
then
\[
\nu_k(\mathcal F_k\cap H_{k,\theta})\to0.
\]

In particular, any positive-mass high-support union-free counterexample to
[[mrw-55a8d9eddd2e]] for some fixed \(0<\theta<1\) must have
\[
B_k(\theta)\not=o(\sqrt{S_k})
\]
along a subsequence.  Thus the killed max-fiber antichain skeletons from
[[mrw-54968b07a069]] are not merely a special case: any surviving obstruction
must have large weighted antichain-cover width in its moving maximum fibers.

## Proof

The max-fiber decomposition gives
\[
\nu_k(\mathcal F_k\cap H_{k,\theta})
=\sum_{m\le k}w_{m,k}\,
\mu_{m-1}\bigl(\mathcal F_{k,m}\cap\{|X|+1>\theta S_k\}\bigr),
\]
where \(\mu_{m-1}\) is the product law on \([m-1]\) with coordinate
probabilities \(q_i\).  The weights satisfy
\[
\sum_{m\le k}w_{m,k}=\nu_k(S\ne\emptyset)\le1.
\]

First sum over \(m\) with \(T_m\le\theta S_k/2\).  For all large \(k\), the event
\(|X|+1>\theta S_k\) lies a fixed positive proportion above the Poisson-binomial
mean \(T_m\).  A Chernoff bound gives
\[
\mu_{m-1}(|X|+1>\theta S_k)\le \exp(-c_\theta S_k),
\]
uniformly over these \(m\), so their total contribution is
\[
O(\exp(-c_\theta S_k)).
\]

Now assume \(T_m>\theta S_k/2\).  Since \(q_i\le1/2\), we have
\[
V_m\ge T_m/2>\theta S_k/4.
\]
Cover \(\mathcal F_{k,m}\) by \(a_{m,k}\) antichains.  Intersecting each
antichain with the high-support condition preserves the antichain property, so
(1) gives
\[
\mu_{m-1}\bigl(\mathcal F_{k,m}\cap\{|X|+1>\theta S_k\}\bigr)
\le C_\theta a_{m,k}S_k^{-1/2}.
\]
After multiplying by \(w_{m,k}\) and summing over the large-\(T_m\) indices,
the contribution is at most
\[
C_\theta S_k^{-1/2}B_k(\theta)=o(1)
\]
by (2).  Combining the two pieces proves the proposition.

The final claim is the contrapositive applied to any family sequence with
\(\limsup_k \nu_k(\mathcal F_k\cap H_{k,\theta})>0\).

## Notes

This proposition is a structural reduction, not a proof of
\(U_k(\theta)\to0\).  It also deliberately states the clean high-support case
\(0<\theta<1\).  The \(\theta=0\) version needs an additional low-maximum
control condition if the antichain-cover widths \(a_{m,k}\) are allowed to grow
with \(k\).

The statement does not use union-freeness directly.  Its role is to identify
what union-freeness must still supply: either a proof that \(B_k(\theta)\) is
subcritical for all union-free high-support families, or a genuine
positive-mass counterexample with \(B_k(\theta)\gtrsim\sqrt{S_k}\) on the
weighted maximum fibers.
