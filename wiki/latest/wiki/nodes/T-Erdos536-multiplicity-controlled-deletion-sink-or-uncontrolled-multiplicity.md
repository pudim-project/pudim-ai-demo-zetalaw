# Theorem: T Erdos536 multiplicity controlled deletion sink or uncontrolled multiplicity

## Statement

Multiplicity-controlled deletion sink or uncontrolled multiplicity model: in the lower-trace-poor branch, either prove a bounded or subcritical upper bound for the multiplicity with which an actual lower trace \(A\) occurs as \(A=C\setminus D\) from active tops, strong enough that product deletion mass transport forces vanishing top mass whenever deletion weights diverge, or construct an explicit uncontrolled-multiplicity configuration with positive \(\nu_k\)-mass that evades the deletion sink.
