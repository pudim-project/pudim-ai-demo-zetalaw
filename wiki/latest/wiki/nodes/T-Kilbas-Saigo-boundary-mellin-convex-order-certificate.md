# Theorem: double Gamma Mellin quotient has positive kernel certificate implying KS Gamma Laplace order

## Statement

For the boundary Kilbas--Saigo variable \(X_{\alpha,m}\) and Gamma comparison variable \(Z_{\alpha,m}\), the double-Gamma Mellin-transform quotient admits a nonnegative kernel or complete-Bernstein representation on the relevant Mellin strip that implies \(Z_{\alpha,m}\le_{Lt}X_{\alpha,m}\) for all \(\alpha\in(0,1]\) and \(m>0\).
