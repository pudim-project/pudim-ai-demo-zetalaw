# Theorem: Erdos536 matching defect absorption by rank or endpoint recursion

## Statement

Every sparse matching-like disjointness component in conditional defect laws below occupied tops is absorbable into one of the existing Erdos536 obstruction controls: an \(o(\sqrt{S_k})\)-rank block, a moving common-core family, or an endpoint-tower recursion whose terminal matched endpoints lift to a same-endpoint fork. Thus matching-like components do not form a fourth terminal obstruction for non-rank-thin positive-mass families.
