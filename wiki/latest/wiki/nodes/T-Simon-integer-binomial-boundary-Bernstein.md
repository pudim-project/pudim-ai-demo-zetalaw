# Theorem: Simon integer binomial boundary Bernstein slice

## Statement

For every integer \(m\ge2\), the Simon binomial moment sequence \(\mu_n(r)=\binom{mn+r}{n}\) has the Bernstein product property on the source interval only at the boundary \(r=m-1\). At \(r=m-1\), the residual \(L_m-R_n\), where \(L_m=m^m/(m-1)^{m-1}\), is a positive finite sum of simple Hausdorff/Laplace kernels.
