# Corollary: Prime-modulus Dirichlet L-resolution

## Statement

For prime \(p\), let \(\chi\) range over Dirichlet characters modulo \(p\). For \(a\in\{1,\ldots,p-1\}\), define
\[
S_a(p,\beta)=\sum_{\chi\bmod p}\overline{\chi(a)}L(\beta,\chi).
\]
Then
\[
\mu_{p,\beta}(a)=\frac{S_a(p,\beta)}{(p-1)\zeta(\beta)}
\quad(1\le a\le p-1),
\qquad
\mu_{p,\beta}(0)=p^{-\beta}.
\]
Substitution of these residues into \(B_p(\beta)\) gives prime-modulus functionals \(F_p\) with
\[
\Sigma(\beta)=\lim_{\substack{p\to\infty\\ p\ {\rm prime}}}
F_p\bigl(\zeta(\beta),\{L(\beta,\chi)\}_{\chi\bmod p}\bigr)
=
\sup_{p\ {\rm prime}}
F_p\bigl(\zeta(\beta),\{L(\beta,\chi)\}_{\chi\bmod p}\bigr).
\]

## Proof

For \(a\not\equiv0\pmod p\), character orthogonality on \((\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z)^\times\) gives
\[
\frac1{p-1}\sum_{\chi\bmod p}\overline{\chi(a)}\chi(n)
=
\begin{cases}
1,& n\equiv a\pmod p,\\
0,& n\not\equiv a\pmod p,
\end{cases}
\]
Hence
\[
\sum_{\substack{n\ge1\\ n\equiv a\pmod p}}n^{-\beta}
=\frac1{p-1}\sum_{\chi\bmod p}\overline{\chi(a)}L(\beta,\chi).
\]
Dividing by \(\zeta(\beta)\) gives the asserted formula for \(\mu_{p,\beta}(a)\). The zero class is
\[
\mu_{p,\beta}(0)
=\frac1{\zeta(\beta)}\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}(mp)^{-\beta}
=p^{-\beta}.
\]
Substituting these expressions into the modular successor entropy \(B_p(\beta)\) gives the functional \(F_p\). The inequality, prime-limit identity, and prime supremum identity follow from the successor entropy theorem along the sequence of primes \(p\to\infty\).
