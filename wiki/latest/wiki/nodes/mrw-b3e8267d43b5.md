# Corollary: Prime-modulus Dirichlet L-resolution

## Statement

For prime \(p\), let \(\chi\) range over Dirichlet characters modulo \(p\). For \(a\in\{1,\ldots,p-1\}\), define
\[
S_a(p,\beta)=\sum_{\chi\bmod p}\chi(a)L(\beta,\chi).
\]
The imported PDF states
\[
\mu_{p,\beta}(a)=\frac{S_a(p,\beta)}{(p-1)\zeta(\beta)}
\quad(1\le a\le p-1),
\qquad
\mu_{p,\beta}(0)=p^{-\beta},
\]
and that the prime-modulus functionals recover \(\Sigma(\beta)\) in the prime limit and by supremum over primes.

## Evidence

The PDF proof uses Dirichlet character orthogonality for nonzero residue classes and substitutes the resulting formula for \(\mu_{p,\beta}\) into \(B_p(\beta)\).
