# Theorem: Dagum beta two endpoint obstruction alpha less than one not completely monotone

## Statement

For \(0\le\alpha<1\), the function \(x\mapsto x^{-\alpha}/(1+x^2)\) is not completely monotone on \((0,\infty)\).
