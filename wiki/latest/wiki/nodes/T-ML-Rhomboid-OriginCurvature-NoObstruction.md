# Theorem: Mittag-Leffler rhomboid origin curvature supports reverse inequality

## Statement

Let \(a_n=1/\Gamma(\beta+n\alpha)\). For every \(1<\alpha<2\) and \(\alpha-1\le\beta\le\alpha\), one has \(2a_0a_2-a_1^2<0\). Equivalently, the expansion of \(|E_{\alpha,\beta}(iy)|^2-E_{\alpha,\beta}(0)^2\) has positive quadratic coefficient, so no small-imaginary-axis obstruction to the global reverse inequality occurs at the origin.
