# Corollary: Strict-deletion tail budget is terminal-window plus endpoint-moment error

## Statement

Use the finite endpoint-tower notation
\[
P=P_0\supseteq P_1\supseteq\cdots\supseteq P_r,\qquad
P_{j-1}=P_j\sqcup X_j\sqcup Y_j .
\]
Let
\[
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{ov}}
=
\bigcup_{j=1}^r
\left(\binom{X_j}{2}\cup\binom{Y_j}{2}\right)
\]
be the same-class endpoint-pair set.  For \(e=\{p,q\}\in
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{ov}}\), write \(q_e=q_pq_q\), and let
\(\ell_e,T_e,\Gamma_e,\beta_e,\pi_{\mathrm{ov}}^e\) be the induced
strict-deletion data of [[mrw-5df7f8135e2c]].  Put
\[
a_e=\Gamma_e+\beta_e.
\]
Let
\[
R_2
=
\sum_{e\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{ov}}}q_e
=
\sum_{j=1}^r
\left(
\sum_{\{p,q\}\subseteq X_j}q_pq_q
+
\sum_{\{p,q\}\subseteq Y_j}q_pq_q
\right).
\]
Let \(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{col}}\subseteq\mathcal E_{\mathrm{ov}}\) be the
collapsed pairs: \(e\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{col}}\) when
\[
e=C_j
\qquad\text{for some }C_j\in\{X_j,Y_j\}.
\]
For such a collapsed pair, let \(D_j\) be the opposite endpoint class at level
\(j\), so \(\{C_j,D_j\}=\{X_j,Y_j\}\).  Define the absorbed third-order tail
\[
T_{\mathrm{abs}}
=
\sum_{\substack{e=C_j\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{col}}}}
q_e a_e\,\nu_{D_j}(\{B\subseteq D_j:|B|\ge3\}).
\]

Then every pair-link-free family \(\mathcal F\subseteq2^P\) and every real
threshold \(L\) satisfy
\[
\nu_P(\mathcal F\cap H_L)
\le
\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r)
+
\left(\sum_{e\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{ov}}}q_ea_e\right)
\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r-2)
+
\sum_{e\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{ov}}}q_e\pi_{\mathrm{ov}}^e
+
T_{\mathrm{abs}},
\]
where \(H_L=\{S\subseteq P:|S|>L\}\).

Moreover,
\[
\sum_{e\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{ov}}}q_ea_e\le 2R_2,
\qquad
\sum_{e\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{ov}}}q_e\pi_{\mathrm{ov}}^e\le R_2^2,
\]
and
\[
T_{\mathrm{abs}}
\le
\frac13
\sum_{\substack{e=C_j\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{col}}}}
q_e Q(D_j)^3,
\qquad
Q(D_j)=\sum_{p\in D_j}q_p .
\]
Consequently,
\[
\nu_P(\mathcal F\cap H_L)
\le
\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r)
+2R_2\,\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r-2)
+R_2^2
+
\frac13
\sum_{\substack{e=C_j\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{col}}}}
q_e Q(D_j)^3 .
\]

## Proof

Start from [[mrw-5df7f8135e2c]]:
\[
\nu_P(\mathcal F\cap H_L)
\le
\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r)
+
\sum_{e\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{ov}}}
q_e\left[
a_e\mathfrak M_{T_e}(L-2-2\ell_e)
+
\pi_{\mathrm{ov}}^e
\right].
\]

If \(e\subseteq C_j\) is a surviving deletion, then [[mrw-5df7f8135e2c]]
gives
\[
\ell_e=r,\qquad T_e=P_r,
\]
so
\[
\mathfrak M_{T_e}(L-2-2\ell_e)
=
\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r-2).
\]

Now let \(e=C_j\) be collapsed.  If \(r>1\), then
[[mrw-5df7f8135e2c]] gives
\[
\ell_e=r-1,\qquad T_e=P_r\cup D_j.
\]
If \(r=j=1\), the induced tower has length zero, \(T_e=P_e=P_r\cup D_j\),
and the same formula below is applied with threshold \(L-2\).  In both cases
[[mrw-791fae526f01]], with the \(b=2\) window, gives
\[
\mathfrak M_{T_e}(L-2-2\ell_e)
\le
\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r-2)
+
\nu_{D_j}(\{B\subseteq D_j:|B|\ge3\}).
\]
Substituting the surviving and collapsed estimates into the previous
strict-deletion inequality gives
\[
\nu_P(\mathcal F\cap H_L)
\le
\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r)
+
\left(\sum_{e\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{ov}}}q_ea_e\right)
\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r-2)
+
\sum_{e\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{ov}}}q_e\pi_{\mathrm{ov}}^e
+
T_{\mathrm{abs}}.
\]

It remains to record the endpoint-moment bounds.  Since \(\Gamma_e\) is an
exact endpoint probability, \(0\le\Gamma_e\le1\), and
\(\beta_e\in\{0,1\}\), one has \(a_e\le2\).  Hence
\[
\sum_{e\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{ov}}}q_ea_e\le2R_2.
\]

For the second-overfull term, [[mrw-5df7f8135e2c]] gives
\[
\pi_{\mathrm{ov}}^e
\le
\sum_{C\in\mathcal C_e}
\sum_{\{a,b\}\subseteq C}q_aq_b,
\]
where \(\mathcal C_e\) is the list of retained endpoint classes in the
induced tower on \(P\setminus e\).  Each retained induced endpoint class is
either one of the original endpoint classes or a subset of one original
endpoint class; in the collapsed case the absorbed opposite class \(D_j\) is
not retained as an endpoint class.  Therefore
\[
\sum_{C\in\mathcal C_e}
\sum_{\{a,b\}\subseteq C}q_aq_b
\le R_2
\]
for every \(e\).  Multiplying by \(q_e\) and summing gives
\[
\sum_{e\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{ov}}}q_e\pi_{\mathrm{ov}}^e
\le
R_2\sum_{e\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{ov}}}q_e
=R_2^2.
\]

Finally, [[mrw-791fae526f01]] gives the product tail bound
\[
\nu_{D_j}(\{B:|B|\ge3\})
\le
\sum_{\{a,b,c\}\subseteq D_j}q_aq_bq_c
\le
\frac{Q(D_j)^3}{6}.
\]
Together with \(a_e\le2\), this yields
\[
T_{\mathrm{abs}}
\le
\frac13
\sum_{\substack{e=C_j\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{col}}}}
q_e Q(D_j)^3.
\]
The final displayed estimate follows by combining these three bounds.

## Notes

- This corollary inserts the \(b=2\) absorbed-window estimate from
  [[mrw-791fae526f01]] into the full strict-deletion iteration
  [[mrw-5df7f8135e2c]].
- The result is an endpoint-moment budget, not a terminal residual theorem.
  It does not prove decay of \(\mathfrak M_{P_r}\), does not close
  \(M_{P_k}(\theta)\), \(U_k(\theta)\), or an \(R_P(\theta)\) lift, and does
  not use any barred terminal mechanism.
- The remaining nonterminal fork is now explicit: either the terminal window
  \(\mathfrak M_{P_r}(L-2r-2)\), the quadratic endpoint-pair budget
  \(R_2^2\), and the absorbed cubic budget are small in the intended prime
  tower, or a near-extremal construction must carry substantial endpoint-pair
  or absorbed-third-order mass.  The next cycle should test whether such
  substantial higher-occupancy mass forces pointwise mixed incidence and
  coherent-component defect.
