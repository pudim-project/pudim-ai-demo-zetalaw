# Theorem: Boudabsa Simon Conjecture 3 boundary Kilbas Saigo lower hyperbolic bound all alpha in (0,1], m>0, x>=0

## Statement

For \(\alpha\in(0,1]\), \(m>0\), and \(x\ge0\), the Boudabsa--Simon boundary Kilbas--Saigo conjecture holds: \(E_{\alpha,m,m-1/\alpha}(-x)\ge (1+C_{\alpha,m}x)^{-1-1/m}\), where \(C_{\alpha,m}=(\alpha m)^{-\alpha/(m+1)}(\Gamma(1+\alpha)G(1-\alpha;\alpha m)G(1+\alpha;\alpha m))^{-m/(m+1)}\).
