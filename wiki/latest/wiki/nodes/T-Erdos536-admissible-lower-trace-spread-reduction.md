# Theorem: Erdos536 admissible lower-trace spread reduction

## Statement

Executable admissibility/spread reduction: for fixed \(0<\theta<1\) and \(\eta>0\), every positive-mass non-rank-thin sequence \(\mathcal F_k\subseteq H_{k,\theta}\) admits a lower-trace model over occupied tops \(C\in\mathcal F_k\) with explicit objects \(\mathcal L_{\mathcal F_k}(C)=\{A\in\mathcal F_k:A\subsetneq C\}\), defect laws \(D=C\setminus A\), top law, lower-trace law, coordinate-coverage statistic \(\Omega_k(C)\), and spread parameters such that zero coverage can only occur through one of the three audited branches: common-core concentration, moving-core/endpoint-shield descent, or sparse matching-like disjointness. Rank-thin exceptional mass is negligible under \(\nu_k\).
