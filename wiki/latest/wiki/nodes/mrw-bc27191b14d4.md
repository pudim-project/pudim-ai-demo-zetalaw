# Proposition: Coherent robust components have defect-bounded normalized mixed overlaps

## Statement

Use the common ancestor-signature setting of [[mrw-750fb7a7e30c]] and
[[mrw-a082a34f6797]].  Thus all corridors live on one weighted vertex set
\(V\), with one ancestor signature partition \(V=\bigsqcup_\tau V_\tau\),
and corridor \(i\) has oriented sides
\[
S_i^0=U_i,\qquad S_i^1=W_i,
\]
selected signature \(\tau_i\), side masses \(M_i^0,M_i^1>0\), and side
defects
\[
D_i^0=(1-\lambda_i)M_i^0,\qquad
D_i^1=(1-\lambda_i)M_i^1,
\qquad 1/2\le\lambda_i\le1.
\]
For distinct corridors \(i,j\) and side bits \(b,c\in\{0,1\}\), call the
side overlap robust when
\[
w(S_i^b\cap S_j^c)>D_i^b+D_j^c.
\tag{1}
\]

Let \(H\) be the complete robust side-overlap multigraph on a finite corridor
set \(I\): for every distinct \(i,j\in I\) and every robust side pair
\((b,c)\), \(H\) contains the edge \(ij\) labelled by \((b,c)\).  Let \(K\)
be a connected parity-consistent component of \(H\), and let
\[
\epsilon_i\in\{0,1\}\qquad(i\in K)
\]
be the parity potential supplied by [[mrw-a082a34f6797]].  Define normalized
sides and normalized defects by
\[
\widehat S_i^a=S_i^{a+\epsilon_i\pmod 2},
\qquad
\widehat D_i^a=D_i^{a+\epsilon_i\pmod 2}
\qquad(a\in\{0,1\}).
\tag{2}
\]
Then every mixed normalized side overlap inside \(K\) is defect-bounded:
\[
i\ne j,\quad a\ne b
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
w(\widehat S_i^a\cap \widehat S_j^b)
\le
\widehat D_i^a+\widehat D_j^b .
\tag{3}
\]

Consequently,
\[
\sum_{\{i,j\}\subseteq K}\sum_{\substack{a,b\in\{0,1\}\\a\ne b}}
w(\widehat S_i^a\cap \widehat S_j^b)
\le
(|K|-1)\sum_{i\in K}(D_i^0+D_i^1).
\tag{4}
\]

In the complete case \(\lambda_i=1\) for all \(i\in K\), every normalized
mixed overlap in (3) has zero weight.  Thus a complete parity-consistent
robust component can have positive-weight sharing between distinct corridors
only through equal normalized sides.

## Proof

Fix distinct \(i,j\in K\) and normalized side bits \(a\ne b\).  Put
\[
\beta=a+\epsilon_i\pmod2,\qquad
\gamma=b+\epsilon_j\pmod2.
\]
Then
\[
\widehat S_i^a=S_i^\beta,\qquad
\widehat S_j^b=S_j^\gamma,
\]
and the relative side parity of this original side pair is
\[
\beta+\gamma
\equiv a+b+\epsilon_i+\epsilon_j
\equiv 1+\epsilon_i+\epsilon_j
\pmod2,
\tag{5}
\]
because \(a\ne b\).

Suppose, for contradiction, that
\[
w(\widehat S_i^a\cap \widehat S_j^b)>
\widehat D_i^a+\widehat D_j^b .
\]
By (2), this says exactly that the original side pair
\((\beta,\gamma)\) is robust.  Since \(H\) is the complete robust
side-overlap multigraph, \(H\) contains the labelled edge \(ij\) with side
parity \(\beta+\gamma\).  This edge lies inside the same connected component
\(K\).

The potential property from [[mrw-a082a34f6797]] says that every robust edge
inside \(K\) has side parity
\[
\epsilon_i+\epsilon_j\pmod2.
\tag{6}
\]
Equations (5) and (6) differ by \(1\), a contradiction.  Therefore the
mixed normalized overlap is not robust, which is precisely (3).

For (4), apply (3) to each unordered pair \(\{i,j\}\subseteq K\) and the two
mixed normalized side choices \((a,b)=(0,1)\) and \((1,0)\).  For a fixed
unordered pair \(\{i,j\}\), the right side is
\[
\widehat D_i^0+\widehat D_i^1+\widehat D_j^0+\widehat D_j^1
=D_i^0+D_i^1+D_j^0+D_j^1.
\]
Summing over all unordered pairs counts each corridor's total defect
\(D_i^0+D_i^1\) exactly \(|K|-1\) times, giving (4).

If every \(\lambda_i=1\), all defects vanish.  Then (3) gives zero weight for
every mixed normalized overlap, so positive shared support can occur only
between equal normalized sides.

## Notes

- The completeness assumption on \(H\) is essential: the proposition uses the
  fact that every robust side pair among vertices of \(K\) has been included
  as an edge.  For a hand-selected subgraph, the conclusion applies only after
  adding all robust side-overlap edges or separately checking that the missing
  side pairs are not robust.
- This is a component-level aggregation of the pairwise relative-parity
  filter [[mrw-10ea41c73237]].  It converts the parity-consistent alternative
  into a two-class normalized overlap form, with all cross-class sharing
  charged to near-purity defects.
- The formal proof uses [[mrw-a082a34f6797]] and the robust-overlap setting of
  [[mrw-750fb7a7e30c]].  The pairwise filter [[mrw-10ea41c73237]], the
  incompatible-overlap bound [[mrw-206678825c7a]], and near-complete
  concentration [[mrw-36595780824f]] are contextual ancestry rather than
  minimal direct dependencies.
- The result does not produce the robust component, force an odd-parity
  component, prove \(M_{P_k}(\theta)\to0\), prove \(U_k(\theta)\to0\), or give
  an \(R_P(\theta)\) lift.  The next global task is still to prove that
  positive high-support mass creates enough overlapping corridors to make
  (4) incompatible with small total defect, or to construct and test a
  coherent two-class assembly against the full pair-link interval.
