# Theorem: Sharper explicit n=2 admissible beta subwindow

## Statement

Let
\[
C_2(x)=\psi''(x)+x\psi^{(3)}(x),
\qquad
P_2(x)=\psi''(x)\psi''(1/x),
\]
and
\[
\mathcal I_2=\{\beta\in\mathbb R:x^\beta C_2(x)-P_2(x)<0\text{ for all }x>0\}.
\]
Then
\[
\left[\frac{19}{8},3\right]\subseteq\mathcal I_2.
\]

## Proof

As in [[wiki/nodes/mrw-5fabc550bd7d|Explicit n=2 admissible beta subwindow]], put
\[
Z_s(a)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(a+k)^{-s}
\]
and
\[
F_{\beta,2}(x)=x^\beta C_2(x)-P_2(x).
\]
The polygamma expansion gives
\[
F_{\beta,2}(x)
=
2x^\beta\bigl(3xZ_4(x)-Z_3(x)\bigr)
-4Z_3(x)Z_3(1/x).
\]
Since
\[
3xZ_4(x)-Z_3(x)<2Z_3(x),
\]
we have
\[
F_{\beta,2}(x)
<
4Z_3(x)\bigl(x^\beta-Z_3(1/x)\bigr).
\]
Thus it is enough to prove
\[
Z_3(1/x)>x^\beta
\]
for \(19/8\le\beta\le3\).

If \(x\ge1\), then
\[
Z_3(1/x)>x^3\ge x^\beta
\]
because \(\beta\le3\).  It remains to consider \(0<x<1\).  Since \(\beta\ge19/8\), it is enough to show
\[
Z_3(1/x)>x^{19/8}.
\]
Write
\[
Z_3(1/x)=x^3\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(1+kx)^{-3}.
\]
For \(f(t)=(1+xt)^{-3}\), monotonicity gives
\[
\sum_{k=3}^{\infty}f(k)>\int_3^\infty f(t)\,dt
=\frac{(1+3x)^{-2}}{2x}.
\]
Therefore
\[
Z_3(1/x)
>
x^3\left(
1+\frac1{(1+x)^3}+\frac1{(1+2x)^3}
+\frac1{2x(1+3x)^2}
\right).
\]

Put \(y=x^{1/8}\).  After multiplying the desired lower-bound inequality by the positive denominator
\[
2y^3(1+y^8)^3(1+2y^8)^3(1+3y^8)^2,
\]
it is equivalent to \(P(y)>0\) for \(0<y<1\), where
\[
\begin{aligned}
P(y)=&144y^{72}-144y^{67}+744y^{64}-744y^{59}
+1636y^{56}-1636y^{51}\\
&+2168y^{48}-1998y^{43}+1896y^{40}-1482y^{35}
+1110y^{32}-684y^{27}\\
&+429y^{24}-192y^{19}+105y^{16}-30y^{11}
+15y^8-2y^3+1.
\end{aligned}
\]
An exact Sturm calculation gives \(P(0)=1\), \(P(1)=1336\), and zero roots in \((0,1)\): the sign variations of the Sturm chain at \(0\) and \(1\) are both \(34\).  Hence \(P(y)>0\) for \(0\le y\le1\).

It follows that \(Z_3(1/x)>x^{19/8}\) for \(0<x<1\).  Therefore \(Z_3(1/x)>x^\beta\) for every \(x>0\) and every \(19/8\le\beta\le3\).  The displayed bound for \(F_{\beta,2}\) gives \(F_{\beta,2}(x)<0\) for all \(x>0\), proving
\[
\left[\frac{19}{8},3\right]\subseteq\mathcal I_2.
\]

## Notes

- This improves the sufficient interval \([5/2,3]\) to \([19/8,3]\).
- It is sharpened by [[wiki/nodes/mrw-da05add5bca1|Euler-Maclaurin n=2 admissible beta subwindow]], which proves \([187/80,3]\subseteq\mathcal I_2\).
- It is still not the largest admissible \(n=2\) interval.
- Together with [[wiki/nodes/mrw-3712cf1c88d8|Refined compact localization for the n=2 lower-envelope maximum]], the current certified lower-envelope gap is
\[
\frac{4629}{2000}<L_2\le\frac{19}{8}.
\]
- Further progress should either sharpen the sufficient edge below \(19/8\) or return to the critical-point equation in [[wiki/nodes/mrw-a3170d192f6c|Critical-point equation for the n=2 lower-envelope maximum]].
