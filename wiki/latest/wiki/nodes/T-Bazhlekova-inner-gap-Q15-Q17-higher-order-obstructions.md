# Theorem: Bazhlekova residual inner gap Q15 Q17 higher order odd derivative obstruction seeds

## Statement

Inside the Bazhlekova residual inner gap, exact recurrence arithmetic gives \(Q_{15}^{6/5,3/50}(7)<0\) and \(Q_{17}^{3/2,6/25}(18)<0\). Hence the odd-derivative small-\(x\) criterion gives uniform \(w_t\)-positivity failure for those two exponent pairs and all \(c,d>0\).
