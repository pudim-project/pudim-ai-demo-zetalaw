# Theorem: Szabo psi shift alpha greater than one endpoint obstruction

## Statement

For \(0<d<1\) and \(lpha>1\), \(y^lpha[\psi(y+d)-\psi(y)-d/y]\) has positive derivative near \(0^+\), so it is not completely monotone on \((0,\infty)\).
