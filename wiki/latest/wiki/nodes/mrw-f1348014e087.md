# Proposition: Threshold-aware CRT certificate for Erdos first-hit block uniformity

## Statement

Use the notation of [[wiki/nodes/mrw-2945dff32e3e|CRT prefix-dispersion certificate for Erdos first-hit block uniformity]].  For a finite block
\[
I=\{A+1,\ldots,B\},
\]
put
\[
G_I=\bigcup_{i\in I}H_i,\qquad \eta_I=\sum_{i\in I}h_i,\qquad L=L_B,\qquad T=n_B.
\]
Let \(D_I\subseteq\{1,\ldots,L\}\) be the eventual CRT support of \(G_I\) modulo \(L\), with \(0\pmod L\) represented by \(L\).  For \(d\in D_I\), define \(\tau_I(d)\) to be the least integer satisfying
\[
\tau_I(d)\ge T,\qquad \tau_I(d)\equiv d\pmod L.
\]
Define the threshold-aware CRT prefix defect
\[
Q_I=
\sup_{x\ge2}
\frac1{\log x}
\sum_{\substack{d\in D_I\\ \tau_I(d)<x}}\frac1{\tau_I(d)}.
\]
Then, with
\[
c_0=1+\frac1{\log2},
\]
one has
\[
\sup_{x\ge2}\mu_x(G_I)\le P_I+Q_I+c_0\eta_I.
\]

Consequently, if there are cutpoints \(0=N_0<N_1<\cdots\) for which the corresponding blocks \(I_r=\{N_{r-1}+1,\ldots,N_r\}\) satisfy
\[
P_{I_r}+Q_{I_r}\le C\eta_{I_r}+\varepsilon_r,
\qquad
\sum_r\varepsilon_r<\infty,
\]
then \(B\) has logarithmic density
\[
\delta=\lim_N\delta_N.
\]

## Proof

Fix the block \(I\).  For \(2\le x<T\), the desired bound follows immediately from the definition of \(P_I\).  Assume \(x\ge T\).

The part of \(G_I\) below \(T\) contributes at most \(P_I\) to the normalized logarithmic mass, by the same threshold-prefix argument used in [[wiki/nodes/mrw-2945dff32e3e|CRT prefix-dispersion certificate for Erdos first-hit block uniformity]].

For \(m\ge T\), the block \(G_I\) is periodic modulo \(L\) with support \(D_I\).  Fix \(d\in D_I\).  The actual integers in this class that can contribute to \(G_I\cap[T,\infty)\) are
\[
\tau_I(d),\ \tau_I(d)+L,\ \tau_I(d)+2L,\ldots.
\]
Therefore
\[
\sum_{\substack{m<x\\m\equiv d\pmod L\\m\ge T}}\frac1m
\le
\mathbf 1_{\tau_I(d)<x}\frac1{\tau_I(d)}
+\frac1L H_{\lfloor x/L\rfloor},
\]
where \(H_M=\sum_{1\le k\le M}1/k\) and \(H_0=0\).  Summing over \(d\in D_I\) gives
\[
\sum_{\substack{m<x\\m\in G_I\\m\ge T}}\frac1m
\le
\sum_{\substack{d\in D_I\\\tau_I(d)<x}}\frac1{\tau_I(d)}
+\frac{|D_I|}{L}H_{\lfloor x/L\rfloor}.
\]
Since \(|D_I|/L=\eta_I\), the first term contributes at most \(Q_I\) after division by \(\log x\), and the second contributes at most
\[
\eta_I\frac{1+\log x}{\log x}
\le
\left(1+\frac1{\log2}\right)\eta_I
=c_0\eta_I.
\]
Combining the threshold-prefix and periodic parts proves
\[
\mu_x(G_I)\le P_I+Q_I+c_0\eta_I
\]
for \(x\ge T\), and hence for all \(x\ge2\).

For the block sequence \(I_r\), the hypothesis gives
\[
\sup_{x\ge2}\mu_x(G_{I_r})
\le
(C+c_0)\eta_{I_r}+\varepsilon_r.
\]
This is the block-uniform hypothesis in [[wiki/nodes/mrw-e0778085804e|Block-uniform first-hit criterion for Erdos residue-class logarithmic density]], so that proposition proves the logarithmic density conclusion.

## Notes

- This proposition repairs the threshold overcount exposed by [[wiki/nodes/mrw-e0971c9b820a|Small-residue obstruction to unshifted CRT prefix-defect summability]].
- It is still a sufficient certificate, not a full solution of Erdos Problem #25.
- The activation-scale bound [[wiki/nodes/mrw-eaf102b5cac0|Activation-scale bound for threshold-aware Erdos first-hit defects]] proves \(Q_I\le L_B\eta_I/(n_B\log n_B)\).  The next target is to improve that crude ratio using overlap or first-cycle entropy, or to build a genuine actual-mass obstruction.
- The first-cycle entropy bound [[wiki/nodes/mrw-7586943cc138|First-cycle entropy bound for threshold-aware Erdos defects]] replaces the crude linear support cost with \(\Phi(T,M)\).  Further improvement must use residue structure, not only \(T\) and \(|D_I|\).
