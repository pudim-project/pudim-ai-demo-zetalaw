# Theorem: T Erdos536 prime biased weighted antichain sink reduction

## Statement

Prime-biased weighted antichain sink reduction: prove that every antichain \(\mathcal A_k\subseteq2^{[k]}\) has \(\nu_k(\mathcal A_k)=o(1)\) for \(\nu_k(i\in S)=1/p_i\), preferably with a quantitative bound of order \(O((\sum_{i\le k}1/p_i)^{-1/2})\), and then audit whether lower-trace-poor admissibility reduces to antichain or comparable-pair exclusion after removing rank-thin mass. If the reduction fails, identify the exact extra union-free/lower-trace condition not covered by Sperner.
