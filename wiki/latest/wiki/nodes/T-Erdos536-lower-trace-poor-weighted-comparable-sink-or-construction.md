# Theorem: Erdos536 lower trace poor weighted comparable sink or construction

## Statement

Lower-trace-poor weighted comparable-pair sink or construction: for the lower-trace-poor branch under \(\nu_k(p_i\in S)=1/p_i\), prove a weighted comparable-pair, shadow, chain, entropy, or LYM estimate forcing admissible high-support non-rank-thin families to have vanishing \(\nu_k\)-mass; otherwise construct an explicit positive-mass non-rank-thin lower-trace-poor family. This branch must not use defect laws, square sums, or lower-trace-rich assumptions.
