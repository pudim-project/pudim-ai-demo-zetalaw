# Proposition: Ambient cosunflower sparsity for biased squarefree supports

## Statement

Let \(P\) be a finite set of primes and let \(\nu_P\) be the product law on \(2^P\) with
\[
\nu_P(p\in S)=\frac1p.
\]
Let \(A,B,C\) be independent \(\nu_P\)-random subsets of \(P\).  The probability that no prime of \(P\) belongs to exactly one of \(A,B,C\) is
\[
\Xi_P
=
\prod_{p\in P}
\left(1-\frac3p\left(1-\frac1p\right)^2\right).
\]
Equivalently, \(\Xi_P\) is the probability that \(A,B,C\) form a squarefree cosunflower, allowing equal sets.

Writing
\[
S_P=\sum_{p\in P}\frac1p,
\]
one has the uniform prime-set asymptotic
\[
\log \Xi_P=-3S_P+O(1).
\]
In particular, along any finite prime sets with \(S_P\to\infty\),
\[
\Xi_P\to0.
\]

For \(0\le\theta<1\), let
\[
H_{P,\theta}=\{S\subseteq P:\ |S|>\theta S_P\}.
\]
Then
\[
\nu_P(H_{P,\theta})\to1
\]
whenever \(S_P\to\infty\), and the conditional ambient cosunflower density inside the full high-support event satisfies
\[
\nu_P^{\otimes3}
\left(
(A,B,C)\text{ is a cosunflower}\mid
A,B,C\in H_{P,\theta}
\right)
\le
\frac{\Xi_P}{\nu_P(H_{P,\theta})^3}
\to0.
\]

Consequently, a proof of \(M_P(\theta)\to0\) cannot rely on a \(P\)-uniform positive lower bound for the unconditioned \(\nu_P^{\otimes3}\)-density of cosunflower triples inside high support.  The full high-support event has measure tending to \(1\), while its ambient random cosunflower triple density tends to \(0\).  A successful argument must use structural information about a cosunflower-free family, such as a biased-measure density-increment, sparse supersaturation, container, compression, or junta mechanism.

## Proof

Fix \(p\in P\) and write \(q=1/p\).  For the coordinate \(p\), the three independent indicators \(1_{p\in A},1_{p\in B},1_{p\in C}\) have no unique \(1\) exactly in the patterns
\[
000,\quad 110,\quad 101,\quad 011,\quad 111.
\]
The coordinate probability is therefore
\[
(1-q)^3+3q^2(1-q)+q^3
=
1-3q(1-q)^2.
\]
Coordinates are independent under \(\nu_P^{\otimes3}\), so multiplying this expression over \(p\in P\) proves the product formula for \(\Xi_P\).

Since \(0<q\le1/2\) for primes and
\[
\log(1-3q(1-q)^2)=-3q+O(q^2)
\]
uniformly for \(0\le q\le1/2\), summing over primes in \(P\) gives
\[
\log\Xi_P
=
-3\sum_{p\in P}\frac1p
+O\left(\sum_{p\in P}\frac1{p^2}\right).
\]
The prime square-reciprocal sum is bounded uniformly by the convergent series \(\sum_p p^{-2}\).  Hence
\[
\log\Xi_P=-3S_P+O(1).
\]
This tends to \(-\infty\) whenever \(S_P\to\infty\).

It remains to check the high-support mass.  Under \(\nu_P\), the support size
\[
|S|=\sum_{p\in P}1_{p\in S}
\]
is a sum of independent Bernoulli variables with mean \(S_P\).  The Chernoff lower-tail estimate used in [[wiki/nodes/mrw-4daa694d9526|Low-support growing-prime criterion for Erdos 536]] gives, for \(0\le\theta<1\),
\[
\nu_P(|S|\le\theta S_P)
\le
\exp\left(-\frac{(1-\theta)^2S_P}{2}\right).
\]
Therefore \(\nu_P(H_{P,\theta})\to1\).

The event that \(A,B,C\) are all in \(H_{P,\theta}\) has probability \(\nu_P(H_{P,\theta})^3\).  The event that they form a squarefree cosunflower while all lying in \(H_{P,\theta}\) is contained in the ambient cosunflower event of probability \(\Xi_P\).  This proves the displayed conditional bound.  The same bound also applies to distinct cosunflower triples, since allowing equal sets can only increase the probability.

This proves the asserted route obstruction.  It is not a counterexample to \(M_P(\theta)\to0\): the high-support event itself is not cosunflower-free.  The conclusion is only that a \(P\)-uniform positive ambient random-triple density is unavailable.  The calculation does not rule out a family-specific sparse supersaturation theorem at a scale comparable to \(\Xi_P\), nor any density-increment or container argument that uses more than the full layer's ambient triple density.

## Notes

- The proposition deliberately counts cosunflower triples under the ambient product measure, not inside a cosunflower-free family.  It is a proof-method obstruction, not a construction of large independent mass.
- Equality triples are included in \(\Xi_P\).  Since the target family condition forbids only three distinct sets, this makes the ambient upper bound stronger for the present diagnostic.
- The calculation explains why the biased residual problem is subtler than a direct density Hales-Jewett analogy: the ambient forbidden configuration is rare even in the whole high-support layer.
- The lower-tail conclusion is stated for fixed \(0\le\theta<1\).  Moving \(\theta\) with \(P\) requires a separate tail estimate.
