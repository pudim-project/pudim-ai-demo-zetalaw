# Proposition: Even-order envelope reduction for polygamma beta windows

## Statement

Let \(n\ge2\) be even, and define
\[
C_n(x)=\psi^{(n)}(x)+x\psi^{(n+1)}(x),
\qquad
P_n(x)=\psi^{(n)}(x)\psi^{(n)}(1/x).
\]
For \(x\ne1\), put
\[
Q_n(x)=\frac{\log(P_n(x)/C_n(x))}{\log x}.
\]
Then the admissible beta set
\[
\mathcal I_n=\{\beta\in\mathbb R:x^\beta C_n(x)-P_n(x)<0\text{ for all }x>0\}
\]
is exactly
\[
\mathcal I_n=
\left\{\beta\in\mathbb R:
\beta>Q_n(x)\text{ for every }0<x<1,
\quad
\beta<Q_n(x)\text{ for every }x>1
\right\}.
\]
The point \(x=1\) imposes no beta-dependent restriction.  Moreover
\[
\lim_{x\to0^+}Q_n(x)=n,\qquad
\lim_{x\to\infty}Q_n(x)=n+1.
\]
Consequently every admissible \(\beta\) must satisfy
\[
n\le\beta\le n+1.
\]

## Proof

By [[wiki/nodes/mrw-f3c6cef2ebb1|Odd-order collapse for polygamma beta windows]],
\[
(-1)^nC_n(x)>0
\]
for all \(n\ge1\) and \(x>0\).  Since \(n\) is even, \(C_n(x)>0\).  Also the standard sign formula
\[
\psi^{(n)}(x)=(-1)^{n+1}n!\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(x+k)^{-n-1}
\]
shows that \(\psi^{(n)}(x)<0\) for all \(x>0\), so
\[
P_n(x)>0.
\]
Therefore
\[
x^\beta C_n(x)-P_n(x)<0
\]
is equivalent to
\[
x^\beta<\frac{P_n(x)}{C_n(x)}.
\]
Taking logarithms gives
\[
\beta\log x<\log(P_n(x)/C_n(x)).
\]
For \(0<x<1\), \(\log x<0\), so this is \(\beta>Q_n(x)\).  For \(x>1\), it is \(\beta<Q_n(x)\).

At \(x=1\), the inequality is \(C_n(1)<P_n(1)\).  Since \(n\) is even,
\[
C_n(1)=n!\big((n+1)\zeta(n+2)-\zeta(n+1)\big),
\qquad
P_n(1)=n!^2\zeta(n+1)^2.
\]
For \(n\ge4\),
\[
C_n(1)<(n+1)n!\zeta(3)<2(n+1)n!<n!^2\le P_n(1).
\]
For \(n=2\), use \(\zeta(3)>9/8\) and \(\zeta(4)<11/10\), which give
\[
P_2(1)=4\zeta(3)^2>\frac{81}{16}
\]
and
\[
C_2(1)=2(3\zeta(4)-\zeta(3))<2\left(\frac{33}{10}-1\right)=\frac{23}{5}<\frac{81}{16}.
\]
Thus \(x=1\) is harmless for every even \(n\ge2\).

It remains to compute the endpoint limits.  As \(x\to0^+\),
\[
\psi^{(n)}(x)=-n!x^{-n-1}+O(1),
\qquad
\psi^{(n+1)}(x)=(n+1)!x^{-n-2}+O(1),
\]
and
\[
\psi^{(n)}(1/x)=-(n-1)!x^n+O(x^{n+1}).
\]
Hence
\[
C_n(x)=n\,n!x^{-n-1}(1+o(1)),
\qquad
P_n(x)=n!(n-1)!x^{-1}(1+o(1)),
\]
so
\[
\frac{P_n(x)}{C_n(x)}
=\frac{(n-1)!}{n}x^n(1+o(1)).
\]
Therefore
\[
\lim_{x\to0^+}Q_n(x)=n.
\]

As \(x\to\infty\),
\[
\psi^{(n)}(x)=-(n-1)!x^{-n}+O(x^{-n-1}),
\qquad
\psi^{(n+1)}(x)=n!x^{-n-1}+O(x^{-n-2}),
\]
and
\[
\psi^{(n)}(1/x)=-n!x^{n+1}+O(1).
\]
Thus
\[
C_n(x)=(n-1)(n-1)!x^{-n}(1+o(1)),
\qquad
P_n(x)=n!(n-1)!x(1+o(1)),
\]
and hence
\[
\frac{P_n(x)}{C_n(x)}
=\frac{n!}{n-1}x^{n+1}(1+o(1)).
\]
This gives
\[
\lim_{x\to\infty}Q_n(x)=n+1.
\]
If \(\beta<n\), the lower constraint fails for sufficiently small \(x\).  If \(\beta>n+1\), the upper constraint fails for sufficiently large \(x\).  Hence every admissible \(\beta\) lies in \([n,n+1]\).

## Notes

- This proposition does not solve the even-order beta-window problem.  It reduces it to the scalar-envelope comparison
\[
\sup_{0<x<1}Q_n(x)
\quad\text{versus}\quad
\inf_{x>1}Q_n(x),
\]
with endpoint pressure \(n\le\beta\le n+1\).
- The \(n=2\) case is the first nontrivial even-order target and may have a stricter lower endpoint than \(2\); this proposition does not claim otherwise.
- The endpoint exclusion \(\beta=2\notin\mathcal I_2\) is proved separately in [[wiki/nodes/mrw-8a146667d25b|Endpoint exclusion for the n=2 polygamma beta window]].
- A first pointwise strict lower obstruction above \(2\), obtained at \(x=1/2\), is proved in [[wiki/nodes/mrw-f27a36284da5|Dyadic lower obstruction for the n=2 polygamma beta window]].
- The upper-envelope side is closed in [[wiki/nodes/mrw-201bbda2c917|Right endpoint theorem for even-order polygamma beta windows]]: \(\beta=n+1\) is admissible for every even \(n\ge2\), and the remaining even-order uncertainty is only the lower envelope on \(0<x<1\).
- A stronger certified pointwise lower obstruction for \(n=2\), using rational interval bounds at \(x=2/7\), is proved in [[wiki/nodes/mrw-30f9a055fa9a|Certified two-sevenths lower obstruction for the n=2 beta window]].
- The coarse bracket [[wiki/nodes/mrw-2a62d2bc84ad|Coarse compact maximum bracket for the n=2 lower envelope]] proves that \(Q_2\) has an interior maximizer on \([1/4,1/3]\) with value above \(1157/500\).
- The sharper bracket [[wiki/nodes/mrw-8c1324a498bf|Sharper compact maximum bracket for the n=2 lower envelope]] narrows this to \([7/25,3/10]\) and raises the certified rational lower obstruction to \(4629/2000\).
- The refined localization [[wiki/nodes/mrw-3712cf1c88d8|Refined compact localization for the n=2 lower-envelope maximum]] further narrows the local maximum bracket to \([1409/5000,293/1000]\).
- The critical-point equation [[wiki/nodes/mrw-a3170d192f6c|Critical-point equation for the n=2 lower-envelope maximum]] turns that localized interior maximizer into an explicit Hurwitz-zeta derivative equation.
- The admissible-subwindow theorem [[wiki/nodes/mrw-5fabc550bd7d|Explicit n=2 admissible beta subwindow]] proves \([5/2,3]\subseteq\mathcal I_2\), giving the first explicit nontrivial sufficient interval for the \(n=2\) beta window.
- The sharper subwindow theorem [[wiki/nodes/mrw-19400778b4b5|Sharper explicit n=2 admissible beta subwindow]] improves this to \([19/8,3]\subseteq\mathcal I_2\).
- The Euler--Maclaurin subwindow theorem [[wiki/nodes/mrw-da05add5bca1|Euler-Maclaurin n=2 admissible beta subwindow]] improves this to \([187/80,3]\subseteq\mathcal I_2\).
- The refined Euler--Maclaurin subwindow theorem [[wiki/nodes/mrw-c3e50abdd2fe|Refined Euler-Maclaurin n=2 admissible beta subwindow]] improves this to \([257/110,3]\subseteq\mathcal I_2\).
- The exact-window theorem [[wiki/nodes/mrw-fd6576e56da0|Exact even-order beta window outside n=2]] proves \(\mathcal I_n=[n,n+1]\) for every even \(n\ge4\), isolating \(n=2\) as the only unresolved even-order case.
