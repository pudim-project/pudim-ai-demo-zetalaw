# Theorem: T Erdos536 unbounded moving coordinate tightness or descent

## Statement

Unbounded moving-coordinate tightness or descent theorem: if local coordinate concentration persists only through coordinates escaping every bounded window, then either the escaping-coordinate mass is tight after prime-weight conditioning and yields a fixed core, moving core, or endpoint shield with strict lexicographic decrease of \(\mathcal C=(\ell_{\mathrm{end}},w_{\mathrm{core}},m_{\mathrm{avail}},r_{\mathrm{width}})\), or the escaping mass is rank-thin/vanishing, or an explicit unbounded moving-coordinate obstruction model is constructed.
