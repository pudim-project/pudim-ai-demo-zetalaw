# Theorem: Ressel two-center exponential lemma holds in collinear configurations

## Statement

For collinear Hilbert vectors u,m,h, the two-center defect H_h(u)+H_m(m)-H_m(u)-H_h(m) is nonnegative, where H_a(v)=exp(-|v-a|)+exp(-|v+a|).
