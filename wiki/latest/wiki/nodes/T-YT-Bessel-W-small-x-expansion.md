# Theorem: small x expansion W_nu z equals 2(nu+1)+z^2/(2(nu+2))+O(z^4)

## Statement

For \(\nu>-1\), the modified Bessel ratio \(W_\nu(z)=zI_\nu(z)/I_{\nu+1}(z)\) satisfies \(W_\nu(z)=2(\nu+1)+z^2/(2(\nu+2))+O(z^4)\) as \(z\to0^+\).
